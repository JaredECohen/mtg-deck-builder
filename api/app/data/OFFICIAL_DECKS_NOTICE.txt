Official product decklists in official_decks.json are names and quantities
taken from MTGJSON (https://mtgjson.com).

MTGJSON publishes that data under the MIT license:
https://www.mtgjson.com/license/

The lists are Wizards of the Coast products (Commander precons and Pioneer
Challenger Decks), not a scrape of a tournament metagame site. Each record
keeps the source URL MTGJSON recorded for that product when it had one.
