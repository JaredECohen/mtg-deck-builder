## Scryfall

Rules text, types, legality, the `game_changer` field, and bulk price
estimates come from Scryfall's bulk data files
(https://scryfall.com/docs/api/bulk-data), under Scryfall's API terms of use
(https://scryfall.com/docs/api).

Scryfall is not affiliated with MTG Deck Lab and does not endorse it. Card
data from Scryfall is not paywalled here. Prices in the bulk file are
estimates and go stale; they are not a promise of what a store will charge.
