## Short answer

In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), Wizards' Game Changers list is a Commander bracket input: cards that push a deck up a bracket when a table uses that vocabulary. Deck Lab's copy of the list is dated 2025-04-22 and holds 60 names. Of those, 14 appear in at least one of the 182 official Commander precons in the MTGJSON snapshot. 17 precons include at least one. The other precons include none.

That is not a power ranking of the products. A precon with zero Game Changers can still be miserable to play against, and a precon with one can still be a pile of ordinary cards around that one name. The count is the intersection of two files: the Game Changer names, and the official deck lists. Prices next to each name are the lowest nonfoil Scryfall price on 2026-11-01, the same bulk file as the [lands, ramp, and draw census](/blog/precon-lands-ramp-draw). How the count is defined is on the [method page](/method).

The list-only bracket floor this app computes, which is not Wizards assigning a table a number, is the core floor for 163 precons, the upgraded floor for 16, and the optimized floor for 3. The exhibition and competitive floors are about intent, so a list cannot show them. The floor leaves the core band when any Game Changer is present, and it reaches the optimized band when there are more than three, or when the mass-land-denial check, two extra-turn cards, or a listed two-card combo matches. In this snapshot the optimized-floor results are Heads I Win, Tails You Lose (Gamble plus two extra-turn cards), Mirror Mastery (Ruination), and Nature of the Beast (From the Ashes).

## What the floor is for

Use the floor when a table has agreed to talk in brackets. 163 of these products do not contain a Game Changer, a mass-land-denial match, a two-card combo from the app's list, or two extra-turn cards. They still want rule zero. 16 contain something the floor treats as upgraded. 3 trip a harder flag. Bringing an optimized-floor list to a table that wanted the core floor is a different ask than goldfishing it alone.

Do not add a Game Changer to a precon and keep calling the receipt the precon. Deflecting Swat at $75.15 is most of a conversation by itself. The [method page](/method) separates that Scryfall price from the product-printing price in the deck block.

## Common mistakes

- Treating "not on the Game Changer list" as "weak." 182 minus the 17 that have one leaves a lot of products, and the land count on those lists is the other article.
- Assuming every expensive precon card is a Game Changer. The price census and this list are different files. A high Scryfall sum can be ordinary cards.
- Reading the optimized floor as a ban. The cards are legal. The floor is a description of the list.
- Copying a Game Changer into a different color identity because the precon had it. Arcane Maelstrom's colors are the colors in that product, not a rule about Deflecting Swat in every red deck.
- Using a foil-only price when this page left the card unpriced. Every name in the table had a nonfoil USD. Cards with none are flagged in the facts file instead of given a made-up number.

## FAQ

### How many Game Changers are in a typical precon?

Most of these products have none. 17 of 182 have at least one, and the names that repeat are in the table. The list itself has 60 entries as of 2025-04-22.

### Does one Game Changer leave the core floor?

Under this app's list-only floor, one Game Changer moves the floor out of the core band, and more than three, or a mass-land-denial match, or two extra-turn cards, or a listed two-card combo, can move it to the optimized band. 16 products land on the upgraded floor and 3 land on the optimized floor. The exhibition and competitive brackets are not something this count can see.

### Which price should I trust for shopping?

For a single card, the number in the table is the cheapest English nonfoil Scryfall price on 2026-11-01. For the sealed product's own printings, use the deck block, which cites MTGJSON AllPricesToday. They answer different questions. Sneak Attack's Scryfall sum is $41.70. That is not a bid for the box.
