## Short answer

In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), a Game Changer is a legal Commander card that pushes a deck up a bracket when the table is using that vocabulary. The names on this page are not a remembered announcement. They are the cards Scryfall marks `game_changer` on the oracle bulk of 2026-11-01, and the build refuses to run if `game_changers.json` disagrees with that field. The file holds 53 names. 11 of them appear in the 174 unique precon lists. 18 of those lists include a flagged card. Anthology reprints are not counted as another copy of the same cards.

That is a narrower fact than "precons are casual." A list with none of these cards can still be miserable, and a list with a single flagged card can still be a pile of ordinary cards around that name. Prices are the lowest English paper nonfoil Scryfall price on 2026-11-01. The [lands, ramp, and draw census](/blog/precon-lands-ramp-draw) uses the same bulk. How a name gets onto the list, and how the bracket floor is read, is on the [method page](/method).

The list-only floor this app computes is the core floor for 154 unique lists, the upgraded floor for 17, and the optimized floor for 3. The optimized results are Heads I Win, Tails You Lose (Gamble, Ral Zarek, Stitch in Time); Mirror Mastery (Ruination); Nature of the Beast (From the Ashes). A floor moves out of the core band when any Game Changer is present, and it reaches the optimized band when the mass-land-denial check, chained extra turns, or a listed combo matches. Exhibition and competitive brackets are about the table, so this count cannot see them.

## How to use the floor

Use the floor when the table has already agreed to talk in brackets. 154 of these unique lists do not contain a Game Changer, a mass-land-denial match, or chained extra turns. They still want a conversation about what the table is willing to play. 17 contain something the floor treats as upgraded, and 3 trip a harder flag. Bringing an optimized-floor list to a table that wanted the core floor is a different ask than goldfishing the deck alone.

Do not add Farewell or Fierce Guardianship to a different precon and keep calling the receipt the precon. Fierce Guardianship at $62.99 is a purchase by itself. The census of lands and ramp is the other article, and a high Scryfall sum there can be ordinary cards.

## Common mistakes

- Using an older copy of the list. This page has 53 names because that is what the 2026-11-01 bulk marks, and Farewell is on it.
- Treating "not on the list" as "weak." Most of the 174 unique lists have none of these cards.
- Reading the optimized floor as a ban. The cards are legal. The floor describes the list.
- Assuming an expensive precon card is a Game Changer. Arcane Maelstrom costs $132.04 with Crop Rotation at $3.35.

## FAQ

### How many of these cards does a typical precon run?

Most run none. 18 of 174 unique lists have a flagged card, and the names that repeat are Farewell and Seedborn Muse, each in 4 lists.

### Does a Game Changer leave the core floor?

Under this app's list-only floor, yes. 17 unique lists land on the upgraded floor and 3 land on the optimized floor. The exhibition and competitive brackets are not something the list can see.

### Which price is for shopping?

The table is the cheapest English nonfoil Scryfall price on 2026-11-01. Sneak Attack's Scryfall sum is $41.70. The deck block is the product printing from MTGJSON, which is a different receipt.
