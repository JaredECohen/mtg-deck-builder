Premium Deck Series, Signature Spellbook, Commander's Arsenal and Commander Collection are four small Magic: The Gathering box-set lines from 2009 to 2022, each built around premium reprints rather than new cards. Premium Deck Series sold all-foil 60-card decks (Slivers, Fire and Lightning, Graveborn); Commander's Arsenal was a 2012 box of foil Commander staples; Signature Spellbooks gave Jace, Gideon and Chandra eight signature spells each; and the Commander Collections reprinted green and black Commander staples with new art.

## What these products were for

All four lines did the same job in different ways: put desirable cards back into print in a premium, collectible form without adding anything new to the game. Premium Deck Series aimed at players who wanted a ready-made, all-foil deck of classic cards. Commander's Arsenal was Wizards' first premium product for the then-new Commander format. Signature Spellbooks, the spiritual successor to the From the Vault series, picked a planeswalker and reprinted eight cards that defined their style, chosen so the cards would end up in decks rather than binders. Commander Collections did the same for a color, with every card a Commander staple and new art throughout.

Because none of them added new cards, their importance comes down to which reprints they carried. For Commander players especially, several of these boxes were for years among the few places to find certain staples; [what is Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) explains why those cards matter so much in that format.

## Premium Deck Series (2009-2011)

Each Premium Deck Series box contained a 60-card all-foil deck, a spindown life counter and a foil deck box, using the modern card frame on cards from Legacy-legal sets. The series ended after three releases; Mark Rosewater later explained that not enough players loved it.

- **Slivers (H09, November 2009):** a five-color Sliver tribal deck with **Sliver Overlord**, **Crystalline Sliver**, **Heart Sliver**, **Wild Pair**, **Coat of Arms** and **Ancient Ziggurat**. Its strategy was simple: play lands that make many colors, then flood the board with Slivers that share their abilities.
- **Fire and Lightning (PD2, November 2010):** a mono-red burn deck with **Lightning Bolt**, **Chain Lightning** (with new art), **Fireblast**, **Price of Progress**, **Grim Lavamancer**, **Figure of Destiny**, **Ball Lightning** and **Jackal Pup** (also new art). It was essentially a Legacy-style burn deck in a box; for how that strategy works, see the [burn deck guide](/blog/mtg-burn-deck-guide).
- **Graveborn (PD3, November 2011):** a black reanimator deck with **Reanimate**, **Animate Dead**, **Exhume**, **Entomb**, **Buried Alive**, **Cabal Therapy** and targets such as **Sphinx of the Steel Wind**, **Inkwell Leviathan** and **Terastodon**. It was the last Premium Deck Series release.

## Commander's Arsenal (2012)

Commander's Arsenal was released in November 2012, after the success of the original 2011 Commander decks. Instead of a deck, it was what Mark Rosewater called a treasure trove of Commander goodies: 18 premium foil cards, 10 oversized foil commanders, 120 card sleeves, double-sided counters for +1/+1 and -1/-1, and a life counter that goes up to 99.

The 18 cards included **Command Tower** (with new art), **Rhystic Study**, **Sylvan Library**, **Scroll Rack**, **Chaos Warp**, **Mind's Eye**, **Duplicant**, **Desertion**, **Decree of Pain** and commanders like **Kaalia of the Vast**, **Edric, Spymaster of Trest**, **The Mimeoplasm** and **Maelstrom Wanderer**. The oversized cards included **Sliver Queen** and **Karn, Silver Golem**, both on the Reserved List; oversized cards aren't tournament legal, so they didn't break that promise. It had a limited print run and was hard to find, which disappointed players who had hoped it would make these staples widely available.

## Signature Spellbook (2018-2020)

Each Signature Spellbook held eight cards, all depicting the featured planeswalker in a special frame, plus a foil copy of one of the eight.

- **Jace (SS1, June 2018):** **Jace Beleren**, **Counterspell**, **Brainstorm**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Gifts Ungiven**, **Negate**, **Blue Elemental Blast** and **Threads of Disloyalty**. It was the first time the blue mana symbol was printed with inverted colors.
- **Gideon (SS2, June 2019):** **Gideon Jura**, **Path to Exile**, **Rest in Peace**, **Worship**, **Martyr's Bond**, **True Conviction**, **Shielded by Faith** and **Blackblade Reforged**, the last with art by Richard Kane Ferguson, who created the sword's original look.
- **Chandra (SS3, June 2020):** **Chandra, Torch of Defiance**, **Past in Flames**, **Young Pyromancer**, **Pyromancer Ascension**, **Fiery Confluence**, **Cathartic Reunion**, **Rite of Flame** and **Pyroblast**. It was sold only through game stores, and Fiery Confluence appeared in foil for the first time.

There was no Signature Spellbook in 2021; the Commander Collection line took over.

## Commander Collection (2020-2022)

Commander Collections were sold only through Wizards Play Network stores, in a regular nonfoil edition and a premium foil edition for premium stores. Each had eight reprints with new art, always including a **Sol Ring** and a **Command Tower** illustrated for that color.

- **Green (CC1, December 2020):** **Worldly Tutor**, **Sylvan Library**, **Seedborn Muse**, **Bane of Progress**, **Freyalise, Llanowar's Fury** and **Omnath, Locus of Mana**, plus Sol Ring and Command Tower.
- **Black (CC2, January 2022):** **Reanimate**, **Toxic Deluge**, **Phyrexian Arena**, **Ophiomancer**, **Ghoulcaller Gisa** and **Liliana, Heretical Healer**, plus Sol Ring and Command Tower.

## How they play

None of these boxes was a balanced, play-out-of-the-box product in the way a Commander precon is, with one exception: the Premium Deck Series decks are complete 60-card decks. Fire and Lightning and Graveborn in particular were close to real Legacy strategies, though updated lists have moved on. Slivers is a fun casual deck that rewards building the board.

The rest are collections. Commander's Arsenal, the Signature Spellbooks and the Commander Collections are best treated as upgrade packs: pick the cards that fit your decks and leave the rest. If you're working with a tight card pool, the ideas in the [budget decks guide](/blog/budget-mtg-decks-under-50) on building around a few key cards apply here too.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Because every card was a reprint, these products didn't change any format directly. Their effect was on supply, and the formats that benefited most were Legacy and Commander.

Several cards carry format restrictions as of October 2026:

- **Mystical Tutor** is banned in Legacy and restricted in Vintage.
- **Entomb** was banned in Legacy on November 10, 2025, alongside Nadu, Winged Wisdom.
- **Sol Ring** is banned in Legacy and restricted in Vintage but is one of the most-played cards in Commander.
- **Rhystic Study**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Gifts Ungiven**, **Worldly Tutor** and **Seedborn Muse** are on Commander's Game Changers list, which caps them in the lower brackets; see [Commander brackets explained](/blog/commander-brackets-explained).

Printing in these products also never changed Modern legality. **Chain Lightning**, for example, appears in Fire and Lightning but isn't Modern-legal, because no Modern-legal set has printed it.

## Cards from these products worth owning

- **Rhystic Study:** one of Commander's most famous card-draw engines.
- **Sylvan Library:** a top green card-selection engine in Legacy and Commander.
- **Reanimate and Entomb:** the core of any reanimator deck in Commander.
- **Toxic Deluge:** one of the most efficient sweepers ever printed.
- **Worldly Tutor and Mystical Tutor:** efficient tutors for high-power Commander decks.
- **Path to Exile and Lightning Bolt:** cheap removal that fits almost any deck in its color.
- **Scroll Rack:** a strong card-selection engine, especially with shuffle effects.

If you find any of these boxes, treat them as staple collections rather than finished decks. Pick the reprints that fit a deck you already play, or build around one. A reanimator Commander deck is a natural home for several of these cards, and you can draft one in the [MTG Deck Lab builder](/).
