Commander 2020 is a set of five preconstructed Magic: The Gathering Commander decks released alongside Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths in spring 2020. It was the last product in the yearly "Commander 20XX" line, and its five three-color decks shipped with 20 new legendary creatures, including five pairs of partners. Its biggest legacy is a cycle of instants you can cast for free while you control your commander, led by **Fierce Guardianship** and **Deflecting Swat**.

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Set code | C20 |
| Release date | April 17, 2020 in Asia; May 15, 2020 elsewhere (delayed by the COVID-19 pandemic) |
| Plane | Ikoria, the plane of monsters, with reprints from across the Multiverse |
| Set size | Five 100-card decks with 322 different cards, 71 of them new |
| Product | Five preconstructed Commander decks, each with an oversized foil commander, four foil legends, tokens and keyword-counter punch cards |

| Formats | New cards are legal in Commander, Legacy and Vintage, not Standard, Pioneer or Modern. Reprints keep their normal legality |

## What the set was for

Commander 2020 was built as part of the Ikoria release instead of a separate fall product. Ikoria is a plane overrun by giant monsters, where some humans, called bonders, form partnerships with the creatures. The decks lean into that setting: every deck has a pair of partner commanders, one a mono-colored Human bonder and the other an allied-color monster, so you can run the pair instead of the face commander.

The design goal was a set of decks built around Ikoria's mechanics, keyword counters, mutate and cycling, while reaching back for older mechanics that fit each theme. Every deck includes **Sol Ring**, **Arcane Signet**, **Command Tower**, **Exotic Orchard** and the new **Bonder's Ornament**, so each one is a sound starting point for new players. The decks use the wedge color combinations: each deck is one color plus its two enemies.

## The decks

### Ruthless Regiment (red-white-black, Mardu)

**Jirina Kudro** (mythic, `{1}{R}{W}{B}`) makes a 1/1 Human Soldier for each time you've cast a commander from the command zone this game and gives your other Humans +2/+0. It's a Humans tokens deck that rewards recasting your commander. The secondary commander is **Kelsien, the Plague**, which pings creatures and gains experience counters as they die. The partner pair is **Trynn, Champion of Freedom** and **Silvar, Devourer of the Free**.

### Arcane Maelstrom (green-blue-red, Temur)

**Kalamax, the Stormsire** (mythic) copies the first instant you cast each turn while it's tapped and grows whenever you copy an instant. It's an instants-and-copies deck that wants to attack with Kalamax and hold up spells. **Xyris, the Writhing Storm** punishes opponents for drawing extra cards with Snake tokens. The partners are **Haldan, Avid Arcanist** and **Pako, Arcane Retriever**, which exile cards from every library and let you cast the noncreature ones.

### Symbiotic Swarm (white-black-green, Abzan)

**Kathril, Aspect Warper** (mythic) looks at the keyword abilities among creature cards in your graveyard, such as flying, deathtouch and lifelink, and puts matching keyword counters on your creatures. It's a self-mill and keyword counters deck. **Tayam, Luminous Enigma** gives your creatures vigilance counters and spends counters to return cheap permanents from your graveyard. The partners are **Nikara, Lair Scavenger** and **Yannik, Scavenging Sentinel**.

### Timeless Wisdom (blue-red-white, Jeskai)

**Gavi, Nest Warden** (mythic) lets you cycle the first card each turn for free and makes a 2/2 Dinosaur Cat token whenever you draw your second card each turn. It's a cycling and card-draw deck. **Akim, the Soaring Wind** makes a flying Bird whenever you first create tokens in a turn and can give your tokens double strike. The partners are **Brallin, Skyshark Rider** and **Shabraz, the Skyshark**, which reward discarding and drawing.

### Enhanced Evolution (blue-black-green, Sultai)

**Otrimi, the Ever-Playful** (mythic) is a 6/6 trampler with mutate that returns mutate creatures from your graveyard to your hand whenever it deals combat damage. It's the dedicated mutate deck. **Zaxara, the Exemplary** taps for two mana of one color and makes a Hydra token whenever you cast an X spell. The partners are **Cazur, Ruthless Stalker** and **Ukkima, Stalking Shadow**.

## Key cards

### The free-spell cycle

Five rare instants let you skip their mana cost entirely if you control a commander. That's easy in Commander, since your commander is usually on the battlefield, and it makes each card a free answer.

| Card | Rarity | What it does | Where it shines |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Fierce Guardianship** | Rare | Counter target noncreature spell | cEDH and high-power blue decks; a Game Changer |
| **Deflecting Swat** | Rare | Choose new targets for a spell or ability | Red decks protecting their commander |
| **Deadly Rollick** | Rare | Exile target creature | Black removal at instant speed |
| **Flawless Maneuver** | Rare | Your creatures gain indestructible until end of turn | White protection against board wipes |
| **Obscuring Haze** | Rare | Prevent all damage opponents' creatures would deal this turn | Green defensive trick |

### Notable reprints

The decks also reprinted plenty of staples: **Sol Ring**, **Arcane Signet**, **Exotic Orchard**, **Beast Whisperer**, **Sun Titan**, **Etali, Primal Storm**, **Windfall**, **Frantic Search**, **Crop Rotation** and a run of dual lands, including Battle for Zendikar's typed duals and several painlands.

## How it plays

Commander 2020 decks are stronger out of the box than older precons. They have better mana, with dual lands and the shared rocks, and real interaction from the free-spell cycle. In today's [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained) an unmodified deck still sits near the low end, but the gap to a casual upgraded deck is smaller than with earlier products. One caveat: **Fierce Guardianship** and **Crop Rotation** are Game Changers as of October 2026, so an otherwise casual Arcane Maelstrom or Symbiotic Swarm deck may need a quick check before a low-bracket game.

Some decks upgrade more easily than others:

- **Ruthless Regiment** wants more cheap Humans and token anthems, and Jirina rewards you for having a commander that gets removed.
- **Arcane Maelstrom** becomes an instants deck with more copy effects. Kalamax has to be tapped to copy, so ways to tap it on the opponent's turn help.
- **Symbiotic Swarm** can lean into self-mill and creatures with many keywords, or into a +1/+1 counters deck with Tayam.
- **Timeless Wisdom** is a natural cycling deck. More cheap cyclers and second-draw triggers make Gavi a steady token engine.
- **Enhanced Evolution** is the hardest to tune, because mutate is complicated. Many players switch it to Zaxara, the Exemplary and X spells.

The partner pairs also add flexibility. You can play one partner pair from a deck in a completely different build, since two partners together give you a wider color identity. For more on reworking a precon, see [how to upgrade a Commander precon](/blog/how-to-upgrade-a-commander-precon).

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Commander 2020 cards aren't legal in Standard, Pioneer or Modern. In Legacy and Vintage the set made little impact, since its strongest cards depend on controlling a commander.

In Commander the free-spell cycle changed things. **Fierce Guardianship** quickly became a staple of competitive Commander (cEDH), where a free counterspell can protect a combo or stop one. **Deflecting Swat** became the red deck's version of free protection, and **Deadly Rollick** and **Flawless Maneuver** became common picks in casual and high-power decks.

No Commander 2020 card is banned in Commander as of October 2026. **Fierce Guardianship** is on the Game Changers list, along with the reprint **Crop Rotation**. **Bonder's Ornament** had its own story in Pauper: it was banned there to slow down Affinity, then unbanned as a trial in May 2026. Our [Pauper guide](/blog/what-is-pauper-mtg) explains how the format's legality works.

## Cards from Commander 2020 worth owning

- **Fierce Guardianship**: a free counterspell for any blue Commander deck.
- **Deflecting Swat**: free protection or redirection for red decks.
- **Deadly Rollick**: free instant-speed creature removal for black.
- **Flawless Maneuver**: free protection from board wipes for white decks.
- **Nesting Grounds**: a strong utility land for any counters deck.
- **Shiny Impetus**: a political aura that ramps you while pointing threats elsewhere.
- **Arcane Signet**: the reprint every Commander deck can use.

## Takeaway

Commander 2020 is one of the best values among older precons. Even if you never play the decks as built, the free-spell cycle and the reprints are worth having. If you want to play one, Timeless Wisdom and Ruthless Regiment upgrade most cleanly. If you're new, start with [what Commander is](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) and use the [deck builder](/) to sketch an upgrade.
