Commander 2015 is a Magic: The Gathering product of five preconstructed 100-card Commander decks, released on November 13, 2015. Each deck is built in a pair of enemy colors and led by a new legend that uses experience counters, a player counter introduced here, and the set also introduced myriad, a multiplayer attack keyword. Meren of Clan Nel Toth, Mizzix of the Izmagnus, the five Confluences, Command Beacon and Blade of Selves all debuted in it.

## What the set was for

Commander 2015 was the fourth release in Wizards' Commander deck series, after Commander (2011), Commander 2013 and Commander 2014, and like the others it was designed to be played out of the box in a four-player game. Each deck had a primary theme supported by its main new commander and a secondary theme supported by a second new legend, plus a reprinted legend as a third option. The goal was to give new players a complete Commander deck while giving experienced players new legends and cards to pull into their own decks. If you're new to the format, start with [what is Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh).

The decks used enemy color pairs, a change of pace: the 2011 and 2013 products were built around three-color decks, and Commander 2014 around mono-colored ones. Commander 2015 had no story of its own, though its legends came from planes like Ravnica (Mizzix, Karlov), Theros (Daxos) and Mirrodin (Ezuri).

## The decks

### Call the Spirits (white-black)

Led by **Daxos the Returned**, which gets an experience counter whenever you cast an enchantment and makes Spirit enchantment creature tokens whose size equals your experience. The secondary commander is **Karlov of the Ghost Council**, a two-drop that grows by two +1/+1 counters whenever you gain life and can spend six counters to exile a creature. The reprint commander is **Teysa, Envoy of Ghosts**.

### Seize Control (blue-red)

Led by **Mizzix of the Izmagnus**, which gains experience when you cast big instants and sorceries and makes every instant and sorcery cost `{1}` less per experience counter. The secondary commander is **Arjun, the Shifting Flame**, a Sphinx that cycles your whole hand each time you cast a spell. The reprint commander is **Melek, Izzet Paragon**.

### Plunder the Graves (black-green)

Led by **Meren of Clan Nel Toth**, which gains experience whenever another creature you control dies, then returns a creature card from your graveyard at your end step, to the battlefield if its mana value is at most your experience and to your hand otherwise. The secondary commander is **Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest**, which puts a +1/+1 counter on each of your creatures whenever a player sacrifices another permanent. The reprint commander is **Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord**.

### Wade into Battle (red-white)

Led by **Kalemne, Disciple of Iroas**, a double-striking, vigilant Giant that gains experience whenever you cast a creature spell with mana value 5 or more, and gets +1/+1 for each counter. The secondary commander is **Anya, Merciless Angel**, which grows and becomes indestructible when opponents fall below half their starting life. The reprint commander is **Gisela, Blade of Goldnight**.

### Swell the Host (green-blue)

Led by **Ezuri, Claw of Progress**, which gains experience whenever a creature with power 2 or less enters under your control and puts that many +1/+1 counters on another creature each combat. The secondary commander is **Kaseto, Orochi Archmage**, a Snake that makes creatures unblockable. The reprint commander is **Prime Speaker Zegana**.

### Experience counters and myriad

Experience counters are counters on you, the player, not on a permanent. Each main commander has its own way to earn them, and they stay even if the commander dies. That's why they suit Commander: your general can come back from the command zone just as strong.

Myriad is built for multiplayer. When a creature with myriad attacks one opponent, you can create a token copy of it attacking each other opponent, and the copies are exiled at end of combat. **Warchief Giant** and **Herald of the Host** have it, and **Blade of Selves** grants it to whatever it equips.

## How it plays

The five decks were built to be roughly even against each other out of the box, so a table of four Commander 2015 precons makes a fair game. The decks are slow by modern standards. They have plenty of five- and six-drops and a mix of strong and filler cards, so games tend to run long and reward players who protect their commander. Myriad creatures, Fiery Confluence and Comet Storm give each deck ways to hit the whole table.

Plunder the Graves and Seize Control have the most room to grow: Meren becomes a strong sacrifice-and-recursion engine with cheap creatures that die on their own, and Mizzix turns into a spell-chaining storm commander once the deck has more cheap rituals and card draw. Call the Spirits and Wade into Battle are more straightforward. For a step-by-step approach, see [how to upgrade a Commander precon](/blog/how-to-upgrade-a-commander-precon), and for the general structure of a 100-card deck, [how to build your first Commander deck](/blog/how-to-build-your-first-commander-deck).

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Commander 2015's new cards were never legal in Standard, Pioneer or Modern, so its impact was almost entirely on Commander.

In Commander, the set's legacy is strong. **Meren of Clan Nel Toth** became one of the defining black-green commanders, and **Mizzix of the Izmagnus** a long-running spellslinger option. **Command Beacon** and **Thought Vessel** became common utility picks in decks of every color. None of the set's new cards are banned in Commander or appear on the Game Changers list as of October 2026, which makes the decks easy to play in casual brackets; see [Commander brackets explained](/blog/commander-brackets-explained). The reprinted **Skullclamp** is banned in Modern and Legacy but legal in Commander.

## Cards from Commander 2015 worth owning

- **Meren of Clan Nel Toth:** a top-tier graveyard commander that rewards every creature that dies.
- **Mizzix of the Izmagnus:** a classic commander for instants and sorceries.
- **Command Beacon:** a colorless land that saves you mana after your commander dies a few times.
- **Thought Vessel:** an easy two-mana rock for any deck.
- **Blade of Selves:** doubles or triples enter-the-battlefield creatures in multiplayer.
- **Mystic Confluence and Fiery Confluence:** flexible spells that rarely sit dead in hand.
- **Grasp of Fate:** one-card removal that scales with the number of opponents.

Commander 2015 is a good example of what made the early annual precons popular: distinct commanders, strong new staples and room to grow. If one of its legends appeals to you, build a tighter 99 around its experience engine and test it in the [MTG Deck Lab builder](/).
