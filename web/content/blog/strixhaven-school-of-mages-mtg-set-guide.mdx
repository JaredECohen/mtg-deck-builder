Strixhaven: School of Mages is the Magic: The Gathering expansion released in April 2021 that took the game to a magic university. It's set on the plane of Arcavios, at a school of five rival colleges, each built on one of the five enemy-color pairs. The headline is a set built around instants and sorceries, the new magecraft and learn mechanics, and the Mystical Archive, a bonus sheet of famous spells reprinted with gorgeous alternate art.

| Fact | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Set code | STX (Mystical Archive: STA) |
| Release date | April 23, 2021 |
| Plane | Arcavios (Strixhaven University) |
| Main set size | 275 cards, plus 63 Mystical Archive cards |
| Headline mechanics | Magecraft, learn and Lessons, ward, modal double-faced cards |
| Standard status | In Standard from April 2021 until the September 2022 rotation; legal in Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage and Commander |

Strixhaven was also where Wizards introduced the term "mana value" in place of "converted mana cost," and where ward became an evergreen keyword. The Mystical Archive cards were never Standard-legal; their legality in other formats follows each card's own history.

## The story

### The school

Strixhaven is the most prestigious university in the Multiverse. Five elder dragons founded its colleges, and each college bears its founder's name and philosophy:

- **Silverquill** (white-black): the magic of words, debate and ink.
- **Prismari** (blue-red): elemental magic as performance art.
- **Witherbloom** (black-green): the magic of life and death, found in the swamps.
- **Lorehold** (red-white): history and archaeology, with spirits of the past.
- **Quandrix** (green-blue): magic as mathematics and the patterns of nature.

The colleges are built around the tension between their two colors rather than what they share, which is a deliberate contrast with Ravnica's guilds.

### The students and the Oriq

The main characters are Will and Rowan Kenrith, twin planeswalkers from Eldraine who come to Strixhaven as students after an invitation from the planeswalker Kasmina. Rowan struggles with her temper and her lightning-charged magic, and the twins' rivalry flares up more than once. One of their professors, Professor Onyx of Witherbloom, is secretly Liliana Vess, hiding out after War of the Spark and researching necromancy she hopes might bring back Gideon Jura.

The threat comes from the Oriq, a secret society that resents the university's control over magical knowledge. Their leader, Extus, is a Silverquill alumnus who was passed over for a prestigious role and turned to forbidden Blood Age magic, raising an army of Mage Hunter horrors. During a big public event, the Oriq launch an attack on campus as a distraction while Extus slips into the vaults beneath the great library, the Biblioplex, to tap a source of raw magical power and summon a monster called the Blood Avatar.

Professor Onyx sees through the misdirection and leads the twins after him. In the confrontation, Rowan and Will combine their magic, take the Blood Avatar away from Extus, and it kills him before it's banished. Will is badly hurt and loses part of his leg, and the hall beneath the library is wrecked. Afterward, Liliana drops her alias and stays on as a professor under her real name. The plane returns in Secrets of Strixhaven years later.

## Mechanics

### Magecraft

Magecraft is an ability word: it triggers whenever you cast or copy an instant or sorcery spell. Archmage Emeritus, a four-mana 2/2 for `{2}{U}{U}`, draws a card each time. Magecraft makes every cheap spell do double duty, and copying a spell triggers it again.

### Learn and Lessons

Lessons are a sorcery subtype. When an effect tells you to learn, you may reveal a Lesson card you own from outside the game (your sideboard in tournaments) and put it into your hand, or you can discard a card and draw a card instead. Pop Quiz, a three-mana instant, draws a card and then lets you learn. Learn gives you a small toolbox: Environmental Sciences finds a basic land, Introduction to Annihilation exiles a nonland permanent, and each college has a Lesson that makes its mascot token.

### Ward

Ward debuted here as an evergreen keyword. When a permanent with ward becomes the target of a spell or ability an opponent controls, that spell or ability is countered unless its controller pays the ward cost. Sedgemoor Witch has ward that costs 3 life. Ward makes removal more expensive without making a creature untouchable.

### Modal double-faced cards

Strixhaven continued the modal double-faced cards from Zendikar Rising and Kaldheim. You choose which face to cast. Each college has a pair of Deans on one card, such as Valentin, Dean of the Vein and Lisette, Dean of the Root, and the mythic MDFCs include Mila, Crafty Companion // Lukka, Wayward Bonder and Rowan, Scholar of Sparks // Will, Scholar of Frost.

### The Mystical Archive

Strixhaven Draft Boosters each contained a card from the Mystical Archive, a sheet of 63 iconic instants and sorceries such as Lightning Bolt, Counterspell, Demonic Tutor, Swords to Plowshares and Time Warp. They had no impact on Standard, but they made opening packs exciting and put new versions of Commander staples into circulation.

## Limited

Strixhaven had five archetypes instead of ten, one for each college. Each college had a gold Apprentice uncommon with magecraft and a Command at rare:

| College | Colors | Draft plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silverquill | White-black | Inklings and spells that target your own creatures, with +1/+1 counters |
| Prismari | Blue-red | Big spells, Elementals and Treasure |
| Witherbloom | Black-green | Pests and lifegain |
| Lorehold | Red-white | Cards leaving your graveyard, Spirits |
| Quandrix | Green-blue | Fractals, +1/+1 counters and lots of lands |

Drafters could also wander into other combinations, since the allied pairs had some support of their own, but the college pairs were where the gold cards lived. The format rewarded counting your instants and sorceries during the draft: a magecraft creature with only six spells to trigger it is a blank. Lessons gave sideboards real value, so picking a couple of good ones (Introduction to Annihilation is a removal spell for any deck) was common. Removal like Mage Hunters' Onslaught and Heated Debate went early, and flyers from Silverquill and Prismari decided a lot of games. ## The meta: how it played in Constructed

### In Standard (April 2021 to September 2022)

Strixhaven joined a crowded Standard and supplied efficient interaction rather than defining a deck on its own. Expressive Iteration and Divide by Zero became core pieces of Izzet spell decks, including the Izzet Epiphany "turns" deck built around Kaldheim's Alrund's Epiphany. That deck dominated enough that on January 25, 2022, Wizards banned Alrund's Epiphany, Divide by Zero and Faceless Haven in Standard. Elite Spellbinder gave white aggro decks a disruptive flier, and Vanishing Verse was a cheap answer in Orzhov and Esper builds. Strixhaven rotated out of Standard on September 9, 2022 (September 1 on MTG Arena), together with Zendikar Rising, Kaldheim and Adventures in the Forgotten Realms.

### In Pioneer, Modern and Legacy

Expressive Iteration is the set's biggest eternal card. It was strong enough in Izzet tempo decks that Wizards banned it in Pioneer on June 7, 2022 and in Legacy on March 6, 2023. As of October 2026 it remains legal in Modern, where it has been a regular in Izzet Murktide and other blue-red tempo decks. Prismari Command also turned up in Pioneer Izzet decks, and Strict Proctor is a known sideboard tool against decks that rely on enter-the-battlefield triggers.

### In Commander

Strixhaven is a spellslinger's playground in Commander. Archmage Emeritus, Professor Onyx, Velomachus Lorehold and the five elder dragons are all popular, and Strixhaven Stadium offers a quirky alternate win. Five Commander 2021 precons, one per college, released alongside the set and introduced commanders such as Osgir, the Reconstructor and Zaffai, Thunder Conductor.

## Cards from Strixhaven worth owning

- **Expressive Iteration**: a Modern staple for blue-red tempo, and a strong Commander card for spell decks.
- **Archmage Emeritus**: a card-draw engine for any Commander spellslinger deck.
- **Prismari Command**: a flexible instant that fits Izzet decks in Pioneer and Modern.
- **Vanishing Verse**: one of the most efficient Orzhov removal spells ever printed.
- **Elite Spellbinder**: disruption on a flier for white decks.
- **Mystical Archive reprints**: Swords to Plowshares, Counterspell and Demonic Tutor are Commander staples in their nicest frames.

## The takeaway

Strixhaven is best remembered for two things: a beloved school setting and a handful of cheap spells that were too efficient for their formats. In Pioneer today, Prismari Command and the magecraft creatures still give Izzet spell decks a backbone even without Expressive Iteration.
