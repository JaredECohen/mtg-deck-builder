## Short answer

A voltron deck needs enough auras or equipment that the creature is lethal in one or two attacks, and enough extra creatures or protection that one removal spell does not end your game. In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), voltron means one creature wearing the rest of the deck. The math is harsh: every aura in your hand does nothing until a creature is on the battlefield, and every aura on a creature that dies goes to the graveyard with it. You do not need twenty auras if six of them already kill. You need the creature to stay in play, or another creature to wear the next wave.

Orzhov Auras, the 2021 Pioneer Challenger product, is the 60-card version. Thirty-one nonlands cost one mana. The curve is the strategy: the creature and the auras have to come down immediately. The goldfish average win is turn 8.09, slower than burn, because auras spend cards to make one body larger instead of adding more attackers. Lurrus of the Dream-Den is in the printed list and is not legal today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01. The companion was the card that made dying auras easy to replay. Without it, the printed deck is a historical object, not a registration sheet. Thoughtseize and Sram are the other price centers, inside a $46.84 total.

Virtue and Valor, Ellivere of the Wild Court, is the Commander version at $127.24. Bear Umbra, Retether, and Hall of Heliod's Generosity are the top nonbasics, 30.1% of the product. Retether and the Hall are cards that get auras back. That is the Commander answer to the same problem Lurrus used to solve in Pioneer: auras in the graveyard are not on a creature. The [tempo page](/blog/mtg-tempo-decks-explained) is the small-creature comparison when you would rather have many bodies than one enchanted one.

## When to choose this

| Situation | Lean in | Walk away |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60-card auras | 31 one-drops, turn 8.09, and a banned companion | Do not register Lurrus |
| Commander auras | Ellivere, with Retether and the Hall as the recursion | $127.24, 30.1% in three cards |
| One removal spell ends your turn | You need a second creature or a way to replay auras | A single creature and a hand of enchantments is a mulligan |
| You want many attackers | Voltron is the wrong shape | Tokens or a normal aggro curve |

### Orzhov Auras

Orzhov Auras is the 2021-10-15 Pioneer Challenger Deck stored as MTGJSON file OrzhovAuras_Q06. Wizards source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/news/pioneer-challenger-decks-2021-08-24.

The creature has to be on the battlefield before the auras do anything. A hand of auras and no creature is a mulligan even though the curve is full of one-drops. Lurrus, the companion that used to replay the auras after the creature died, is not a card you can register in Pioneer today. Thoughtseize has to take the removal spell, not a random card, because the life you pay is the race.

Orzhov Auras's nonland curve is 2 at 0, 31 at 1, 8 at 2, and it plays 19 lands in the main deck.

Orzhov Auras has 15 white, 12 black mana sources and 19 land cards.

Orzhov Auras won 93.3% of 120 goldfish games, average winning turn 8.09 (median 8.0, seed 1729, aggro policy, opponent at 20 life).

### Virtue and Valor

Virtue and Valor, commanded by Ellivere of the Wild Court is the 2023-09-08 Commander Deck stored as MTGJSON file VirtueAndValor_WOC. Wizards source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/wilds-of-eldraine-commander-decklists.

Ellivere's recursion is the Commander answer to auras in the graveyard. Retether and Hall of Heliod's Generosity do nothing if you have not put auras in the yard, and they are a large piece of the receipt. One removal spell on the enchanted creature is the game you built the deck to survive. A second creature, or a way to get the auras back, is the actual count. Six auras that already kill are enough. Twenty auras and one creature is a hand that does nothing the turn the creature dies.

Virtue and Valor's nonland curve is 5 at 1, 16 at 2, 15 at 3, 11 at 4, 7 at 5, 5 at 6, 1 at 7+, and it plays 39 lands in the 99.

Virtue and Valor has 25 white, 26 green mana sources and 39 land cards.

On the 2026-11-01 AllPricesToday nonfoil printings, Virtue and Valor is $127.24 for main deck, sideboard, and commander. The three most expensive nonbasic names are 1 Bear Umbra ($14.51), 1 Retether ($12.25), 1 Hall of Heliod's Generosity ($11.58), 30.1% of that priced total.

Virtue and Valor won 96.7% of 120 goldfish games, average winning turn 10.54 (median 10.0, seed 1729, midrange policy, opponent at 40 life).

Every card in Virtue and Valor is legal in commander today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01.

## Common mistakes

- Registering the Pioneer auras product as printed. Lurrus is not legal. The goldfish was run on the printed list anyway so you can see the curve, not so you can copy it.
- Drawing seven auras and no creature and keeping the hand. The one-drop count is high so this happens less often. It still happens, and the hand does nothing.
- Spending Thoughtseize on a random card and then losing the creature you needed the life total to race with. Thoughtseize is $6.63 of a $46.84 product. It has to take the removal spell.
- Buying Ellivere's expensive recursion and not playing enough auras to recur. Retether is a blank if the graveyard is empty.
- Equating auras and equipment without checking what happens when the creature dies. Some equipment stays. Auras do not. The Pioneer list is auras.

## FAQ

### How many auras is enough?

Enough that one creature becomes lethal, which on these curves means a lot of one-mana auras in the 60-card list (31 one-drops, not all of them auras) and a Commander list that can get the auras back. Count the auras that actually grant evasion or power, not every enchantment.

### Can I play Lurrus in Pioneer?

No. Lurrus of the Dream-Den is not legal today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01. The 2021 product included it as a companion.

### Why is the Commander aura deck almost three times the price?

Virtue and Valor is $127.24 against the Pioneer product's $46.84. Bear Umbra alone is $14.51, and the recursion pieces are the rest of the top three. Different formats, different expensive cards.

### Is voltron the same as tempo?

Tempo uses several small creatures and disruption. Voltron uses one creature and a stack of auras. Spirits goldfishes faster than the auras product because the creatures are the threats, not the costumes.
