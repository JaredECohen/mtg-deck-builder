Commander 2014 is the Magic: The Gathering preconstructed Commander product released in November 2014: five mono-color 100-card decks, each led by a planeswalker that can be your commander, the first time the rules allowed it. It introduced the lieutenant ability word, gave legendary cards to five famous characters from Magic's past, and printed everyday Commander cards like Commander's Sphere, Myriad Landscape and Arcane Lighthouse, plus the Legacy sideboard staple Containment Priest.

## What the set was for

Commander's annual decks had been three-color in 2013. For 2014, lead designer Ethan Fleischer and developer Ian Duke went the other way: one deck per color, built to show what mono-color Commander looks like, and each led by a planeswalker. A line on each card, "can be your commander," made that legal for the first time.

The set is also a history lesson. Its five planeswalkers are shown at moments in their past, before later story events changed them:

- **Nahiri, the Lithomancer** before she was sealed away in the Helvault on Innistrad, which later set up the story of Shadows over Innistrad.
- **Teferi, Temporal Archmage** before he gave up his spark to save his homeland in Time Spiral.
- **Ob Nixilis of the Black Oath** before he became a demon.
- **Freyalise, Llanowar's Fury** before she died sealing a time rift.
- **Daretti, Scrap Savant**, a goblin artificer from the plane of Fiora (the setting of Conspiracy).

It also gave long-mentioned characters their first cards: Jazal Goldmane, Stitcher Geralf, Ghoulcaller Gisa, Feldon of the Third Path and Titania, Protector of Argoth. A short story, "The Lithomancer," ran alongside the release and showed Nahiri's part, alongside Sorin and Ugin, in imprisoning the Eldrazi on Zendikar.

## The decks

| Deck | Color | Commander | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forged in Stone | White | Nahiri, the Lithomancer | Equipment and Soldier tokens |
| Peer Through Time | Blue | Teferi, Temporal Archmage | Artifacts, untapping, control |
| Sworn to Darkness | Black | Ob Nixilis of the Black Oath | Zombies, sacrifice and life drain |
| Built from Scratch | Red | Daretti, Scrap Savant | Artifacts in the graveyard |
| Guided by Nature | Green | Freyalise, Llanowar's Fury | Elves and mana |

- **Nahiri** makes Kor Soldier tokens and attaches Equipment to them, cheats Equipment into play, and has an ultimate that creates an indestructible +5/+5 double strike sword.
- **Teferi** digs for cards, untaps up to four permanents, and with his ultimate lets you activate planeswalker abilities at instant speed.
- **Ob Nixilis** drains each opponent, makes 5/5 flying Demons, and his ultimate turns sacrificed creatures into cards and life.
- **Daretti** loots and swaps artifacts between your graveyard and the battlefield.
- **Freyalise** makes mana Elves, destroys artifacts and enchantments, and draws a card for each green creature you control.

Each deck also came with a legendary creature that matches its theme, all reprints: Kemba, Kha Regent; Lorthos, the Tidemaker; Drana, Kalastria Bloodchief; Bosh, Iron Golem; and Ezuri, Renegade Leader.

### The historic legends

The five "first card ever" legends were designed top-down, starting from the character, and each works as an alternate commander for its deck:

- **Jazal Goldmane** (white), a first strike 4/4 that pumps your attackers by the number of attacking creatures.
- **Stitcher Geralf** (blue) mills each player and stitches two creature cards into one big Zombie token.
- **Ghoulcaller Gisa** (black), Geralf's sister, sacrifices a creature to make a number of 2/2 Zombies equal to its power. The siblings' cards reference each other: Geralf makes one big Zombie, Gisa splits a big creature into many small ones.
- **Feldon of the Third Path** (red) makes a temporary artifact copy of a creature card in your graveyard.
- **Titania, Protector of Argoth** (green) returns a land when she enters and makes a 5/3 Elemental whenever one of your lands goes to the graveyard.

### New mechanics

**Lieutenant.** An ability word for bonuses that turn on while you control your commander. The five rare lieutenants each get +2/+2 and an extra ability. Thunderfoot Baloth, for example, also gives your other creatures +2/+2 and trample, and Tyrant's Familiar deals 7 damage to a creature whenever it attacks. The catch is that removing your commander turns them off.

**Offerings.** A cycle of five rares, each with two effects that you share with an opponent you choose. Sylvan Offering gives you and that opponent an X/X Treefolk, then you and an opponent each get X Elf Warriors. They're political cards: you choose who benefits alongside you.

**Double-faced tokens.** The decks included tokens printed on both sides, a packaging change that later Commander products kept.

The reprints were heavy on staples: Sol Ring, Cyclonic Rift, Skullclamp, Wurmcoil Engine, Grave Titan, Sun Titan, Solemn Simulacrum, Reliquary Tower, Swiftfoot Boots, Thran Dynamo, Martial Coup, Grand Abolisher and the five Medallions, among many others.

## How it plays

Mono-color decks have perfect mana but fewer answers, so each of these decks has a clear identity and clear weaknesses. Out of the box they sit at the casual end of the [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained). As of October 2026, Cyclonic Rift in Peer Through Time is the only card in the five decks on the official Game Changers list.

- **Forged in Stone** is an aggressive Equipment deck. It's strong early but runs out of steam if its Equipment gets destroyed.
- **Peer Through Time** is a slow artifact control deck. Teferi's untap ability pairs with mana rocks for big turns.
- **Sworn to Darkness** is one of the most consistent: Ob Nixilis drains every turn, and the Zombie and sacrifice package makes it resilient.
- **Built from Scratch** recycles artifacts with Daretti and can be powerful but needs practice.
- **Guided by Nature** is the classic Elf ramp deck, with Freyalise making extra Elves and plenty of Elf lords.

Upgrade advice is mostly the same as for any precon, see our [precon upgrade guide](/blog/how-to-upgrade-a-commander-precon): the 2014 decks have several clunky seven-drops and slow lands that can be swapped for cheaper interaction.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Commander 2014's new cards weren't legal in Standard, Pioneer or Modern when it came out, so its impact landed in Commander and Legacy.

- **Containment Priest** became a Legacy and Vintage sideboard card against reanimator and Show and Tell decks, and later a Modern sideboard option once it was reprinted in a Modern-legal set.
- **Dualcaster Mage** paired with Twinflame or Heat Shimmer for an infinite combo of hasty copies, mostly in Commander and casual play.
- **Commander's Sphere** became one of the most played mana rocks in Commander for years.
- **Myriad Landscape** and **Arcane Lighthouse** became Commander regulars, Lighthouse especially as an answer to hexproof commanders.
- **Song of the Dryads** became popular removal for commanders, since it leaves the creature on the battlefield as a land instead of sending it to the command zone.

In Commander, none of the set's new cards has been banned as of October 2026. Some reprints have ban histories in other formats (Skullclamp is banned in Modern and Legacy), but those predate the set.

## Cards from Commander 2014 worth owning

- **Commander's Sphere**: color fixing and card draw in any deck.
- **Myriad Landscape**: two basics for one land, great in two-color decks.
- **Arcane Lighthouse**: a free answer to hexproof threats.
- **Song of the Dryads**: removal that sticks.
- **Containment Priest**: a Legacy and Commander graveyard hoser.
- **Titania, Protector of Argoth**: a strong payoff in lands-matter decks.
- **Masterwork of Ingenuity**: a cheap extra copy of your best Equipment.

## The takeaway

Commander 2014 is a good look at how mono-color Commander plays, and it's the set that made planeswalker commanders a thing.
