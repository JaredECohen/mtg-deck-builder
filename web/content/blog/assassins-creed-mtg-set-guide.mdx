Assassin's Creed is the Universes Beyond set that brought Ubisoft's stealth-action series to Magic: The Gathering, released in July 2024. It's a compact, roughly 100-card set that went straight into Modern and the eternal formats, built around Assassin creatures, the new freerunning mechanic and a heavy dose of historic cards.

## The story

Assassin's Creed is a series about a secret war. The Assassins fight for free will, and the Templars want order through control, and both sides hunt for Pieces of Eden, artifacts left behind by an ancient precursor civilization called the Isu. The games tell their stories through the Animus, a machine that lets people in the present day relive the genetic memories of their ancestors. The Magic set uses that framing directly: Desmond Miles, the modern-day hero of the early games, has his own card, and The Animus is a legendary artifact that lets your legends become copies of creature cards you've "remembered."

Instead of retelling one game, the set visits many of them. Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad represents the original game's Holy Land setting, Ezio Auditore da Firenze carries the Renaissance Italy trilogy, and Ratonhnhaké꞉ton covers the American Revolution. Edward Kenway, Adéwalé, Breaker of Chains and Mary Read and Anne Bonny bring the pirate era of Black Flag, with Jackdaw, Edward's ship, as a Vehicle. Arno Dorian comes from revolutionary Paris, Evie Frye and Jacob Frye from Victorian London, and Shay Cormac represents the Assassin who turned Templar.

The ancient-world games get a big share too. Bayek of Siwa and Aya of Alexandria represent Origins in Ptolemaic Egypt, Cleopatra appears as Cleopatra, Exiled Pharaoh, and Kassandra, Eagle Bearer and Alexios, Deimos of Kosmos bring Odyssey's Greece, along with The Spear of Leonidas and Sokrates, Athenian Teacher. Valhalla's Viking saga is represented by Eivor, Wolf-Kissed, Sigurd, Jarl of Ravensthorpe and Havi, the All-Father, and Basim Ibn Ishaq brings Mirage's Baghdad.

The Isu thread ties it all together. The Capitoline Triad stands in for the precursor gods, What Must Be Done names a choice to release Juno, and the Pieces of Eden appear as legendary artifacts: Apple of Eden, Isu Relic, Staff of Eden, Vault's Key, Crystal Skull, Isu Spyglass and Excalibur, Sword of Eden. Haytham Kenway, a Knight with protection from Assassins, gives the Templars a commander of their own.

## Mechanics

### Freerunning

Freerunning is an alternative cost. If you dealt combat damage to a player this turn with an Assassin or your commander, you may cast the spell for its freerunning cost instead of its normal cost. Eagle Vision normally costs five mana to draw three cards, but you can freerun it for `{1}{U}`. Chain Assassination is a four-mana removal spell that drops to `{1}{B}` once you've connected. In practice, freerunning rewards you for attacking first in your main phase and casting spells afterward, and it plays especially well in Commander because your commander always counts.

### Assassins matter

Many cards care about the Assassin creature type. Payoffs such as Ezio Auditore da Firenze, which gives every Assassin spell you cast freerunning `{B}{B}`, push you toward a tribal deck. Roshan, Hidden Magister makes all your other creatures Assassins, and Brotherhood Regalia turns any equipped creature into an unblockable Assassin.

### Historic

Historic means artifacts, legendaries and Sagas, and it's the set's other big theme. Basim Ibn Ishaq draws a card whenever you cast a historic spell, Excalibur, Sword of Eden costs less based on the total mana value of your historic permanents, and Desynchronization bounces every nonland permanent that isn't historic. Since the set is packed with legendary creatures and Equipment, most decks built from it end up historic-heavy.

### Equipment, disguise, cloak and memory

Hidden blades and other weapons appear as Equipment, many of which attach themselves when they enter, like Hidden Blade and Hookblade. Disguise (from Murders at Karlov Manor) returns on cards like Bayek of Siwa and Arno Dorian: you can cast them face down for `{3}` as a 2/2 with ward `{2}`, then flip them up for their disguise cost. Become Anonymous uses cloak. Memory counters mark creature cards exiled by Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad and The Animus, which can then copy those remembered creatures.

## How it plays

Assassin's Creed wasn't built as a draft set. Its seven-card Beyond Boosters are meant for collecting and opening, not booster draft, so the set's real home is constructed play, especially Commander. Most of its legends are designed as commanders, and the color pairs map onto clear deck ideas:

- **Blue-black Assassins:** Basim Ibn Ishaq and Lydia Frye reward cheap evasive attackers, and freerunning lets you cast your spells after combat.
- **Red-white Equipment:** Kassandra, Eagle Bearer, Bayek of Siwa and Aya of Alexandria lean on legendary weapons, double strike and lifelink.
- **Grixis pirates:** Edward Kenway turns tapped attackers into Treasure and steals cards with Vehicles.
- **Mardu Assassins:** Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad recycles Assassins from your graveyard as attacking copies.
- **Naya Vikings:** Eivor, Wolf-Kissed and Sigurd, Jarl of Ravensthorpe care about Sagas and lands.

The Starter Kit is the easiest way to try it out of the box. Its red-white deck, Ancient Arsenal, and blue-black deck, Brotherhood Agents, come with their own exclusive legends, and those exclusive cards are Modern-legal too. If you're new to building around a commander, start with [how to build your first Commander deck](/blog/how-to-build-your-first-commander-deck).

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Assassin's Creed went directly into Modern, but its new cards didn't become fixtures of the top competitive Modern decks in the months after release, as far as tournament coverage shows. The set's Constructed value came mostly from reprints of cards that already saw play, like Fatal Push, Path to Exile and Assassin's Trophy.

Its lasting footprint is in Commander. Black Market Connections became the set's most-played new card in Commander by EDHREC's rankings, since it offers ramp, card draw or a body every turn in exchange for life, which is cheap in a 40-life format. Ezio Auditore da Firenze, Edward Kenway and Altaïr Ibn-La'Ahad all got built as commanders, and Crystal Skull, Isu Spyglass found a home in artifact and legendary decks. As of October 2026, none of the set's cards are on the Commander banned list, and Scryfall lists none of them as Game Changers. For how that affects power levels, see [Commander brackets explained](/blog/commander-brackets-explained), and for the format's basics, [what is Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh).

## Cards from Assassin's Creed worth owning

- **Black Market Connections:** a strong, flexible engine for any black Commander deck.
- **Ezio Auditore da Firenze:** a cheap, aggressive mono-black commander for Assassin tribal.
- **Crystal Skull, Isu Spyglass:** top-of-library card advantage for historic decks.
- **Desynchronization:** a one-sided-ish reset for artifact and legend decks.
- **Excalibur, Sword of Eden:** a splashy finisher for legendary-heavy Voltron decks.
- **The reprints:** Path to Exile, Fatal Push and Assassin's Trophy are useful in many formats.
