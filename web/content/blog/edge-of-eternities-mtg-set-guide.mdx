Edge of Eternities is Magic: The Gathering's first full science-fiction premier set, released in August 2025. It takes the game off any familiar plane and into a distant star system called Sothera, where spaceships, planets and a collapsing sun replace castles and forests. It also marked a Standard rotation, and several of its cards, from Quantum Riddler to Mightform Harmonizer, became pillars of Standard for the following year.

## The story

Edge of Eternities is set in the Sothera system, a region at the edge of the Multiverse whose star is collapsing into a supervoid. Several cultures share the system: the Eumidians, the Kav, the Illvoi, and two rival religious movements, the Monoists and the Summists, who see the dying star very differently. Magic's old enemies the Eldrazi lurk in the background.

The plot follows the crew of a ship called the Seriema. Its engineer, Sami, is a scrappy artificer whose real goal is surprisingly personal: finding their missing cat, Mirri. With Sami is Tannuk, a Kavu pilot. The crew ends up holding the Endstone, a powerful artifact that everyone wants. Syr Vondam of the Sunstar Free Company believes he is destined to claim it, the Monoist Alpharael seeks it for his faith, and the planeswalker Tezzeret joins the Seriema with plans of his own.

Haliya, a squire of the Sunstar knights, is sent to steal the stone from the Seriema and fails. Over the story she turns against Vondam after his order massacres innocent Kav, and their conflict becomes one of the story's main threads. The chase takes the crew to Infinite Guideline Station, where Monoists, Summists and Eldrazi clash, and where Sami finally finds Mirri frozen in a stasis cask.

In the finale, Haliya refuses to return to the Free Company and leaves Vondam paralyzed rather than killing him. A mysterious masked being reclaims the Endstone through a portal, leaving a cryptic message, and Tezzeret seems satisfied simply to have seen who it belongs to. The crew escapes toward the Wurmwall at the edge of Sothera. Sami gets their cat back, Tezzeret gets a glimpse of something greater, and the bigger mysteries are left open.

Many of those story beats are on cards: Sami, Wildcat Captain, Tannuk, Steadfast Second, Haliya, Guided by Light, Syr Vondam, Sunstar Exemplar, Alpharael, Stonechosen, Tezzeret, Cruel Captain, The Seriema, The Endstone and Infinite Guideline Station.

## Mechanics

### Station, Spacecraft and Planets

Station is the set's signature. A Spacecraft or Planet with station lets you tap another creature you control, as a sorcery, to put charge counters on it equal to that creature's power. At each printed threshold, the permanent gains new abilities. Most Spacecraft become artifact creatures at their top threshold; Planets are lands that never become creatures but unlock powerful mana or utility abilities.

Lumen-Class Frigate is a two-mana white Spacecraft that gives your other creatures +1/+1 once it has two charge counters, and becomes a 3/5 flying, lifelink creature at 12. Evendo, Waking Haven is a green Planet that taps for `{G}` and, at 12 charge counters, can produce `{G}` for each creature you control. Station turns creatures that can't attack well into progress on something bigger.

### Warp

Warp is an alternative cost from your hand. Cast the card for its warp cost and it gets exiled at the beginning of the next end step; after that, you may cast it from exile on a later turn. In effect, you get a cheap one-turn version now and the full card later. Quantum Riddler costs five but can warp in for `{1}{U}` to draw a card, then return as a full 4/6 flier. Nova Hellkite warps in for `{2}{R}`, pings a creature and attacks with haste, then comes back later. Warp is great with enter-the-battlefield effects.

### Void

Void is an ability word that checks whether a nonland permanent left the battlefield this turn or a spell was warped this turn. Plasma Bolt deals 2 damage normally, or 3 if void is on. Decode Transmissions draws two cards and drains opponents instead of costing you life when void is active. Warp turns void on by itself, so the two mechanics work together.

### Lander tokens and landfall

A Lander is an artifact token: pay `{2}`, tap and sacrifice it to search for a basic land and put it onto the battlefield tapped. Galactic Wayfarer and Sami's Curiosity make them at common. They smooth your mana and trigger landfall, which returns in green and red. Mightform Harmonizer doubles a creature's power whenever a land enters under your control.

## Limited

Edge of Eternities has ten two-color archetypes, each signposted by a gold uncommon:

- **White-blue second spell:** Station Monitor makes flying Drone tokens when you cast your second spell each turn.
- **Blue-black artifacts and card flow:** Alpharael, Dreaming Acolyte draws and filters.
- **Black-red void:** Interceptor Mechan returns cards from your graveyard and rewards things leaving the battlefield.
- **Red-green landfall:** Tannuk, Memorial Ensign pings opponents whenever a land enters.
- **Green-white +1/+1 counters:** Haliya, Ascendant Cadet spreads counters.
- **White-black aggressive midrange:** Syr Vondam, the Lucent gives your team +1/+0 and deathtouch when he enters or attacks.
- **Blue-red artifacts:** Mm'menon, Uthros Exile puts a counter on a creature whenever an artifact enters.
- **Black-green graveyard and sacrifice:** Seedship Broodtender mills three and can later sacrifice itself to return a creature or Spacecraft card to the battlefield.
- **Red-white tapped creatures and station:** Sami, Ship's Engineer makes Robots when you have two or more tapped creatures.
- **Green-blue Landers and ramp:** Biomechan Engineer makes a Lander and turns extra mana into cards late in the game.

The commons are deep in removal: Gravkill, Bombard, Embrace Oblivion, Banishing Light, Orbital Plunge and Radiant Strike are all strong picks. Warp creatures are excellent because they give you a cheap early play and a full card later. Spacecraft vary a lot; cheap ones with good early thresholds are much better than expensive ones that need a lot of creature power to turn on.

Station and warp both reward board presence and a smooth [mana curve](/blog/mana-curve-explained), and Lander tokens make splashing easier than usual. Because Spider-Man was designed mainly for four-player Pick-Two Draft, Edge of Eternities stayed the primary eight-player draft format for the Pro Tour that fall.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Edge of Eternities had more Standard impact than most premier sets, partly because it arrived at a rotation.

Quantum Riddler became one of the defining Standard cards of late 2025. It was a key piece of Izzet Cauldron, the deck Wizards described as the strongest in Standard in its November 10, 2025 ban announcement. That update banned Vivi Ornitier, Screaming Nemesis and Proft's Eidetic Memory; no Edge of Eternities card was banned. Quantum Riddler kept appearing afterward in blue decks, including a Simic Nature's Rhythm list that won the 1,025-player Regional Championship in Turin in March 2026, which also played Ouroboroid and Seam Rip.

Green landfall was the other big story. Icetill Explorer and Mightform Harmonizer were singled out by Wizards as strong additions to mono-green landfall right after rotation, and by September 2026 Mono-Green Landfall was the most successful Standard deck in RCQs, with about 28% of the winner's metagame in Wizards' analysis. Selesnya Landfall versions add Dyadrine, Synthesis Amalgam. Elsewhere, Haliya, Guided by Light shows up in Orzhov life gain, Umbral Collar Zealot and Biotech Specialist in sacrifice decks, and Devastating Onslaught in Temur Ferocious lists.

In Modern, Quantum Riddler found a home as a blink and reanimation target in Esper Goryo's Vengeance and Phelia blink decks around Pro Tour Edge of Eternities in September 2025. Tezzeret, Cruel Captain picked up play in Vintage artifact decks. In Commander, Ouroboroid, Exalted Sunborn and The Eternity Elevator are popular, and the Planets give every color a strong utility land.

## Cards from Edge of Eternities worth owning

- **Quantum Riddler:** a Standard and Modern playable that keeps finding homes in blue decks.
- **Icetill Explorer:** a lands-matter engine for Standard landfall and Commander.
- **Mightform Harmonizer:** the payoff that made mono-green landfall the top Standard deck in 2026.
- **Ouroboroid:** explosive growth for green creature decks in Standard and Commander.
- **Seam Rip:** cheap, flexible removal that fits main decks and sideboards.
- **The shock lands:** essential for multicolor mana in Standard, Pioneer and Commander.

## The takeaway

Edge of Eternities rewards building around its mechanics rather than splashing them: play enough creatures to station your ships, use warp to get two uses out of key creatures, and let Landers fuel landfall. In Limited, take removal and cheap warp creatures early. In Standard, it's one of the most important sets in the pool, and green landfall is the clearest example of [a deck archetype](/blog/mtg-deck-archetypes-explained) it built.
