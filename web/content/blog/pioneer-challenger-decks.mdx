## Short answer

In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), Pioneer Challenger decks are retail products, not a metagame report. MTGJSON publishes eight of them, and this page is only those lists. Each is a sixty-card main deck plus a sideboard. Every card had an English paper nonfoil price on 2026-11-01, so none of these sums is missing a name. Those sums are the lowest such price. The deck block is the printing in the box. Definitions are on the [method page](/method).

Land counts run from 19 in Orzhov Auras to 26 in Dimir Control. Gruul Stompy plays 25. The [Commander census](/blog/precon-lands-ramp-draw) lives in a different band entirely. The cheap end of this file is Azorius Spirits at $16.20. The expensive end is Lotus Field Combo at $43.67.

Izzet Phoenix includes Expressive Iteration. Orzhov Auras includes Lurrus of the Dream-Den. Those are the only Pioneer legality flags on the 2026-11-01 bulk. Dimir Control includes Narset, Parter of Veils, which is a Commander Game Changer sitting in a Pioneer product. It is legal in Pioneer on this bulk. Do not register Phoenix or Auras as printed. Check the ban list again before you submit anything else, because a bulk date is a snapshot.

## Curve, overlap, and what each deck is doing

| Dimir Control | 26 | 0 | 4 | 12 | 8 | $29.21 |

| Izzet Phoenix | 21 | 0 | 23 | 10 | 15 | $27.41 |

| Orzhov Auras | 19 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 31 | $34.75 |
| Orzhov Humans | 22 | 0 | 0 | 6 | 16 | $38.56 |

Read the one-drop column before the archetype label. Orzhov Auras has 31 one-drops and 0 removal: the creature and the aura want to be down immediately, and the deck does not interact by killing things. Mono-Red Burn has 20 one-drops and a removal count of 16, which is Lightning Strike, Wild Slash, Wizard's Lightning, and Bonecrusher Giant. Under this count, damage to any target is removal even when you point it at the player. The draw count is 4, all Light Up the Stage, and ramp is 0.

Azorius Spirits and Orzhov Humans both peak at mana value 2: 22 cards in Spirits and 22 in Humans. Spirits draws 4 and removes 0. Humans draws 0 and removes 6. Humans is a creature deck that expects to be ahead before a four-drop arrives. Spirits spends its cheap interaction on the spell that would stabilize the opponent.

Gruul Stompy's ramp count is 10, from Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, and Chandra, Torch of Defiance. Chandra also deals damage, so the removal count of 13 includes her, and draw is 0. The curve tops out higher than those one-drops, so calling the deck burn because it is red misses the land count and the elves.

Izzet Phoenix has 23 draw cards, mostly cantrips, and it still has 4 cards in the top mana-value bucket. Expressive Iteration is the spell the deck is known for. It is not a cantrip, and it is not legal. Lotus Field Combo ramps 4, draws 8, and removes 0. The combo is the land and the mana, not a combat plan, and it does not remove permanents, which is the tell. Dimir Control is the other end of the same format: 26 lands, 12 removal, 4 draw. It is the list that is allowed to have a hand full of answers.

The lists also share a few specific cards, which is a shared tool and not a shared strategy. Bonecrusher Giant // Stomp shows up in Gruul Stompy and Mono-Red Burn. Caves of Koilos shows up in Orzhov Auras and Orzhov Humans. Concealed Courtyard shows up in Orzhov Auras and Orzhov Humans. Consider shows up in Dimir Control and Izzet Phoenix. Dig Through Time shows up in Dimir Control and Lotus Field Combo. Opt shows up in Izzet Phoenix and Lotus Field Combo. A pain land in both Orzhov lists, or a cantrip in both a control deck and a combo deck, is as much of a metagame as these sealed products can show.

## Common mistakes

- Reading Mono-Red Burn as the low land count. The table's low end is Orzhov Auras, and Gruul Stompy is a different red deck.
- Registering Izzet Phoenix or Orzhov Auras as printed. The bulk of 2026-11-01 flags Expressive Iteration and Lurrus of the Dream-Den.
- Reading Burn's removal count of 16 as creature removal. The rule counts damage to any target.
- Treating these products, from separate seasons, as a Pioneer metagame. There is no licensed standings feed behind this page.

## FAQ

### Which lists can I register?

Phoenix and Auras are the lists with a legality flag on 2026-11-01, named above. The others had no Pioneer ban or not-legal flag on that bulk. A ban printed after that date is not in this file.

### Why does Gruul count as ramp?

The elves add green, and Chandra adds mana as well as dealing damage. The ramp count is 10. Burn's ramp count is 0.

### What do the shared cards mean?

They mean separate products wanted the same tool. Bonecrusher Giant // Stomp shows up in Gruul Stompy and Mono-Red Burn. They do not mean the decks share an archetype. Lotus Field still removes 0 permanents, and Dimir removes 12.
