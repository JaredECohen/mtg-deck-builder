## Short answer

In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), Pioneer Challenger Decks are official Wizards products, and MTGJSON republishes them under the same MIT grant as the Commander precons. This snapshot has 8 of them. There is no ninth list hiding in the file. Each one is a sixty-card main deck plus a sideboard. This page does not invent a metagame around them. It counts what those eight published lists contain, prices them from the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01, and says which cards are not legal in Pioneer on that same bulk.

Land counts run from 19 in Orzhov Auras to 26 in Dimir Control. That is a constructed band, not the Commander median from the [precon census](/blog/precon-lands-ramp-draw). Every one of the eight had a nonfoil Scryfall price for every card, so none of these totals is missing a name. The cheapest sum is Azorius Spirits at $16.20. The dearest is Lotus Field Combo at $43.67. Those are lowest-English-nonfoil sums, not the product-printing totals in the deck blocks. The [method page](/method) is the definition.

Two cards in these products are not Pioneer-legal on the 2026-11-01 Scryfall bulk: Expressive Iteration in Izzet Phoenix, and Lurrus of the Dream-Den in Orzhov Auras. The other six products had no card flagged banned or not legal in Pioneer. Legality changes. The bulk date is the date of the sentence.

Ramp, draw, and removal use the same sentence rules as the Commander census. A mana elf counts as ramp. A cantrip counts as draw. A spell that deals damage to any target counts as removal even when the plan is to point it at a player. That last rule is why Mono-Red Burn's removal count is 16: Lightning Strike, Wild Slash, Wizard's Lightning, and Bonecrusher Giant. Its draw count is 4, and the only draw card is Light Up the Stage. Its ramp count is 0.

Gruul Stompy's ramp count is 10, from Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, and Chandra, Torch of Defiance. Chandra also deals damage, so she is in the removal count of 13 as well. A card in two counts is not two cards. Izzet Phoenix's draw count is 23 because the list is cantrips. Lotus Field Combo's ramp count is 4 and its removal count is 0. The combo is not a removal plan, and this table does not pretend the goldfish can perform it.

## What not to conclude

These eight lists are products from two Challenger seasons. They are not a Pioneer metagame report, and this site does not have a licensed feed of current Pioneer standings. A card's presence here means Wizards put it in a Challenger deck, which MTGJSON republishes. It does not mean the card won a tournament last weekend.

Azorius Spirits at $16.20 is the cheap end of this particular file. Lotus Field Combo at $43.67 is the expensive end. Neither number is a format staple's price, and neither is the cost of the product's original printings. Sideboards are in the Scryfall sum and in the deck block. They are not in the land count, which is the main deck only.

## Common mistakes

- Registering Izzet Phoenix as printed. Expressive Iteration is not legal in Pioneer on the 2026-11-01 bulk.
- Registering Orzhov Auras as printed. Lurrus of the Dream-Den is the card the bulk flags.
- Reading Mono-Red Burn's removal count of 16 as sixteen pieces of creature removal. The rule counts damage to any target. The plan is still the opponent's life total.
- Comparing these land counts with the Commander median of the other article. 26 lands is the high end here. It would be a very low Commander precon.
- Treating the deck block's product-printing price as the Scryfall sum in the table. Open the block for the printing. Use the table for the bulk price.

## FAQ

### Are Pioneer Challenger decks still for sale as a current product?

This page does not know a store's shelf. It knows the 8 lists MTGJSON publishes. The newest fact attached to each list is the Scryfall price date 2026-11-01, not a reprint announcement.

### Which of the eight is legal to register today?

Six had no Pioneer ban or not-legal flag on 2026-11-01. Izzet Phoenix and Orzhov Auras each had one flagged card, named above. Check the ban list again before you submit a deck. The bulk is a snapshot.

### Why is Gruul Stompy's ramp count not zero?

Llanowar Elves and Elvish Mystic add green, and Chandra adds mana as well as dealing damage. The ramp count is 10. Burn's ramp count is 0 because nothing in that list matches the ramp rule.

## How this was made

The lists are MTGJSON Pioneer Challenger Deck files in the committed official snapshot. Types, rules text, legality, and the lowest nonfoil price are Scryfall bulk data, fetched 2026-11-01.
