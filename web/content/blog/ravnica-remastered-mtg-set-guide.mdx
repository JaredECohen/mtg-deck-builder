Ravnica Remastered is a Magic: The Gathering reprint set released in January 2024 that gathers cards from every trip to the city-plane of Ravnica into a single draftable set. It's built around the ten two-color guilds, carries all ten shock lands at rare, and put a retro-frame card in every Draft Booster. It added no new cards; its job was to reprint, and to give players one more draft of the guilds.

| Fact | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Set code | RVR |
| Release date | January 12, 2024 |
| Plane | Ravnica, drawing on every Ravnica block from 2005 to 2019 |
| Set size | About 290 cards in the main set, plus retro-frame and other variants |
| Product | Draft Boosters (15 cards, one retro-frame card each) and Collector Boosters |
| Headline mechanics | Guild mechanics from three Ravnica eras: populate, surveil, riot, afterlife, spectacle, convoke, dredge, mentor and more |
| Formats | All reprints, so each card keeps its normal legality; the set was never Standard-legal, though some of its cards (such as the shock lands) are legal in Standard as of October 2026 through newer printings |

## What the set was for

Remastered sets do two things: they put in-demand cards back into circulation, and they offer a curated draft environment built from a plane's best moments. Ravnica Remastered pulled from the original Ravnica: City of Guilds block, the Return to Ravnica block, and the Guilds of Ravnica era that ended with War of the Spark.

The flavor is a greatest-hits tour rather than a new story. Ravnica is a city that covers an entire world, run by ten guilds, each a pair of colors with its own job: the Azorius write the laws, the Boros enforce them, the Dimir trade in secrets, the Golgari recycle the dead, the Izzet run the city's infrastructure (and its experiments), and so on. Cards from three eras of that story sit side by side, so you can draft the original Savra, Queen of the Golgari next to War of the Spark planeswalkers like Teferi, Time Raveler and Liliana, Dreadhorde General, both from the Ravnica-set climax of the Gatewatch saga. The set's legends include guild leaders like Aurelia, Exemplar of Justice, Lavinia, Azorius Renegade, Tajic, Legion's Edge and Niv-Mizzet, Parun, the dragon who founded the Izzet League.

## Mechanics

Rather than new mechanics, RVR assigns each guild its signature keywords from across Ravnica's history. A few examples:

- **Azorius (white-blue):** detain and addendum effects that tax or lock down opponents.
- **Dimir (blue-black):** surveil, which lets you look at the top cards of your library and put any into your graveyard, plus cards that care about a stocked graveyard.
- **Rakdos (black-red):** spectacle, an alternate cost you can pay if an opponent lost life this turn; Light Up the Stage is the best-known example.
- **Gruul (red-green):** riot, letting a creature enter with either haste or a +1/+1 counter. Rhythm of the Wild gives riot to all your nontoken creatures.
- **Selesnya (green-white):** convoke and populate, for tapping creatures to help pay for spells and copying your tokens.
- **Orzhov (white-black):** afterlife and drain effects, where creatures leave flying Spirits behind when they die.
- **Izzet (blue-red):** instants, sorceries and payoffs like Guttersnipe, which deals 2 damage to each opponent whenever you cast one.
- **Golgari (black-green):** dredge and scavenge, filling and mining your graveyard, with Golgari Grave-Troll as a dredge headliner.
- **Boros (red-white):** mentor and attacking bonuses that reward aggression.
- **Simic (green-blue):** adapt and +1/+1 counters, with cards that grow and evolve.

Split cards from the Guilds of Ravnica era, such as Expansion // Explosion and Find // Finality, also return. They give you two half-spells on one card; you cast one or the other. For definitions of all these keywords, see the [MTG keywords glossary](/blog/mtg-keywords-glossary).

## The reprints that mattered

The headline was the full cycle of ten shock lands: Blood Crypt, Breeding Pool, Godless Shrine, Hallowed Fountain, Overgrown Tomb, Sacred Foundry, Steam Vents, Stomping Ground, Temple Garden and Watery Grave. Each taps for two colors and can enter untapped if you pay 2 life, which makes them mana base staples in Modern, Pioneer, Legacy and Commander. If you're building a mana base, our [mana curve guide](/blog/mana-curve-explained) covers how land counts and costs fit together.

## Limited

RVR drafts like a classic Ravnica set: each of the ten two-color guilds is an archetype, with a Guildgate and a Signet for each pair to help you stretch into a third color. A few principles carried over from earlier Ravnica formats:

- **Pick a guild, but stay flexible.** Each guild has a clear plan, from Boros and Rakdos aggression to Simic and Golgari value, so reading which guild is open matters more than in a normal set. Our [guide to drafting signals](/blog/how-to-draft-mtg-signals) explains how to spot it.
- **Gold uncommons are the glue.** Cards like Mayhem Devil, Sunhome Stalwart and the Guildmages reward committing to a pair.
- **Removal is spread across colors.** Black commons like Last Gasp and Disembowel and red burn like Skewer the Critics kept creature decks honest.
- **Bombs exist at mythic.** Liliana, Dreadhorde General, Aurelia, Exemplar of Justice and Utvara Hellkite each take over a game.

Every Draft Booster also contained one retro-frame card of any rarity, so drafts had a "which old-border card did you open" bonus.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

A reprint set doesn't create new decks, so RVR's Constructed impact was about supply. Its biggest effect was making the shock lands, Teferi, Time Raveler, Karn, the Great Creator and Arclight Phoenix easier to find for Modern and Pioneer players in early 2024. For Commander, it reprinted a long list of staples at once, from Signets to Guardian Project.

A few reprints are worth knowing for their ban status as of October 2026: Deathrite Shaman remains banned in Modern and Legacy (it was banned in both years before this reprint) but is legal in Pioneer; Teferi, Time Raveler and Karn, the Great Creator are banned in Pioneer; and Karn is restricted in Vintage. None of those changes came from this set. For how the ban list works, see our [ban list explainer](/blog/mtg-ban-list-explained).

## Cards from Ravnica Remastered worth owning

- **The shock lands**: the most useful dual lands in non-rotating formats.
- **Teferi, Time Raveler**: a Modern and Legacy staple that stops instant-speed interaction.
- **Karn, the Great Creator**: a Modern artifact-hate and toolbox planeswalker.
- **Arclight Phoenix**: the namesake of Modern and Pioneer Phoenix decks.
- **Guardian Project** and **Chromatic Lantern**: Commander staples with broad use.
- **Mayhem Devil**: a sacrifice payoff for Pioneer and Commander.
- **Life from the Loam**: the engine of lands decks in Legacy and Modern.

Ravnica Remastered is the easiest single place to find the shock lands and a guild-ready collection of Commander staples.
