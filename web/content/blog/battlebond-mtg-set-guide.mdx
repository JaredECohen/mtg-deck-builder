Battlebond is a Magic: The Gathering supplemental set released in June 2018, and it was the first booster set designed to be drafted for Two-Headed Giant, the format where two-player teams share a life total. Set on the arena plane of Kylem, it's built around teammates: pairs of legends that find each other, spells your partner can help pay for, and cards that treat each player as a friend or a foe. Its cards never entered Standard, but many became Commander staples.

## The story

Kylem is a plane obsessed with competition, and its biggest event is the Valor's Reach, a tournament where fighters compete in pairs before roaring crowds. Battlebond's legends are those teams. Each pair has a gimmick that shows how they fight together: Sylvia Brightspear is a knight who rides alongside the dragon Khorvath Brightflame; Pir, Imaginative Rascal is a boy whose imaginary friend Toothy is very real on the battlefield; and Zndrsplt, Eye of Wisdom and Okaun, Eye of Chaos are a coin-flipping duo whose fortunes rise together. Regna, the Redeemer and Krav, the Unredeemed are an angel and demon bound together, and Virtus the Veiled is a deadly assassin whose bodyguard is the giant Gorm the Great.

The headliners are the Kenrith siblings, Will Kenrith and Rowan Kenrith, two young planeswalkers who arrive on Kylem and enter the tournament as a team. Their mythic planeswalker cards were designed to work with a teammate, with abilities that can target any player. Battlebond was their first appearance, and the twins later turned out to be the children of King Algenus Kenrith of Eldraine, which became their home plane's set in Throne of Eldraine.

The set's story is light and cheerful: a sporting event with a crowd, a commentator's sense of drama, and rival teams. That tone carries through card names like Play of the Game, The Crowd Goes Wild and Fan Favorite.

## Mechanics

### Partner with

Each legendary pair carries "Partner with [name]." When one enters, its controller can have a target player search for the other and put it into their hand, so in Two-Headed Giant you can hand your teammate the matching card. In Commander, partner with lets the two named legends be your commanders together. Zndrsplt and Okaun is a blue-red coin-flip pair, Pir and Toothy are a green-blue +1/+1 counters and card-draw pair, and Regna and Krav are a white-black life-gain and sacrifice pair. If you're new to the two-commander idea, our [Commander overview](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) explains it.

### Assist

Assist lets another player help pay a spell's generic mana. Huddle Up is a common where two target players each draw a card, and your teammate can cover up to `{2}` of the cost. Play of the Game, a rare that exiles all nonland permanents, lets a teammate pay up to `{6}`. In multiplayer Commander, assist turns into table politics: you can offer to help pay for a spell that hurts a shared enemy.

### Friend or foe

Friend or foe cards ask you to choose, for each player, whether they're a friend or a foe, then reward friends and punish foes. Pir's Whim lets each friend put a land onto the battlefield while each foe sacrifices an artifact or enchantment. In Two-Headed Giant that's simply your team versus theirs; in Commander, it's a deal-making tool.

### Support

Support returns from Oath of the Gatewatch: it puts a +1/+1 counter on each of up to N other target creatures, and Battlebond lets you target your teammate's creatures too. Together Forever and Generous Patron use it, and Generous Patron draws you a card whenever you put counters on a creature you don't control.

Battlebond introduced the five "two or more opponents" dual lands (Sea of Clouds, Morphic Pool, Luxury Suite, Spire Garden and Bountiful Promenade), which effectively always enter untapped in multiplayer. They've been reprinted many times since and remain among the set's most-played cards in Commander.

## Limited

Battlebond was drafted by teams of two, with each team building two decks to play Two-Headed Giant against another team. Because you were drafting alongside a teammate, the format rewarded talking about colors and plans before and during the draft.

The format rewarded things that don't matter in one-on-one Limited. Because a team shares 30 life and attacks together, support cards that can put counters on a teammate's creatures got better, and assist spells like Lava-Field Overlord, a nine-mana Dragon your partner can help pay for, let teammates pool mana. Partner with legends were natural first picks, since opening one gave your teammate a strong second card. Big rares like Archfiend of Despair and the Kenrith planeswalkers could take over a game, and removal was valuable because it could hit either opponent. For more on standard team and multiplayer formats, see our guide to [Two-Headed Giant](/blog/two-headed-giant-mtg).

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Battlebond's new cards are legal only in Commander, Legacy and Vintage (plus reprints in the formats where they were already legal), so its Constructed footprint is mostly in Commander.

- **Commander.** Najeela, the Blade-Blossom became a long-running competitive Commander deck, using its five-color ability to take extra combats with a team of Warriors. Pir and Toothy, Zndrsplt and Okaun, Regna and Krav and Sylvia and Khorvath gave casual players partner pairs with built-in themes. The reprints made Doubling Season, Land Tax, Seedborn Muse and Mycosynth Lattice easier to find. As of October 2026, Seedborn Muse is on the official Commander Game Changers list; see our [Commander brackets guide](/blog/commander-brackets-explained) for what that means for deck power.
- **Legacy.** True-Name Nemesis is the card from this set with real Legacy history; it was already a Legacy staple from its Commander 2013 printing, and Battlebond was its first printing in a booster.
- **Bans.** No card first printed in Battlebond has been banned in Commander or Legacy as of October 2026. Mycosynth Lattice is banned in Modern, but that ban dates to 2011 and its Battlebond reprint isn't Modern-legal in the first place.

## Cards from Battlebond worth owning

- **Doubling Season**: one of the most powerful tokens and counters cards in Commander.
- **Najeela, the Blade-Blossom**: a top-tier competitive Commander pick and a fun Warriors commander.
- **Seedborn Muse**: a Game Changer that turns every opponent's turn into another untap step.
- **Land Tax**: a white ramp engine for Commander and Legacy.
- **Spellseeker**: a cheap tutor on a body.
- **The five "two or more opponents" duals**: easy upgrades for any multiplayer mana base.
- **Pir, Imaginative Rascal and Toothy, Imaginary Friend**: a ready-made counters and card-draw commander pair.

Battlebond is a niche set by design, but it's one of the best value sets for Commander players. If you play multiplayer, pick up the dual lands and the partner pair that fits your style first. You can sketch a partner deck in the [Deck Lab builder](/).
