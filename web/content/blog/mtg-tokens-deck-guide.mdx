## Short answer

A token deck falls apart when the card that turns bodies into damage is answered and the tokens were never cards you can replay. In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), tokens are permanents the game created. They did not come from your hand, so a sweeper does not just kill creatures, it deletes mana you spent on cards that are now gone and bodies you cannot recast. The deck works when it can make the tokens again, or when it can protect the turn it attacks. It does not work when every token-maker costs four mana and you have one of them.

The two official Commander decks show the budget split inside one archetype. Token Triumph, Emmara, Soul of the Accord, prices at $36.09 on the printings we could price, and it goldfishes an average win on turn 10.90 with a 95.8% solitaire win rate. That is a combat clock from a lot of small bodies. Rebellion Rising, Neyali, costs $131.72. Flawless Maneuver and Clever Concealment alone are $21.13 and $11.63. The expensive cards are not the token-makers. They are the spells that keep the tokens alive for one attack. If you buy the token-makers and skip the protection, you bought the half of the deck a sweeper deletes.

Both lists play 39 and 38 lands. A token deck still needs to cast the makers on curve. The [tribal guide](/blog/mtg-tribal-decks-guide) covers overlapping creature types. Tokens are not a tribe. They are a way of getting more bodies than you drew, and they die all at once.

## When to choose this

| Situation | Lean in | Walk away |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You expect a sweeper most games | Neyali spends real money on protection spells | A token deck with no way to save the turn |
| You want the cheap version of the plan | Emmara's priced total is $36.09 | Neyali at $131.72, driven by two instants |
| You want one giant creature | Tokens win by having many bodies | Voltron, which bets on one creature |
| The maker costs more than the attack step is worth | Stop adding makers and add a way to use the ones you have | An anthem or a protection spell, which is what the expensive Neyali cards are |

### Token Triumph

Token Triumph, commanded by Emmara, Soul of the Accord is the 2022-12-02 Commander Deck stored as MTGJSON file TokenTriumph_SCD. Wizards source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/starter-commander-decks-decklists-2022-10-20.

Emmara makes a body when she becomes tapped, so the turn you tap her is the turn you get the token, and a sweeper after that deletes the body without giving you a card back. Make the tokens again, or do not attack into the sweeper. This is the cheap version of the plan: the priced total is the whole precon we could price, and the commander itself had no nonfoil retail on that printing. The combat clock is a lot of small bodies, not one protected attack.

Token Triumph's nonland curve is 4 at 1, 12 at 2, 18 at 3, 9 at 4, 9 at 5, 5 at 6, 3 at 7+, and it plays 39 lands in the 99.

Token Triumph has 29 white, 32 green mana sources and 39 land cards.

On the 2026-11-01 AllPricesToday nonfoil printings, Token Triumph is $36.09 for priced cards only, 1 unpriced card (Emmara, Soul of the Accord). The three most expensive nonbasic names are 1 Champion of Lambholt ($3.97), 1 Idol of Oblivion ($2.76), 1 Eternal Witness ($1.71), 23.4% of that priced total.

Token Triumph won 95.8% of 120 goldfish games, average winning turn 10.9 (median 11, seed 1729, midrange policy, opponent at 40 life).

Every card in Token Triumph is legal in commander today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01.

### Rebellion Rising

Rebellion Rising, commanded by Neyali, Suns' Vanguard is the 2023-02-10 Commander Deck stored as MTGJSON file RebellionRising_ONC. Wizards source: https://mtg.wiki/wiki/Phyrexia:_All_Will_Be_One/Commander_decks.

Neyali's expensive cards are the protection spells, not the token makers. Flawless Maneuver and Clever Concealment are there so the attack step survives. If you buy the makers and skip those two instants, you bought the half of the deck one sweeper deletes. Cast the protection on the turn you attack, not on the turn you are afraid. A token deck with no way to save the turn is a different, worse deck.

Rebellion Rising's nonland curve is 4 at 1, 16 at 2, 16 at 3, 12 at 4, 8 at 5, 2 at 6, 3 at 7+, and it plays 38 lands in the 99.

Rebellion Rising has 25 white, 25 red mana sources and 38 land cards.

On the 2026-11-01 AllPricesToday nonfoil printings, Rebellion Rising is $131.72 for main deck, sideboard, and commander. The three most expensive nonbasic names are 1 Flawless Maneuver ($21.13), 1 Clever Concealment ($11.63), 1 Roar of Resistance ($7.18), 30.3% of that priced total.

Rebellion Rising won 95.8% of 120 goldfish games, average winning turn 10.17 (median 9, seed 1729, midrange policy, opponent at 40 life).

## Common mistakes

- Building tokens with no anthem and no protection, then losing to one sweeper and calling the archetype weak. Neyali's receipt is the protection.
- Counting tokens as card advantage without a way to make more. The first wave dying is a loss of mana, not a loss of cards, and you still need the next maker.
- Cutting lands because the tokens are free. The makers are not free. These products play 38 and 39 lands.
- Comparing the two precons as the same purchase. One is a $36 combat deck. The other is a $132 deck whose top costs are instants.
- Reading the goldfish as the token plan's resilience. The sim never casts a sweeper. The 96% win rate is a solitaire attack step.

## FAQ

### Are tokens card advantage?

Only if you spent less card-for-card than the opponent spends answering them, or if one card keeps making tokens. A single 1/1 that dies to a shock was not advantage.

### Why is the Neyali deck so much more expensive?

Flawless Maneuver is $21.13 and Clever Concealment is $11.63 on the product printings, inside a $131.72 total. Emmara's whole priced list is $36.09. The archetype is not the price. The specific cards are.

### How fast do these decks kill a goldfish?

Turn 10.90 for Emmara and turn 10.17 for Neyali, over 120 games against one opponent at 40 life, seed 1729. That is combat damage, not an infinite token combo.

### Do I need a sacrifice outlet?

Not for these two lists to function as combat decks. A sacrifice outlet is a different plan: you want the tokens to die. That is the aristocrats page, not this one.
