Magic: The Gathering | Marvel's Spider-Man is a Universes Beyond expansion released in September 2025 that brings Peter Parker, Miles Morales, the wider Spider-Verse and Spidey's rogues' gallery into a compact, Standard-legal set. It's built around two new mechanics, web-slinging for the heroes and mayhem for the villains, and it was designed with a new four-player Pick-Two Draft in mind. Its biggest Constructed contributions turned out to be a flexible dual land and a few graveyard tricks rather than the headline heroes.

## The story

Spider-Man doesn't tell one new story. Like most Universes Beyond sets, it adapts existing source material, here decades of Marvel comics, and packs the most famous moments into individual cards.

The core cast is the Parker family and its orbit. Peter Parker // Amazing Spider-Man is a double-faced card that starts as a scientist and transforms into the hero. Aunt May, Mary Jane Watson, J. Jonah Jameson and Gwen Stacy // Ghost-Spider round out the supporting cast, and Miles Morales // Ultimate Spider-Man represents the second Spider-Man most fans now know from the films and games.

The set also leans hard into the Spider-Verse: the idea that many universes each have their own Spider-hero. That's how you get Spider-Ham, Peter Porker, Spider-Punk, Spider-Man 2099, Spider-Man India, Spider-UK, Spider-Woman, Stunning Savior and Cosmic Spider-Man in the same card file. The "legend rule" usually stops you from controlling two legends with the same name; the mythic Spider-Verse switches it off for your Spiders, which is a neat bit of flavor.

The villains are the other half of the set. The Sinister Six get a card of their own, Behold the Sinister Six!, which brings back six differently named creatures from your graveyard. Norman Osborn // Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Master Planner, Electro, Assaulting Battery, Kraven the Hunter, Mysterio, Master of Illusion and Sandman, Shifting Scoundrel all appear, along with the symbiotes: Eddie Brock // Venom, Lethal Protector, Carnage, Crimson Chaos and Gwenom, Remorseless.

The set's sagas retell the most famous comic arcs in three chapters each: Origin of Spider-Man, The Death of Gwen Stacy, Kraven's Last Hunt, The Clone Saga and Maximum Carnage. There's also a crossover with the wider Marvel universe in The Soul Stone, one of the Infinity Stones, which introduces the harness mechanic. Since the set samples many stories, there's no single ending; it's a celebration of the character's whole history.

## Mechanics

### Web-slinging

Web-slinging is an alternative cost. You can cast the card for its web-slinging cost if you also return a tapped creature you control to its owner's hand. Usually that's a creature that just attacked. Spider-Sense is a two-mana counterspell for instants, sorceries or triggered abilities that you can cast for `{U}` by returning a tapped creature. Silk, Web Weaver normally costs four but can come down for `{1}{G}{W}` the same way.

It plays like a gentler ninjutsu: attack, then swap a tapped creature for a better one at a discount. The best targets to return are creatures with good enter-the-battlefield abilities, because you get to use them again.

### Mayhem

Mayhem lets you cast a card from your graveyard for its mayhem cost if you discarded it this turn, following normal timing rules. Unlike madness, you don't have to cast it the instant it's discarded; you have until the end of the turn. Electro's Bolt deals 4 damage to a creature for `{2}{R}`, or for `{1}{R}` from the graveyard after you discard it. Swarm, Being of Bees is a flash flier you can recast for `{B}` when you discard it.

Mayhem rewards discard outlets, such as Ultimate Green Goblin, which discards a card each upkeep and makes a Treasure. It's the black-red villain mechanic.

### Connive (returning)

Connive returns from Streets of New Capenna: draw a card, then discard a card, and if you discarded a nonland card, put a +1/+1 counter on the creature that connived. Norman Osborn is an unblockable 1/1 that connives whenever he hits a player, and the discard side pairs neatly with mayhem.

### Spiders, Villains and harness

Plenty of cards care about creature types. Mary Jane Watson draws a card when a Spider enters, once per turn, and Doctor Octopus, Master Planner pumps your other Villains. Harness appears on The Soul Stone: pay a big cost and exile a creature, and its infinity ability turns on, returning a creature card from your graveyard every upkeep.

## Limited

Spider-Man is smaller than a usual premier set, with roughly 60 fewer cards, and Wizards built it around Pick-Two Draft: a four-player format in which you take two cards at a time instead of one. Sealed and other draft styles are still possible, but the reduced card count means only five supported archetypes rather than ten. Draftsim's set guide describes them like this:

- **Green-white web-slinging:** the Spider heroes, with signposts Spider-Man India and Spider-Girl, Legacy Hero. Considered one of the two best decks.
- **Blue-black Villains and connive:** Morbius the Living Vampire is the signpost and was widely rated the set's best signpost uncommon.
- **White-blue modified creatures:** counters, Auras and Equipment, paid off by SP//dr, Piloted by Peni.
- **Black-red mayhem:** discard outlets and mayhem cards. It underperformed.
- **Red-green big spells:** ramp into spells with mana value 4 or more. Also seen as a weaker lane unless it was clearly open.

Removal was the strongest part of the common slot: Web Up and Sudden Strike in white, Scorpion's Sting and Venom's Hunger in black, and Electro's Bolt in red. The hybrid commons, especially Spider Manifestation and Gallant Citizen, were seeded to help any deck in their colors and were good picks. Reviewers generally found the format slow, which made card advantage and counterspells better than usual. Pick-Two Draft itself got a lukewarm reception from players.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Spider-Man entered a Standard dominated by Izzet Cauldron, the deck built around Vivi Ornitier and Agatha's Soul Cauldron. In its first weeks, Wizards' own analysis of over 600 tournament decks (published October 2025) found that the most-played Spider-Man card by far was Multiversal Passage, which showed up in about a third of all decks, including Izzet Cauldron and Dimir Midrange. A land that can be any basic type, untapped for 2 life, is simply good in every multicolor deck.

The other early adopters were narrower. Superior Spider-Man was the main new card in Sultai graveyard decks, where copying a creature card in a graveyard turns into a reanimation effect. Jackal, Genius Geneticist appeared in Simic Ouroboroid lists, Hydro-Man, Fluid Felon in Dimir Midrange, and Aunt May in mono-white life gain.

In November 2025, Wizards banned Vivi Ornitier, Screaming Nemesis and Proft's Eidetic Memory in Standard. No Spider-Man card has been banned in Standard as of October 2026. Through 2026, Spider-Man cards kept showing up as supporting pieces: a Simic Nature's Rhythm deck that won the 1,025-player Regional Championship in Turin in March 2026 played four Spider Manifestation and two Multiversal Passage, and a September 2026 metagame report from Wizards listed Superior Spider-Man in Dimir Excruciator and Spider-Sense in Azorius Momo sideboards.

Outside Standard, Spider-Sense picked up Modern play quickly, appearing in Affinity decks and sideboards in the set's first weeks; it's a cheap answer to triggered abilities, which Modern has plenty of. Superior Spider-Man also saw early experimentation in Modern Goryo's Vengeance. In Commander, the legends are the draw, with Gwenom, Remorseless, Peter Parker and Miles Morales leading the way. For how cards like Spider-Sense earn sideboard slots, see [sideboarding 101](/blog/sideboarding-101).

## Cards from Marvel's Spider-Man worth owning

- **Multiversal Passage:** a flexible untapped dual that fits nearly any multicolor deck.
- **Spider-Sense:** a one- or two-mana answer to spells and triggers that plays in Standard and Modern sideboards.
- **Superior Spider-Man:** a reusable graveyard copy effect for reanimator and self-mill decks.
- **Spider Manifestation:** a mana creature that keeps showing up in green ramp decks.
- **Gwenom, Remorseless:** a strong Commander card that turns life into cards.
- **Agent Venom:** flash card draw for sacrifice decks in Standard and Commander.

## The takeaway

Marvel's Spider-Man is a small set with a big personality. Web-slinging rewards attacking with creatures that have good enter effects, and mayhem rewards discard. In Limited, take the removal and stick to the five supported color pairs. In Constructed, the cards that lasted were utility pieces like Multiversal Passage, Spider-Sense and Superior Spider-Man, not the flashiest heroes. Want to try a list built around them?
