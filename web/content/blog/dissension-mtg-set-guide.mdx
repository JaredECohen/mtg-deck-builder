Dissension is the Magic: The Gathering expansion released in May 2006 that completed the original Ravnica block by adding its last three guilds: the Azorius Senate, the Cult of Rakdos and the Simic Combine. Its story is the moment the ancient Guildpact finally breaks, and its card pool gave Magic the Spell Snare, Infernal Tutor, Protean Hulk and three of the ten shock lands.

## The story

Ravnica is a world-spanning city ruled by ten guilds, bound for ten thousand years by the **Guildpact**, a magical treaty that gave each guild a role and kept them from destroying each other. The block's novels, by Cory J. Herndon, follow **Agrus Kos**, a veteran officer of the Wojek (the Boros city guard). In the first book Kos uncovers a conspiracy around the Guildpact's ten-thousandth anniversary, and by the end of the second, he has died in the line of duty. Dissension opens with Kos still on the job as a ghost.

The city is coming apart. The guilds turn on each other, and several apocalyptic threats arrive at once. Five ancient monsters called the **nephilim** rampage through the city. Rakdos cultists awaken their demon lord, **Rakdos**, from a centuries-long slumber, and he goes on a rampage of his own. The Simic, led by the visionary **Momir Vig**, unleash **Project Kraj**, a biological experiment that absorbs living tissue across the city and grows out of control. And the Boros flagship, the sky fortress Parhelion, crashes into Prahv, the Azorius headquarters.

Behind much of the chaos is **Szadek**, master of House Dimir, the secret guild most citizens didn't even believe existed. His schemes have pushed the Guildpact into a paradox it can't survive. Kos's final act is to arrest Szadek in public, exposing the Dimir to the whole city. With that, the Guildpact's spell finally collapses. Many leaders die in the chaos, including the Azorius Grand Arbiter **Augustin IV** and Momir Vig, while Rakdos and Kraj are stopped. The guilds are left to replace the magical treaty with fragile, negotiated cooperation, and that unstable peace is the city Return to Ravnica later revisits.

## Mechanics

Each of Dissension's three guilds has its own mechanic.

### Forecast (Azorius)

Forecast is an ability you use while the card is still in your hand, during your upkeep, once per turn, by revealing it and paying a cost. You get a small effect every turn without spending the card, then you can cast it later. **Pride of the Clouds** (rare) can be forecast to make a 1/1 flying Bird each upkeep, and **Steeling Stance** (common) gives a creature +1/+1. Forecast rewards slow, controlling Azorius decks.

### Hellbent (Rakdos)

Hellbent cards get better when you have no cards in hand. **Infernal Tutor** (rare) reveals a card from your hand and finds another copy of it, but if you're hellbent, it searches your library for anything. **Demonfire** (rare) can't be countered while you're hellbent, and **Cackling Flames** (common) deals 5 damage instead of 3. Rakdos decks empty their hands fast to turn these on.

### Graft (Simic)

A creature with graft N enters with N +1/+1 counters. Whenever another creature enters, you may move one of those counters onto it. That spreads your stats around the board and feeds the Simic's many cards that care about counters. **Simic Initiate** (common) is a one-mana 1/1 with graft 1, and **Plaxcaster Frogling** (uncommon) gives creatures with counters shroud.

### Split cards and hybrid

Dissension brought back split cards from Invasion. Each has two halves you can choose between, one for each guild it represents, such as **Hit // Run** and **Research // Development**. The set also continued the Ravnica block's hybrid mana. A cost like `{B/R}` can be paid with black or red, so **Rakdos Guildmage** fits in either mono-color deck. If hybrid symbols are confusing, our guide to [how to read a Magic card](/blog/how-to-read-a-magic-card) breaks them down.

### Mana: shock lands, bounce lands and Signets

Each guild got a shock land (**Hallowed Fountain**, **Blood Crypt** and **Breeding Pool**), a common bounce land like **Azorius Chancery** that returns a land to your hand but taps for two mana, and a common Signet artifact like **Simic Signet**.

## Limited

Ravnica block was drafted oldest set first, as all block drafts were until Mirrodin Besieged: Ravnica: City of Guilds, then Guildpact, then Dissension. With all ten guilds spread across three sets, drafters usually picked a two-color guild, sometimes splashing a third color through the bounce lands and Signets. Dissension's pack pushed toward its three guilds:

- **Azorius (white-blue)**: a defensive deck with fliers and tappers like **Minister of Impediments**, using forecast for value each turn.
- **Rakdos (black-red)**: an aggressive deck that empties its hand to turn on hellbent, with removal like Cackling Flames.
- **Simic (green-blue)**: graft creatures that move counters around, plus tricks like **Helium Squirter** that give creatures with counters flying.

The commons with a guild-color "sacrifice it unless" clause, such as **Court Hussar**, rewarded staying in a guild's two colors. The bounce lands and Signets at common made splashing easier than in most formats of the time.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

### Standard (2006-2007)

Dissension spent a year and a half in a Standard full of Ravnica block cards, Coldsnap, Time Spiral and the Ninth and Tenth Edition core sets. Its cards mostly slotted into existing guild-based decks rather than creating a single dominant archetype. Simic Sky Swallower was a popular big finisher for control and ramp decks, Spell Snare and Court Hussar saw play in blue decks, and the Signets and bounce lands supported many of the format's controlling strategies. No Dissension card was banned in Standard, and the set rotated out when Lorwyn arrived in October 2007.

### Modern, Legacy and Vintage

Dissension's longer legacy is in eternal formats:

- **The shock lands** are the backbone of Modern mana bases, usually paired with fetch lands.
- **Spell Snare** has been a Modern and Legacy staple in blue decks, because so many key cards in those formats cost two.
- **Utopia Sprawl** powers green ramp decks in Modern, often alongside Arbor Elf.
- **Infernal Tutor** is a key card in Legacy and Vintage storm decks, which empty their hands before casting it.
- **Protean Hulk** became infamous for its combo with Flash, which led to Flash being banned in Legacy in June 2007. Protean Hulk itself was banned in Commander from 2008 to 2017, and Flash was banned in Commander in 2020.
- **Ghost Quarter** has been played everywhere from Standard to Vintage, and combines well with land-search hate like Leonin Arbiter.

As of October 2026, the shock lands and Spell Snare remain the set's most played Constructed cards. Our [Modern format guide](/blog/mtg-modern-format-guide) explains where these fit today.

### Commander

Dissension's legends are some of the earliest two- and three-color commanders still played. **Grand Arbiter Augustin IV** leads tax-heavy Azorius decks that many tables find frustrating, **Momir Vig** rewards casting lots of creatures, and **Experiment Kraj** is a favorite for +1/+1 counter combos. The Signets are long-time mana-rock staples. If you're new to the format, our guide to [what Commander is](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) covers the basics.

## Cards from Dissension worth owning

- **Hallowed Fountain, Blood Crypt and Breeding Pool**: staples in Modern and Commander.
- **Spell Snare**: a cheap counter that's perfect against two-mana threats.
- **Infernal Tutor**: a storm staple for Legacy and Vintage.
- **Utopia Sprawl**: fast mana for green Modern decks.
- **Azorius, Rakdos and Simic Signets**: reliable Commander mana rocks.
- **Ghost Quarter**: utility land destruction for almost any deck.
- **Trygon Predator**: a solid artifact and enchantment answer in Commander.

Dissension is the end of the original Ravnica story and the moment the Guildpact broke. Its shock lands and cheap blue interaction still matter in non-rotating formats, and its legends remain fun to build around. If you want to try its cards in a modern deck, start in the [Deck Lab builder](/).
