## Short answer

In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), the official Commander precon is a full deck, not a pile of theme cards with a few lands taped on. This census starts from 182 Commander products in the MTGJSON snapshot, and eight of them are Commander Anthology reprints of an earlier list, the same cards in the same counts. The medians below use 174 unique lists. The reprints are Breed Lethality, Built From Scratch, Devour for Power, Evasive Maneuvers, Guided By Nature, Heavenly Inferno, Plunder the Graves, and Wade into Battle. Counting Heavenly Inferno twice would pretend the sample learned something new the second time the same deck was boxed.

Each of those products is a complete deck once the commanders are counted with the main list. 169 unique lists are a single commander plus a 99-card main list. 5 are partner decks, and those keep 98 cards in the main list. A token that merely shares a name with a real card is not thrown out. Dinosaur Egg in Veloci-Ramp-Tor is the creature.

Across the unique lists, the median is 38 lands, 10 ramp cards, and 10 draw cards. Ramp means a nonland that adds mana, puts a land you searched onto the battlefield, or makes a Treasure for you. Draw means a nonland that draws you cards or lets you play cards exiled from the top of your library. Cycling does not count, because that draw is reminder text. A card that only triggers when someone draws, such as Psychosis Crawler or Smothering Tithe, is not a draw spell. The [method page](/method) has the full rule, including why blink is not removal.

## Where the lists actually sit

| Count | Low | Lower quartile | Median | Upper quartile | High | Mean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lands | 34 | 37 | 38 | 39 | 44 | 38.1 |
| Ramp | 2 | 8 | 10 | 13 | 27 | 10.7 |
| Draw | 1 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 19 | 10.2 |
| Removal | 3 | 6 | 7 | 10 | 19 | 7.9 |

The land column is the tight column. 132 of the 174 lists, 75.9%, sit between 37 and 39 lands. The low end is From Cute to Brute, Hatsune Miku, and Political Puppets at 34. The high end is Tricky Terrain and Reap the Tides at 44, and World Shaper at 42. Nothing in the unique sample is built like a sixty-card aggro deck. If a new Commander list is creeping toward 34 lands, it is at the edge of what Wizards has actually shipped, not at a secret smarter number.

Ramp spreads more. Adaptive Enchantment at 2, and Arm for Battle and Eternal Bargain at 3 are the sparse end, and Animated Army at 27, From Cute to Brute at 23, and Tinker Time at 22 are the decks that are mostly mana. 105 lists, 60.3%, fall between 8 and 13. Draw runs from Heavenly Inferno at 1, Power Hungry at 3, and Arm for Battle at 4 up to Quick Draw, Peace Offering, and Jeskai Striker at 19, with 99 lists (56.9%) between 8 and 12. Heavenly Inferno is that low draw count once. The anthology copy is the reprint, not a second deck that also forgot to draw cards.

Removal, under a definition that counts damage to any target and a spell that gives a creature -N/-N, and that does not count blink, has a median of 7. The low cluster is 20 Ways to Win, Deep Clue Sea, and Prismari Performance at 3, and the high end is Draconic Destruction at 19, From Cute to Brute at 18, and Heavenly Inferno at 15. 109 lists sit in the middle half, 62.6%. The most common color identity is red, blue, and white, on 13 lists (7.5%).

Prices are the lowest English paper nonfoil Scryfall price on 2026-11-01, with a foil price used only for a commander that has no nonfoil price, and that commander is labeled. 14 unique lists needed that foil fallback. 18 lists still have some other card with no usable price, so their totals are partial. Among the 156 complete totals, the median is $75.41 and the mean is $86.51. The middle half runs from $60.87 to $95.01. Grave Danger is the cheap end at $23.86. Heads I Win, Tails You Lose is the expensive end at $266.03. Deck blocks on this page show the product printing from MTGJSON instead, which is a different receipt.

## What to copy

Start a new Commander list at the medians: about 38 lands, 10 ramp pieces, and 10 draw pieces. Move off that only when the plan demands it. A landfall deck can justify climbing toward 44 lands. A deck that wants to be Animated Army can justify 27 ramp cards. Cutting lands because the ramp spell "is a land" is how lists miss the band these products actually occupy. The floor of the sample is still 34 lands.

If the list you are pricing is among the 18 with a missing card, do not treat the total as the whole deck. The facts file names the missing cards. A foil-priced commander is in the total and labeled, which is not the same thing as a card with no price at all.

## Common mistakes

- Treating an anthology reprint as new evidence. Breed Lethality, Built From Scratch, Devour for Power, Evasive Maneuvers, Guided By Nature, Heavenly Inferno, Plunder the Graves, and Wade into Battle are already in the sample under the earlier release.
- Reading the removal median of 7 as a stack of unconditional exile spells. Damage to any target counts. Blinking your own creature does not.
- Comparing these land counts with a Pioneer Challenger deck. Those lists are a different census, on the [Challenger page](/blog/pioneer-challenger-decks).

## FAQ

### Why is Heavenly Inferno not listed twice?

The Commander Anthology printing matches the earlier Heavenly Inferno list card for card. The draw count of 1 is that list, counted once. The same rule covers all 8 anthology duplicates.

### Are the prices the sealed decks?

No. 156 unique lists had a price for every counted card on 2026-11-01, and a labeled foil commander counts as priced.

### Which list should I open first?

Open Veloci-Ramp-Tor for 14 ramp cards on 39 lands. Open Land's Wrath when you want the same ramp count at $30.35 instead of $107.34.
