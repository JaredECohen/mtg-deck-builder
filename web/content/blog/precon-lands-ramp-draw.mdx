## Short answer

In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), a Commander precon ships with a commander and a main list. This count uses the 182 Commander Deck products in the MTGJSON snapshot, with collector editions left out as duplicate lists. Tokens that MTGJSON parked in the main list are not counted as cards. Across those lists, the median land count is 38, the median ramp count is 10, and the median draw count is 11.

Read those as copies, not as a promise about power. Ramp means a nonland that adds mana, puts a land you searched onto the battlefield, lets you play an extra land, or creates a Treasure for you. Draw means a nonland that draws you cards or exiles the top of your library and lets you play those cards. A land that happens to draw stays in the land count only. One card can sit in more than one of those counts. The full rules are on the [method page](/method).

The middle of the land distribution is tight. 138 of the 182 lists, 75.8%, play between 37 and 39 lands. 169 of the lists have a 99-card non-token mainboard. The rest are short because the product file included a token or, in a few cases, a shorter published list.

## The spread

| Count | Low | Lower quartile | Median | Upper quartile | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lands | 34 | 37 | 38 | 39 | 44 |
| Ramp | 2 | 8 | 10 | 13 | 27 |
| Draw | 2 | 8.2 | 11 | 13.8 | 28 |
| Removal | 3 | 6 | 7.5 | 10 | 19 |

Means sit next to those medians: 38.1 lands, 10.6 ramp, 11.1 draw, and 7.9 removal. Removal here is a nonland that destroys or exiles a permanent, deals damage to a creature or any target, forces a sacrifice, fights, or gives creatures a minus. A counterspell is not removal. That is why a burn spell pointed at a player still adds to the removal count, and why the removal median of 7.5 is not "seven and a half kill spells" in the casual sense.

The lowest land counts are From Cute to Brute and Hatsune Miku at 34, then Political Puppets at 34. The high end is Tricky Terrain and Reap the Tides at 44, then World Shaper at 42. Nothing in this set is a sixty-card aggro mana base. The floor of the whole sample is still 34 lands.

Ramp is wider. Adaptive Enchantment is the low at 2. Animated Army is the high at 27, with From Cute to Brute at 23. 109 lists, 59.9%, fall between 8 and 13 ramp copies. Draw runs from 2 on the two Heavenly Inferno products up to 28 on Timeless Wisdom. Arm for Battle is the next low, at 4. 90 lists sit between 8.2 and 13.8 draw copies, 49.5% of the sample.

The most common color identity is red-blue-white, on 13 lists (7.1%). Identity here is the commander colors MTGJSON stored, not a guess from the mana symbols in the 99.

Prices are a different column. They are the lowest Scryfall nonfoil dollar price for each English printing in the default-cards bulk dated 2026-11-01, summed for the main list, the sideboard, and the commanders. They are not the sealed product's exact printing, and a card with no nonfoil price is left out and named. 160 lists priced completely. 22 did not, so a total for those is a partial receipt. Among the complete ones, the median is $74.22, the mean is $85.23, and the middle half runs from $60.53 to $94.65 (80 lists, 50.0%). The cheapest complete list is Grave Danger at $23.84. The dearest complete list is Heads I Win, Tails You Lose at $265.03.

## What to copy

Copy the median when you do not yet have a reason not to. A new Commander list that plays about 38 lands, about 10 ramp pieces, and about 11 draw pieces is inside the band these products actually shipped. Moving toward 34 lands is the edge of this sample, not a default. Moving toward 44 is the other edge, and it belongs to lists whose plan is the lands.

Do not copy a price from a single famous card and call it the deck. The complete-list median of $74.22 and the complete-list high of $265.03 are both real, and they are not the same shopping trip. If your list is one of the 22 with a missing price, the total on this page is short by those cards. The facts file names them.

## Common mistakes

- Cutting lands because a ramp spell "counts as a land." Ramp is a separate count. The land median is 38 with the ramp median of 10 already included as spells.
- Treating the cheapest complete precon, Grave Danger at $23.84, as the cost of the median list. The median complete receipt is $74.22.
- Reading the removal median of 7.5 as a stack of unconditional kill spells. Damage to any target counts. A fight spell counts. A counterspell does not.
- Comparing a precon land count with a sixty-card Pioneer list. The Pioneer Challenger products are a different census, written up on the [Challenger deck page](/blog/pioneer-challenger-decks).
- Quoting a product-printing total from the deck block as if it were this Scryfall sum. They are different price files, on purpose.

## FAQ

### Are these the sealed-deck prices?

No. The sums are the lowest nonfoil Scryfall price for each card on 2026-11-01, times the quantity, skipping cards with no nonfoil price. 160 Commander lists had a price for every counted card. The deck block on this page is the other file: MTGJSON's price for the printing in the product.

### Why is the removal median not a whole number?

The median of an even count of lists can fall between two lists. For removal that midpoint is 7.5. The land median lands on 38, a whole number, because the two central lists agree.

### Which list should I open first?

Open Veloci-Ramp-Tor if you want a list above the ramp median, at 14 ramp and 39 lands. Open Land's Wrath if you want a landfall list at 37 lands whose Scryfall sum is $30.35. Then change one number at a time and write down which count you moved.
