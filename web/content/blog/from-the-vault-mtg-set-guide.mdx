From the Vault was a Magic: The Gathering series of premium collector boxes released every year from 2008 to 2017, each holding 15 or so all-foil reprints around a single theme. The ten sets ran from From the Vault: Dragons to From the Vault: Transform and reprinted some of the most famous cards in the game, including Jace, the Mind Sculptor, Sol Ring, Sensei's Divining Top and Avacyn, Angel of Hope. This guide covers all ten in one place.

## What the series was for

From the Vault was Wizards' first attempt at an annual premium product for collectors. Each box picked a theme, gathered famous cards that fit it and printed them in foil, usually with a new frame or new art. The boxes were sold in limited runs, so they became collectibles on release.

The series had two sides. Some boxes were full of cards tournament players needed, which made them a way to get hard-to-find staples. Others leaned on nostalgia and flavor. The foil quality of the era was a frequent complaint (some cards curled badly), and after Transform in 2017 Wizards ended the line and replaced it with Signature Spellbook, a smaller nonfoil collection. Today the spirit of From the Vault lives on in Secret Lair drops and premium Commander products.

## The early boxes (2008-2010)

### From the Vault: Dragons (2008)

The first box collected 15 Dragons from across Magic's history, from Shivan Dragon and the 16-mana Draco to Kokusho, the Evening Star, Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind, Bladewing the Risen and the original Nicol Bolas. It also included Dragonstorm and Form of the Dragon. Its most interesting card was **Hellkite Overlord**, which appeared in the box weeks before Shards of Alara, the set it actually belonged to, as a preview card.

### From the Vault: Exiled (2009)

Exiled collected cards that had been banned or restricted somewhere. It's still one of the strongest lists in the series: **Sensei's Divining Top**, **Skullclamp**, **Lotus Petal**, **Necropotence**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Strip Mine**, **Gifts Ungiven**, **Tinker**, **Balance**, **Channel**, **Berserk**, **Trinisphere**, **Goblin Lackey**, **Kird Ape** and **Serendib Efreet**. Many of these remain on ban or restricted lists as of October 2026, which only adds to the joke.

### From the Vault: Relics (2010)

Relics was all artifacts: **Sol Ring**, **Mox Diamond**, **Aether Vial**, **Isochron Scepter**, **Nevinyrral's Disk**, **Memory Jar**, **Masticore**, **Karn, Silver Golem**, **Sundering Titan**, **Zuran Orb**, **Black Vise**, **Ivory Tower**, **Jester's Cap** and **Mirari**. Like Dragons, it carried a preview: **Sword of Body and Mind** debuted here before Scars of Mirrodin.

## The middle boxes (2011-2013)

### From the Vault: Legends (2011)

Legends gathered 15 legendary creatures, many of them early Commander favorites: **Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker**, **Ulamog, the Infinite Gyre**, **Teferi, Mage of Zhalfir**, **Oona, Queen of the Fae**, **Doran, the Siege Tower**, **Progenitus**, **Sharuum the Hegemon**, **Kresh the Bloodbraided**, **Rafiq of the Many**, **Captain Sisay**, **Omnath, Locus of Mana**, **Visara the Dreadful**, **Sun Quan, Lord of Wu** and **Cao Cao, Lord of Wei**. Its preview was **Mikaeus, the Lunarch**, which appeared here just before Innistrad.

### From the Vault: Realms (2012)

Realms was all lands, and most of them are still heavily played: **Ancient Tomb**, **Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**, **Maze of Ith**, **Vesuva**, **Dryad Arbor**, **Forbidden Orchard**, **Windbrisk Heights**, **Cephalid Coliseum**, **Glacial Chasm**, **High Market**, **Shivan Gorge**, **Murmuring Bosk**, **Boseiju, Who Shelters All**, **Grove of the Burnwillows** and **Desert**.

### From the Vault: Twenty (2013)

Twenty marked Magic's 20th anniversary with 20 cards, one representing each year of the game's history. It mixed old and new: **Dark Ritual**, **Swords to Plowshares**, **Hymn to Tourach**, **Fyndhorn Elves**, **Impulse**, **Wall of Blossoms**, **Thran Dynamo**, **Tangle Wire**, **Fact or Fiction**, **Chainer's Edict**, **Akroma's Vengeance**, **Gilded Lotus**, **Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni**, **Char**, **Venser, Shaper Savant**, **Chameleon Colossus**, **Cruel Ultimatum**, **Green Sun's Zenith**, **Kessig Wolf Run** and the chase card, **Jace, the Mind Sculptor**.

## The late boxes (2014-2017)

### From the Vault: Annihilation (2014)

Annihilation was mass destruction: **Wrath of God**, **Living Death**, **Upheaval**, **Armageddon**, **Cataclysm**, **Martial Coup**, **Terminus**, **Smokestack**, **Rolling Earthquake**, **Firespout**, **Fracturing Gust**, **Decree of Annihilation**, **Virtue's Ruin**, **Burning of Xinye** and **Child of Alara**.

### From the Vault: Angels (2015)

Angels collected 15 of them: **Avacyn, Angel of Hope**, **Iona, Shield of Emeria**, **Baneslayer Angel**, **Aurelia, the Warleader**, **Platinum Angel**, **Akroma, Angel of Wrath**, **Akroma, Angel of Fury**, **Exalted Angel**, **Serra Angel**, **Archangel of Strife**, **Lightning Angel**, **Iridescent Angel**, **Jenara, Asura of War**, **Tariel, Reckoner of Souls** and the X spell **Entreat the Angels**.

### From the Vault: Lore (2016)

Lore picked cards tied to important moments in Magic's story: **Umezawa's Jitte**, **Dark Depths** (with a Marit Lage token), **Cabal Ritual**, **Beseech the Queen**, **Mind's Desire**, **Tolaria West**, **Memnarch**, **Glissa, the Traitor**, **Momir Vig, Simic Visionary**, **Conflux**, **Obliterate**, **Phyrexian Processor**, **Near-Death Experience**, **Unmask** and **Helvault**.

### From the Vault: Transform (2017)

The last box was all transforming cards, mostly from the Innistrad sets and Magic Origins: **Archangel Avacyn**, **Delver of Secrets**, **Huntmaster of the Fells**, **Bloodline Keeper**, **Garruk Relentless**, **Arlinn Kord**, the five Origins planeswalkers (**Jace, Vryn's Prodigy**, **Liliana, Heretical Healer**, **Nissa, Vastwood Seer**, **Chandra, Fire of Kaladesh** and **Kytheon, Hero of Akros**), **Elbrus, the Binding Blade**, **Arguel's Blood Fast** and the meld pair **Bruna, the Fading Light** and **Gisela, the Broken Blade**, which combine into Brisela, Voice of Nightmares. For how these cards work, see our [double-faced cards guide](/blog/double-faced-cards-explained).

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

From the Vault never introduced new decks, but because it reprinted so many tournament staples in foil, it fed every eternal format.

- **Legacy:** Jace, the Mind Sculptor, Swords to Plowshares, Mox Diamond, Ancient Tomb, Maze of Ith, Dark Depths, Umezawa's Jitte and Delver of Secrets are or were core Legacy cards. Realms and Lore in particular overlap with the Legacy Lands deck, which uses Maze of Ith, Glacial Chasm and Dark Depths. As of October 2026, Sol Ring, Strip Mine, Necropotence, Mystical Tutor, Skullclamp, Sensei's Divining Top, Memory Jar, Tinker, Balance and Channel are banned in Legacy. Our [Legacy guide](/blog/mtg-legacy-format-guide) explains the format.
- **Modern:** Aether Vial, Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker, Umezawa's Jitte, Huntmaster of the Fells and the Origins planeswalkers are Modern-legal, while Skullclamp, Sensei's Divining Top and Dark Depths are banned there.
- **Vintage:** many Exiled and Relics cards (Sol Ring, Lotus Petal, Mystical Tutor, Necropotence, Strip Mine, Tinker, Balance, Channel, Memory Jar) are restricted rather than banned, so they're still played as one-ofs.
- **Commander:** the series is packed with Commander staples. As of October 2026, Mystical Tutor, Necropotence, Gifts Ungiven, Mox Diamond, Ancient Tomb and Glacial Chasm are on the official Game Changers list, while Tinker, Balance, Channel, Sundering Titan, Iona, Shield of Emeria and Upheaval are banned in Commander. See our [Commander primer](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) for how the format's bans and brackets work.

## Cards from From the Vault worth owning

- **Jace, the Mind Sculptor**: the series' most famous reprint.
- **Sol Ring**: in almost every Commander deck.
- **Ancient Tomb** and **Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth**: lands for Legacy and Commander.
- **Mox Diamond**: fast mana for Legacy and Commander.
- **Umezawa's Jitte**: still one of the best Equipment ever printed.
- **Avacyn, Angel of Hope**: a Commander finisher.
- **Maze of Ith**: Legacy Lands and Commander defense.
- **Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker**: a Modern and Commander combo piece.

From the Vault is a time capsule of Magic's greatest hits from the late 2000s and 2010s, and its best boxes (Exiled, Relics, Realms and Twenty) are as playable as they are collectible.
