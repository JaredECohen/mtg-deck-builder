Modern Masters is the Magic: The Gathering reprint set released in June 2013 that collected powerful cards from Eighth Edition through Alara Reborn into one high-power draft set. It was the first paper Masters set, built to put Modern staples like Tarmogoyf, Dark Confidant and Vendilion Clique back into circulation without touching Standard, and it started a reprint line that continues today.

## What the set was for

Modern became an official format in 2011. Its card pool started with Eighth Edition and Mirrodin, and many of its key cards came from sets that had been out of print for years. By 2013, cards like Tarmogoyf and Dark Confidant had become hard to find and a real barrier to entry, and Wizards could not reprint them in Standard sets without warping Standard.

Modern Masters was the solution. It reprinted cards only from the Modern era, never touched Standard, and was designed as a draft set so that opening packs was fun on its own. Wizards described the plan to answer the familiar question of when Tarmogoyf would be reprinted, and they printed it in a limited run, so it eased but did not end the supply problem. The set has no story of its own; each card keeps the flavor of the plane it came from.

The format was a hit. It led to Modern Masters 2015, Modern Masters 2017 and the broader family of Masters and Remastered sets that followed. ## The reprints that mattered

### The mythics

The 15 mythics were the headline: Tarmogoyf, Dark Confidant, Vendilion Clique, Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker, Elspeth, Knight-Errant, Sarkhan Vol, Progenitus, Vedalken Shackles, Sword of Fire and Ice, Sword of Light and Shadow, and the five Kamigawa Dragon Spirits (Kokusho, the Evening Star, Keiga, the Tide Star, Ryusei, the Falling Star, Yosei, the Morning Star and Jugan, the Rising Star).

### Modern staples at rare and uncommon

Rares included Cryptic Command, Blood Moon, Aether Vial, Chalice of the Void, Arcbound Ravager, Glen Elendra Archmage, Knight of the Reliquary, Maelstrom Pulse, Engineered Explosives, Pact of Negation, Summoner's Pact, Life from the Loam, Academy Ruins, Gifts Ungiven, Blinkmoth Nexus, Glimmervoid and City of Brass. Uncommons brought Path to Exile, Lightning Helix, Electrolyze, Kitchen Finks, Murderous Redcap, Manamorphose and Mulldrifter.

### Commander favorites

Doubling Season, Kiki-Jiki, the Kamigawa Dragons, Kokusho in particular, Progenitus, Reveillark, Woodfall Primus, Adarkar Valkyrie, Meloku the Clouded Mirror and Grand Arbiter Augustin IV all made the set a Commander goldmine too.

## Mechanics

Modern Masters added no new mechanics. Its returning keywords come from the blocks it drew on:

- **Affinity for artifacts and modular** (Mirrodin): Myr Enforcer costs `{1}` less for each artifact you control, and Arcbound Ravager moves its counters when it dies.
- **Splice onto Arcane** (Kamigawa): reveal a card like Glacial Ray while casting an Arcane spell to add its effect for an extra cost.
- **Suspend and storm** (Time Spiral): exile cards to cast later for free, or copy Grapeshot for every spell cast before it.
- **Evoke, persist, changeling and Kindred** (Lorwyn and Shadowmoor): Mulldrifter can be evoked for its card draw, Kitchen Finks comes back after dying, and Kindred spells like Crush Underfoot carry a creature type, so they count for tribal effects.
- **Sunburst and domain** (Fifth Dawn and Alara): Skyreach Manta grows with each color spent on it.
- **Retrace** (Shadowmoor's Eventide): Worm Harvest and Cenn's Enlistment can be recast from the graveyard by discarding a land.

Each of these mechanics shaped one of the draft archetypes below.

## Limited

Modern Masters was designed around ten two-color draft archetypes, most of them built on old tribal and mechanical themes:

- **White-blue, artifacts and affinity:** Esperzoa, Sanctum Gargoyle and Thirst for Knowledge feeding Myr Enforcer and Frogmite.
- **Blue-black, Faeries:** flash fliers like Spellstutter Sprite, Pestermite and Thieving Sprite.
- **Black-red, Goblins:** Murderous Redcap, Marsh Flitter and other Goblin token makers.
- **Red-green, suspend and storm:** Rift Elemental and Manamorphose with Grapeshot and Empty the Warrens.
- **Green-white, Saprolings:** Thallid Shell-Dweller, Pallid Mycoderm and Sporesower Thallid making tokens over time.
- **White-black, Rebels:** Amrou Scout and Blightspeaker searching up other Rebels.
- **Blue-red, splice onto Arcane:** Glacial Ray, Dampen Thought and Desperate Ritual.
- **Black-green, graveyard:** Worm Harvest and other self-mill payoffs.
- **Red-white, Giants:** Stinkdrinker Daredevil, Thundercloud Shaman, Crush Underfoot and Feudkiller's Verdict.
- **Green-blue, ramp and sunburst:** Kodama's Reach into Skyreach Manta and other five-color threats.

Several archetypes are "linear," meaning their key cards are weak outside the deck. Esperzoa and Myr Enforcer, for example, are excellent with plenty of artifacts and poor without them, so drafters had to read signals and commit. The format was faster than most 2013 Standard-set drafts, because Affinity, Goblins and Giants could all curve out aggressively, but the Faerie and ramp decks could still win long games with card advantage. The commons were unusually strong for the time, and the foil in every pack added another card to evaluate. ## The meta: how it played in Constructed

As a reprint set, Modern Masters didn't create new decks. Its effect was on access: Affinity (Arcbound Ravager, Myr Enforcer, Blinkmoth Nexus, Glimmervoid), Faeries (Spellstutter Sprite, Vendilion Clique) and Blood Moon decks all became easier to build, and Tarmogoyf and Dark Confidant became somewhat easier to find for midrange players.

The set also contained a complete Modern combo. Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker copies Pestermite (a common here), the copy enters and untaps Kiki-Jiki, and the loop repeats for as many hasty tokens as you want. That combo was a fixture of red Modern decks for years, and having both halves in one draft set meant lucky drafters could assemble it in Limited too. Knight of the Reliquary, Maelstrom Pulse and Kitchen Finks were likewise core pieces of Modern midrange decks at the time, and the reprints helped those decks grow.

Because it was printed in limited quantities, many of the chase cards stayed scarce, and later Masters sets reprinted several of them again. Bridge from Below, one of its rares, is banned in Modern as of October 2026. In Commander, Gifts Ungiven and Grand Arbiter Augustin IV are on the official Game Changers list as of October 2026, which places decks using them in the higher [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained).

## Cards from Modern Masters worth owning

- **Tarmogoyf:** the card the set was built around, still a Modern and Legacy playable.
- **Cryptic Command:** a defining blue instant for Modern control.
- **Path to Exile:** cheap removal that's legal in nearly every format.
- **Blood Moon:** punishes greedy mana bases in Modern.
- **Doubling Season:** one of Commander's most-played enchantments.
- **Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker:** combo piece in Modern and Commander.
- **Aether Vial and Chalice of the Void:** long-running Modern and Legacy staples.
- **Kokusho, the Evening Star:** a classic Commander dragon.

Modern Masters is the reason Masters sets exist, and its card list is a snapshot of what Modern looked like in its first years.
