Eternal Masters is a Magic: The Gathering reprint set released in June 2016, built to put Legacy, Vintage and Commander staples back into circulation. It was the first Masters set aimed at eternal formats rather than Modern, and its booster packs carried cards like **Force of Will**, **Wasteland**, **Mana Crypt** and **Jace, the Mind Sculptor**. It was also designed as a draft set, with ten two-color archetypes built from some of the strongest commons and uncommons Magic had ever printed.

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Set code | EMA |
| Release date | June 10, 2016 (Magic Online June 17 to July 6, 2016) |
| Universe | Multiversal reprints, no new cards |
| Set size | 249 cards: 101 commons, 80 uncommons, 53 rares, 15 mythic rares |
| Product | 15-card draft boosters with one foil of any rarity in every pack in place of a basic land; boosters only, no preconstructed decks |
| Headline themes | Legacy staples, Commander favorites, ten two-color draft archetypes; no Reserved List cards |
| Formats | Every card keeps its normal legality: all are legal in Legacy, Vintage and Commander unless banned there, and some are also legal in Modern or Pauper. None was ever Standard legal through this set |

## What the set was for

By 2016 Legacy had a supply problem. Its key cards had been printed in small numbers in the 1990s and 2000s, and many had not been reprinted in paper boosters since. The Reserved List keeps some cards, like the original dual lands, from ever being reprinted, but plenty of staples had no such protection. Eternal Masters was Wizards' attempt to help: a premium booster set packed with the cards Legacy and Commander players needed, following the model of the Magic Online-only Vintage Masters.

The set has no story or plane. It collects cards from across Magic's history, some with new art, and it changed the rarity of several cards to make the draft work. **Brainstorm**, **Daze**, **Hymn to Tourach** and **Chain Lightning** moved up to uncommon, while **Sinkhole** jumped from common to rare. Its packs were sold only through game stores and printed in a limited run, so the set eased the supply problem without solving it.

The set also had a short life on Magic Online, where it was available to draft for about three weeks in June and July 2016. That digital run let players try the format without hunting for paper boosters, and it added copies of the eternal staples to the online economy as well. Because Eternal Masters left out every Reserved List card, the biggest Legacy gaps, such as the original dual lands, stayed exactly where they were. Wizards' answer to those cards has always been the same: they won't be reprinted, so the set focused on everything else.

## The reprints that mattered

### Legacy's core

- **Force of Will** (mythic): the free counterspell that defines Legacy. You can pay 1 life and exile a blue card from your hand instead of paying its cost, which keeps fast combo decks honest.
- **Wasteland** (rare): a land that sacrifices itself to destroy a nonbasic land, the main check on greedy mana bases.
- **Daze** (uncommon): a free soft counter that returns an Island to your hand.
- **Jace, the Mind Sculptor** (mythic): the planeswalker whose Brainstorm ability is the strongest card-selection engine in the format.
- **Sneak Attack** (mythic): put a huge creature into play for `{R}`, the engine of Sneak and Show.
- **Deathrite Shaman** (rare): a one-drop that ramped off fetch lands and drained opponents, a pillar of fair Legacy decks at the time.
- **Baleful Strix** and **Shardless Agent** (rares): value cards that powered the multicolor midrange decks of the era.
- **Hymn to Tourach** (uncommon): two random discards for `{B}{B}`.

### Commander's wish list

- **Mana Crypt** (mythic): zero mana for two colorless, with a coin flip that can deal you 3 damage. Before Eternal Masters it existed only as a book promo.
- **Natural Order**, **Vampiric Tutor**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Enlightened Tutor** and **Gamble**: tutors at every rarity from rare to mythic.
- **Necropotence** (mythic): pay life to take cards, one of the strongest black card-advantage engines ever printed.
- **Sylvan Library**, **Sensei's Divining Top** and **Argothian Enchantress**: card-selection and enchantment staples.
- **Maze of Ith** and **Karakas**: utility lands that shut down attackers and legendary creatures.
- **Toxic Deluge**, **Balance**, **Wrath of God** and **Swords to Plowshares**: removal for every budget.

## Limited

Eternal Masters drafted like a greatest-hits cube with rarity limits. The commons and uncommons were unusually strong: **Counterspell**, **Swords to Plowshares**, **Brainstorm**, **Faithless Looting**, **Night's Whisper**, **Rancor** and **Bloodbraid Elf** were all in the pack. Wizards built ten two-color archetypes:

| Colors | Archetype | Signpost-style example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White-blue | Fliers | **Thunderclap Wyvern** |
| Blue-black | Reanimator | **Extract from Darkness** |
| Black-red | Sacrifice and morbid | **Torrent of Souls** |
| Red-green | Fast aggro | **Bloodbraid Elf** |
| Green-white | Enchantments | **Armadillo Cloak** |
| White-black | Recursion of enters-the-battlefield creatures | **Gravedigger** |
| Blue-red | Spells, flashback and graveyard spells | **Wee Dragonauts** |
| Black-green | Elves | **Shaman of the Pack** |
| Red-white | Tokens and going wide | **Flame-Kin Zealot** |
| Green-blue | Threshold | **Werebear** |

Removal and card advantage were deep, so games ran longer and more interactive than a typical retail set. Blue was especially strong because it had **Counterspell**, **Brainstorm** and **Peregrine Drake** at common or uncommon. The black-green Elves deck, with **Llanowar Elves**, **Elvish Vanguard**, **Heritage Druid** and **Imperious Perfect**, could snowball if it came together. Reanimator was the flashy deck: **Entomb** or a looting effect into **Animate Dead** on a fat creature like **Inkwell Leviathan**.

The foil in every pack was a separate random card, so a draft could hand you a second rare or a premium copy of a playable common. If you want to recreate the experience, the [cube draft guide](/blog/mtg-cube-draft-guide) shows how to build something similar from your own collection.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Eternal Masters added no new cards, so it didn't change what Legacy decks could do. What it changed was who could play them. In its first months, extra copies of **Force of Will**, **Wasteland**, **Daze** and **Deathrite Shaman** made tempo and blue midrange decks easier to build, and the supply of **Sneak Attack**, **Jace, the Mind Sculptor** and **Sensei's Divining Top** helped Sneak and Show and Miracles. The set didn't come close to meeting demand, but it set a pattern for later eternal-focused reprint sets.

Several cards in the set are banned now. Verified against Scryfall and Wizards' announcements, as of October 2026:

- **Legacy:** **Mana Crypt**, **Balance**, **Necropotence**, **Mystical Tutor** and **Vampiric Tutor** were banned before the set was printed. **Sensei's Divining Top** was banned in April 2017 after Miracles dominated the format, **Deathrite Shaman** in July 2018, and **Entomb** on November 10, 2025.
- **Vintage:** **Mana Crypt**, **Balance**, **Necropotence**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Vampiric Tutor** and **Brainstorm** are restricted.
- **Modern:** **Chrome Mox**, **Sensei's Divining Top** and **Deathrite Shaman** are banned. Most of the set was never Modern legal.
- **Commander:** **Mana Crypt** was banned in September 2024, and **Balance** and **Karakas** are also on the list.

Commander players also feel the set through the Game Changers list, which marks cards that push a deck into a higher bracket. As of October 2026 that list includes **Force of Will**, **Necropotence**, **Natural Order**, **Chrome Mox**, **Vampiric Tutor**, **Mystical Tutor**, **Enlightened Tutor**, **Gamble** and **Braids, Cabal Minion**, all of which appear in Eternal Masters. ## Cards from Eternal Masters worth owning

- **Force of Will**: still the price of entry for blue in Legacy and high-power Commander.
- **Wasteland**: a permanent Legacy staple and a strong Commander utility land.
- **Swords to Plowshares**: the best one-mana removal in every format it's legal in.
- **Sylvan Library**: card selection that green decks almost always want.
- **Toxic Deluge**: a flexible sweeper for black Commander decks.
- **Natural Order**: a game-ending tutor for big green creatures.
- **Sneak Attack**: the engine of one of Legacy's longest-lived combo decks.

## Takeaway

Eternal Masters didn't introduce anything new, but it was one of the most important reprint sets ever made, both for Legacy players and for anyone building strong Commander decks. If you own its staples, a Legacy tempo deck is the natural place to use them: cheap threats, **Brainstorm**, **Daze**, **Wasteland** and **Force of Will**.
