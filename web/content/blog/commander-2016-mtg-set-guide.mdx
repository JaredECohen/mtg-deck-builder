Commander 2016 is a set of five preconstructed Magic: The Gathering Commander decks released in November 2016, and it was the first product to give four-color decks real commanders. It also introduced partner, the mechanic that lets you run two commanders at once, and it printed Atraxa, Praetors' Voice, Breya, Etherium Shaper and the partner pairs that still define competitive Commander.

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Set code | C16 |
| Release date | November 11, 2016 |
| Setting | No single plane; legends drawn from across the multiverse |

## The decks

Before this product, Magic had no four-color legendary creatures at all, so a four-color deck had to use a five-color commander or give up a color. Commander 2016 fixed that with one four-color face commander per deck, each missing a different color, plus a set of two-color partner legends that could combine into the same four colors.

### Breed Lethality (white-blue-black-green)

Led by **Atraxa, Praetors' Voice**, a 4/4 flier with vigilance, deathtouch and lifelink that proliferates at your end step. The deck is about +1/+1 counters and proliferate, spreading counters across the board and growing them every turn. Its partners are Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker, Reyhan, Last of the Abzan and Ikra Shidiqi, the Usurper.

### Entropic Uprising (blue-black-red-green)

Led by **Yidris, Maelstrom Wielder**, a trampling Ogre that gives your spells cascade for the rest of the turn once it deals combat damage to a player. The deck plays chaos and value: cascade, wheel effects and big spells. Its partners are Thrasios, Triton Hero, Vial Smasher the Fierce and Kydele, Chosen of Kruphix.

### Invent Superiority (white-blue-black-red)

Led by **Breya, Etherium Shaper**, an artifact creature that makes two Thopters when it enters and sacrifices two artifacts at a time to deal 3 damage to a player, shrink a creature or gain 5 life. The deck is an artifact deck with Thopters, Equipment and sacrifice outlets. Its partners are Silas Renn, Seeker Adept, Bruse Tarl, Boorish Herder and Akiri, Line-Slinger.

### Open Hostility (white-black-red-green)

Led by **Saskia the Unyielding**, a vigilant, hasty Soldier that has you choose a player, then copies combat damage your creatures deal onto that player. It's the aggressive deck, built to focus one opponent at a time. Its partners are Tymna the Weaver, Ravos, Soultender and Tana, the Bloodsower.

### Stalwart Unity (red-green-white-blue)

Led by **Kynaios and Tiro of Meletis**, a pair of rulers printed as one card. At your end step you draw a card, then each player may put a land from their hand onto the battlefield, and each opponent who doesn't draws a card instead. It's a group-hug and politics deck. Its partners are Ludevic, Necro-Alchemist, Kraum, Ludevic's Opus and Sidar Kondo of Jamuraa.

## Mechanics

### Partner

Partner appears on fifteen two-color legendary creatures. If both of your commanders have partner, you can run both, and your deck's color identity is the combination of theirs. That opened up color combinations no single commander covered, and it made pairs like Thrasios and Tymna a backbone of competitive play for years.

### Undaunted

Undaunted, which debuted in this product, makes a spell cost `{1}` less for each opponent you have. It's built for multiplayer: Sublime Exhalation is a seven-mana wrath that costs four in a four-player game, and Curtains' Call kills two creatures for three mana with three opponents.

### Basic landcycling and fixing

To help four-color mana bases, a cycle of gold uncommons has basic landcycling `{2}`, such as Migratory Route and Sylvan Reclamation, so early in the game they become lands and late in the game they're real spells. Ash Barrens, a colorless land with basic landcycling `{1}`, debuted here and became a Commander staple.

## How it plays

Out of the box, the five decks are battle-ready against each other, and their power level suits casual tables. Each deck gives you three ways to play: the face commander, a partner pair, or a different partner as a lone commander. That flexibility was the product's best feature. Breed Lethality can run Atraxa on counters, or you can pair Ishai with Reyhan for a counters deck that moves its counters around.

The decks share a common weakness: four colors with a mostly basic mana base. Expect some stumbles until you add more dual lands and mana rocks. The easy upgrades are:

- **Mana:** more two-color lands, Arcane Signet and other color-fixing rocks.
- **Interaction:** cheaper removal, since many included answers cost four or more.
- **Focus:** cut off-theme cards and add more payoffs for the deck's plan, like proliferate cards in Atraxa or extra Thopter makers for Breya.

## The meta: how it played in Commander

Commander 2016's impact went far beyond casual tables. Partner became one of the most important mechanics in competitive Commander, since two cheap commanders give a deck more flexibility than one. Thrasios, Triton Hero and Tymna the Weaver in particular became core pieces of competitive four-color decks, often paired with each other or with Kraum, Ludevic's Opus. Wizards brought partner back in later Commander products and Commander Legends, building on this design.

Atraxa, Praetors' Voice became one of the most popular commanders of its era. Proliferate works with +1/+1 counters, planeswalker loyalty and poison counters all at once, so Atraxa leads superfriends, counters and infect decks. Breya, Etherium Shaper later got a Double Masters reprint, which made it legal in Modern too.

As of October 2026, none of Commander 2016's new cards are banned in Commander, and Scryfall lists none of the set's cards as Game Changers. ## Cards from Commander 2016 worth owning

- **Thrasios, Triton Hero and Tymna the Weaver:** cheap, efficient partners that still lead competitive decks.
- **Atraxa, Praetors' Voice:** a flexible four-color commander for counters or superfriends.
- **Ash Barrens:** an easy addition to any Commander mana base.
- **Selfless Squire:** a flash Fog for yourself that grows into a real threat.
- **Deepglow Skate:** a big payoff for any counters deck.
- **Kraum, Ludevic's Opus:** card draw that punishes spell-heavy opponents.
- **Chromatic Lantern and Sol Ring:** reprints that belong in many multicolor decks.
