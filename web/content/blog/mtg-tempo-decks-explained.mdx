## Short answer

A tempo deck trades raw damage for the right to decide what resolves. In Magic: The Gathering (MTG), tempo means spending your mana and your cards so that the opponent's expensive spell resolves a turn too late, or not at all, while your small creatures are already attacking. You give up the ability to win a long game of card advantage. If the game reaches the point where both players have seven lands and full hands, the tempo deck is usually the one that is behind.

Azorius Spirits, the 2021 Challenger product, is the clean version of that trade. Twenty-two of its nonlands cost two mana. The goldfish average win is turn 6.62, close to burn, on a $22.40 product total that is the cheapest list in this set of guides. The expensive cards are the creatures, not a shock land. You can see the whole plan: small fliers, a little disruption, a clock that does not need a six-drop.

Izzet Phoenix is the cautionary official list. Its goldfish win rate is only 56.7%, and the average winning game is turn 9.78, which is not a tempo clock. Expressive Iteration is not legal today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01. The product was a real Challenger deck in 2022. It is not a list you can copy into a tournament now. Tempo decks also rot when a key cantrip is banned, because the deck was the cantrip plus the threat, not the threat alone. The [aggro page](/blog/should-you-play-aggro) is the comparison when you want damage instead of disruption.

## When to choose this

| Situation | Lean in | Walk away |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small creatures plus cheap interaction | Spirits: 22 two-drops, turn 6.62, $22.40 | A control list that does not attack |
| A cantrip the format later banned | Do not register Phoenix as printed | Expressive Iteration is not Pioneer-legal on the 2026-11-01 bulk |
| You want the long game | Tempo gives that up on purpose | Control or a real midrange top end |
| The mana base is the budget | Spirits spends its money on creatures | Phoenix's top cost is Steam Vents |

### Azorius Spirits

Azorius Spirits is the 2021-10-15 Pioneer Challenger Deck stored as MTGJSON file AzoriusSpirits_Q06. Wizards source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/news/pioneer-challenger-decks-2021-08-24.

Attack with the two-drop fliers and spend the cheap interaction on the spell that would stabilize the opponent, not on the first spell they cast. If the game reaches a board of five-drops, the two-drops are no longer ahead. That is the trade this deck makes on purpose. Cutting the interaction to add more creatures makes a worse aggro deck, not a better tempo deck.

Azorius Spirits's nonland curve is 8 at 1, 22 at 2, 7 at 3, and it plays 23 lands in the main deck.

Azorius Spirits has 13 white, 16 blue mana sources and 23 land cards.

On the 2026-11-01 AllPricesToday nonfoil printings, Azorius Spirits is $22.40 for main deck, sideboard, and commander. The three most expensive nonbasic names are 4 Watcher of the Spheres ($3.08), 4 Rattlechains ($1.48), 4 Supreme Phantom ($1.40), 26.6% of that priced total.

Azorius Spirits won 96.7% of 120 goldfish games, average winning turn 6.62 (median 6.0, seed 1729, aggro policy, opponent at 20 life).

Every card in Azorius Spirits is legal in pioneer today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01.

### Izzet Phoenix

Izzet Phoenix is the 2022-11-04 Pioneer Challenger Deck stored as MTGJSON file IzzetPhoenix_Q08. Wizards source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/news/pioneer-challenger-decks-2022-decklists-2022-09-29.

Do not register this list. The cantrip the deck was built around is not legal in Pioneer on the current bulk, and the combat clock without that cantrip is not a tempo clock. Steam Vents is the expensive card. Paying for the land and then being unable to cast the banned spell is the trap. Rebuild the shell only after you have a legal way to put Phoenix in the graveyard and bring it back on the turn you wanted.

Izzet Phoenix's nonland curve is 15 at 1, 11 at 2, 3 at 3, 6 at 4, 4 at 7+, and it plays 21 lands in the main deck.

Izzet Phoenix has 17 blue, 15 red mana sources and 21 land cards.

On the 2026-11-01 AllPricesToday nonfoil printings, Izzet Phoenix is $35.25 for main deck, sideboard, and commander. The three most expensive nonbasic names are 1 Steam Vents ($10.77), 3 Narset, Parter of Veils ($3.30), 4 Mystical Dispute ($2.16), 46.0% of that priced total.

Izzet Phoenix won 56.7% of 120 goldfish games, average winning turn 9.78 (median 10.0, seed 1729, aggro policy, opponent at 20 life).

Expressive Iteration is not legal today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01.

## Common mistakes

- Calling Phoenix a current Pioneer deck. The ban is in the Scryfall bulk. The goldfish was run on the printed list anyway, bans included, so the clock is a historical measurement.
- Playing tempo into a board stall. The two-drops do not win a game of five-drops. You needed the game to end while they were still large enough.
- Cutting the interaction to add more creatures, then wondering why the deck became a worse aggro deck. The disruption is the reason the small creatures get to attack.
- Paying Phoenix prices for a banned cantrip shell. Steam Vents is the top line item. The card you cannot cast is not worth the land.
- Expecting a 100% goldfish rate. Spirits won 96.7%. Phoenix won 56.7%. The second number is a deck that does not reliably turn sideways.

## FAQ

### What does tempo mean if it is not just aggro?

Aggro spends every card on damage. Tempo spends some cards to slow the opponent's expensive plays so the small creatures stay ahead. Spirits' curve is the creature half of that. The disruption is the other half.

### Can I play the Phoenix Challenger deck in Pioneer?

Not as printed. Expressive Iteration is not legal today per the Scryfall bulk of 2026-11-01. Check the current ban list before you rebuild the shell.

### Why is Spirits so much cheaper?

The product-printing total is $22.40, and the top costs are creatures at a few dollars, not a $10 land. Phoenix is $35.25 with Steam Vents alone at $10.77.

### Is a turn-9 goldfish still tempo?

No. Turn 9.78 for Phoenix says the printed list does not present a fast combat clock once the simulator is just attacking. Spirits at 6.62 does.
