Dominaria United is the Magic: The Gathering expansion released in September 2022 that brought the game back to its oldest home plane, Dominaria, to start the Phyrexian invasion storyline. It was a multicolor, legends-heavy set built as a tribute to the Invasion block, and it gave the game one of its most dominant creatures of the decade in Sheoldred, the Apocalypse. The set has since rotated out of Standard, but its best cards are still everywhere in Pioneer, Modern and Commander.

## The story

Dominaria United opens with an enemy that is already inside the house. The Phyrexians, led on Dominaria by the praetor Sheoldred, have conquered the swampy region of Urborg and planted sleeper agents across the plane: soldiers, nobles and heroes who look like themselves but have been compleated, turned into Phyrexians in secret. Karn, the ancient golem planeswalker, has seen the threat coming and spends the story trying to get Dominaria's quarrelsome nations to stand together.

The first big scene is a peace summit between the Keldons and New Benalia, attended by the fire mage Jaya Ballard. Karn and Teferi interrupt it with news of the coming invasion, and several Benalish knights are exposed as Phyrexian agents on the spot. That shock is enough to forge a New Coalition, echoing the alliance that fought Phyrexia during the original Invasion. Benalia, Keld, the Tolarian academies, Shiv, Llanowar and Yavimaya all send forces. Along the way the story revisits a long list of legends, from Jhoira and Stenn to Radha, Danitha Capashen and the reborn Ertai, who has been compleated and now serves Phyrexia.

The climax is the Battle of the Mana Rig, a fight over a massive Phyrexian-held machine. The Coalition wins the ground, but the cost is brutal. Ajani Goldmane, the lion-headed planeswalker who had been helping Karn, reveals that he was captured and compleated off-screen. He kills Jaya, destroys the Golgothian Sylex that Karn had planned to use against the invaders, and helps the Phyrexians capture Karn. The set closes with the Phyrexians regrouping, Elesh Norn's plans coming into view and the multiverse-wide invasion still to come, a thread that runs through The Brothers' War, Phyrexia: All Will Be One and March of the Machine.

## Mechanics

### Domain

Domain is an ability word that counts the basic land types among lands you control, up to five. The more types you have, the better the card gets. **Leyline Binding** (rare) costs one less for each type, so a four- or five-type mana base turns a six-mana exile effect into a one- or two-mana instant-speed answer. **Herd Migration** (rare) makes a 3/3 Beast for each type. Dual lands with basic land types, like the set's common tapped duals, are what make domain work.

### Kicker

Kicker returns from the Invasion era as an optional extra cost paid when you cast the spell. In this set almost every kicker cost is a different color from the card itself, which pushes you toward two colors with a splash. **Archangel of Wrath** (rare) is a 3/4 flying, lifelink Angel for four mana, and each kicker you pay (one black, one red) adds 2 damage to any target when it enters.

### Enlist

Enlist lets an attacking creature tap one of your untapped, non-attacking creatures to add that creature's power to its own for the turn. The helper has to be free of summoning sickness. **Coalition Warbrute** (common) is a trampling four-drop with enlist, so it can borrow a small creature's power to push big damage through. It's a way to turn a board of mismatched creatures into one real threat.

### Read ahead

Read ahead is a new keyword for Sagas. When you cast one, you choose which chapter to start on and skip the earlier ones. **The Cruelty of Gix** (rare) lets you take a creature or planeswalker from an opponent's hand, tutor any card, or reanimate a creature from any graveyard, and read ahead lets you go straight to the chapter you need.

### Stun counters

Stun counters became an evergreen mechanic in this set. If a permanent with a stun counter would untap, you remove a counter instead, so each counter costs it one untap. **Impede Momentum** (common) taps a creature and puts three stun counters on it, which locks it down for several turns.

### Legends

Every pack has a legendary card, and the set has more than 40 legendary creatures. It's why the set is so popular in Commander: there are legends at every rarity, including two-color uncommon signposts for each draft archetype. If kicker and Saga chapters are new to you, our guide on [how to read a Magic card](/blog/how-to-read-a-magic-card) covers how those frames work.

## Limited

Dominaria United was designed as a multicolor draft format where the default deck is two colors plus a splash. Each color has a clear base theme (white goes wide, blue cares about instants and sorceries, black removes and sacrifices, red attacks and burns, green ramps and enables domain), and every pair has a legendary uncommon signpost:

- **White-blue: flying tempo.** Evasive creatures and tap effects; Raff, Weatherlight Stalwart rewards casting spells.
- **Blue-black: control.** Card advantage and removal, with Vohar, Vodalian Desecrator looting toward late-game value.
- **Black-red: reckless aggro.** Lagomos, Hand of Hatred makes a hasty attacker every turn.
- **Red-green: domain stompy.** Big creatures with enlist; Radha, Coalition Warlord pumps your attackers.
- **Green-white: domain tokens.** Zar Ojanen, Scion of Efrava grows a wide board.
- **White-black: aristocrats.** Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim drains whenever creatures come and go.
- **Blue-red: spells aggro.** Balmor, Battlemage Captain pumps your team when you cast instants and sorceries.
- **Black-green: domain midrange.** Bortuk Bonerattle brings back creatures from the graveyard.
- **Red-white: token aggro.** Baird, Argivian Recruiter makes Soldiers when your creatures are pumped.
- **Green-blue: kicker.** Nael, Avizoa Aeronaut and a pile of kicker cards that do double duty.

The format played at a moderate speed. Because the five tapped common dual lands and Shadow Prophecy made splashing easy, strong commons and uncommons with off-color kicker, like Battlewing Mystic and Tribute to Urborg, often pulled decks into a third color. Removal and two-for-ones rewarded patient players more than all-in aggro. For a refresher on reading these color pairs, see our [deck archetypes guide](/blog/mtg-deck-archetypes-explained).

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

After Wizards lengthened Standard's rotation window, Dominaria United stayed in the format for nearly three years, from September 2022 to the summer 2025 rotation. Its defining card was **Sheoldred, the Apocalypse**. A four-mana creature with deathtouch and a life swing on every draw step was simply hard to beat in a fair fight, and it became the top-end threat of black midrange decks for its entire Standard run.

At the 2022 World Championship a few months after release, Esper Midrange made up roughly 70 percent of the Standard field, and Sheoldred sat at the top of its curve alongside The Wandering Emperor. Over the following years Grixis Midrange and Rakdos Midrange carried Sheoldred, Cut Down and Ertai Resurrected, while **Domain Ramp** used Herd Migration and Leyline Binding to punish slower decks. Liliana of the Veil, back in Standard for the first time since its original printing, slotted into those same black shells. No Dominaria United card was ever banned in Standard, which is notable for a set with this much power.

The set's reach outside Standard is just as large. In Pioneer and its Arena cousin Explorer, Rakdos Midrange picked up both Sheoldred and Liliana of the Veil right away, and Azorius Control added Leyline Binding and Temporary Lockdown. In Modern, Leyline Binding became a key piece of Domain Zoo, where fetchlands and shocklands let it cost a single white mana. Sheoldred also appears in Modern black decks. As of October 2026, none of these cards are banned in Pioneer, Modern, Legacy or Vintage.

Commander is where the set's legends live on. Sheoldred, Jodah, the Unifier, Braids, Arisen Nightmare, Defiler of Vigor and Serra Paragon are all popular, and the painland reprints (Shivan Reef, Caves of Koilos and the rest of the cycle) are among the set's most-played cards in the format. If you build around Sheoldred or Braids, our [guide to Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) explains how legends become a deck's commander.

## Cards from Dominaria United worth owning

- **Sheoldred, the Apocalypse**: a Pioneer and Modern staple and a strong Commander pick for any black deck.
- **Leyline Binding**: one of Modern's best answers in domain shells, and a flexible exile spell in Pioneer.
- **Liliana of the Veil**: a reprint that made the card Pioneer-legal, where it powers black midrange.
- **Cut Down**: cheap, efficient removal that still sees Pioneer play.
- **Temporary Lockdown**: a white sweeper for low-cost permanents, good against aggro and token decks.
- **The painland cycle**: five dual lands that improve any two-color Commander or Pioneer mana base.
- **Jodah, the Unifier**: a five-color commander built around legends.

Dominaria United is easiest to understand as a set about building strong two-color decks with a splash, and about legends. Its Standard years are over, but if you play Pioneer, Sheoldred and Liliana of the Veil are still the heart of black midrange.
