Dragon's Maze is the Magic: The Gathering expansion released in May 2013, the small third set of the Return to Ravnica block. All ten Ravnican guilds send a champion to race through an ancient maze hidden in the city's streets, and Jace Beleren ends up at its center. The set brought fuse split cards and gold cards for every guild, and its best card, Voice of Resurgence, has been a Constructed staple ever since.

## The story

The Return to Ravnica block takes place after the Guildpact, the magical treaty that bound Ravnica's ten guilds together, has been destroyed. Without it, the guilds jockey for power and the city edges toward war. The dragon Niv-Mizzet, guildmaster of the Izzet League, has spent the block chasing a mystery: a pattern of mana lines running through landmarks of all ten guilds, carved into the foundations of the city when it was built. With help from the notes of Jace Beleren, a planeswalker who'd been studying the same puzzle, the Izzet mage Ral Zarek uncovers the truth. It's the Implicit Maze, a hidden path left by Azor, founder of the Azorius Senate, meant to be revealed only if the Guildpact ever failed.

The maze can only be solved if all ten guilds run it at the same time. Niv-Mizzet announces that each guild will send one champion, and whoever reaches the end, the Forum of Azor, will win the great power waiting there for their guild. The runners gather at the Transguild Promenade: Lavinia for the Azorius, Tajic for the Boros, Mirko Vosk for the Dimir, Varolz for the Golgari, Ruric Thar for the Gruul, Teysa Karlov for the Orzhov, Exava for the Rakdos, Emmara Tandris for the Selesnya, Vorel for the Simic, and for the Izzet, Ral Zarek, who takes the place of the original runner, Melek. Those runners are the set's legends, and the race is full of ambushes and betrayals as the guilds sabotage each other.

Jace enters the race as a wild card. When he reaches the Forum, the runners are already fighting, and the shapeshifting Dimir guildmaster Lazav keeps stirring them up. Azor's design included a failsafe: if the guilds arrived in conflict, the Forum's guardian would hand them the Supreme Verdict, a spell that would wipe out every guild. To keep anyone from using it, Jace links the minds of all ten runners so they can see each other's thoughts and stop fighting, and nearly tears his own mind apart doing it. The Forum's guardian, seeing a mind that can hold every guild's perspective at once, declares Jace the Living Guildpact, the mediator whose word binds the guilds. That role drives Jace's story for years, including the Ravnica crisis of War of the Spark.

The story was told in Doug Beyer's e-book *The Secretist, Part Three*.

## Mechanics

### Fuse

Fuse is the set's only new mechanic, and it appears on split cards: two half-cards printed sideways on one card. You can cast either half normally, or, if you're casting from your hand, pay both costs and cast both halves as one spell. **Turn // Burn** (uncommon) shrinks a creature to a 0/1 with Turn and deals 2 damage with Burn. Fused together, they kill almost anything. Each fuse card's halves cross two guilds, so they're flexible in a three-color deck.

### The guild mechanics

Dragon's Maze brings back every guild's keyword from the first two sets, which made it the only set in the block with all ten:

| Guild | Mechanic | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azorius (W/U) | Detain | Until your next turn, the target can't attack, block or use activated abilities |
| Dimir (U/B) | Cipher | Encode the spell on a creature to cast a copy whenever it hits a player |
| Rakdos (B/R) | Unleash | Enter with a +1/+1 counter, but then the creature can't block |
| Gruul (R/G) | Bloodrush | Discard the creature to pump an attacking creature |
| Selesnya (G/W) | Populate | Copy a creature token you control |
| Orzhov (W/B) | Extort | Pay an extra mana per spell to drain 1 |
| Izzet (U/R) | Overload | Pay more to change "target" to "each" |
| Golgari (B/G) | Scavenge | Exile from your graveyard to put +1/+1 counters on a creature |
| Boros (R/W) | Battalion | Triggers when it attacks with at least two other creatures |
| Simic (G/U) | Evolve | Gets a counter when a bigger creature enters under your control |

### Gates and Maze's End

Dragon's Maze reprinted all ten Guildgates and added **Maze's End** (mythic), a land that can return to your hand to fetch a Gate and wins the game if you control ten Gates with different names. Boosters had no basic lands; that slot held Guildgates, and shock lands from earlier sets could also appear. ## Limited

Dragon's Maze was drafted with the other two sets of the block, opening Dragon's Maze first, then Gatecrash, then Return to Ravnica. That brought all ten guilds into one draft. Return to Ravnica had covered five guilds and Gatecrash the other five, so Dragon's Maze's job was to tie them together, with gold cards for every guild and fuse cards bridging two.

With ten guilds on the table, three-color decks were common. The Guildgates in every pack made splashing much easier, and the common Cluestones (three-mana mana rocks that later cash in for a card) added fixing. Strong commons and uncommons included Turn // Burn, **Far // Away**, **Warleader's Helix** and **Putrefy**, plus efficient gold creatures like **Tithe Drinker**, an Orzhov two-drop with lifelink and extort. Our [deck archetypes guide](/blog/mtg-deck-archetypes-explained) explains how guild-style themes give a draft deck a plan.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

### Standard, 2013 to 2014

Dragon's Maze spent its first months in a Standard built from Return to Ravnica block, the Innistrad block and Magic 2013, then carried on after Theros arrived in fall 2013. **Voice of Resurgence** was the set's breakout card, an aggressive two-drop that punished opponents for casting instants on your turn, and it became a fixture of green-white creature decks. **Advent of the Wurm** gave those decks an instant-speed threat. **Blood Baron of Vizkopa** was a key creature in Orzhov and Esper midrange, where its protection from white and black made it hard for many decks to answer. **Aetherling** became a premier control finisher, especially in blue-white and Esper control during the Theros era, since it could blink out of removal and become unblockable. **Ral Zarek** and **Warleader's Helix** fed Izzet and Boros-colored spell decks.

No Dragon's Maze card was banned in Standard. The set rotated out in September 2014, when Khans of Tarkir arrived and the Return to Ravnica block left the format with Magic 2014.

### Pioneer, Modern, Legacy and Commander

Dragon's Maze is part of the very first Pioneer block, since the format starts with Return to Ravnica. As of October 2026, Voice of Resurgence has been one of the set's lasting Constructed cards. It was a pillar of Modern creature-toolbox and Collected Company decks during its prime, and it has been played in Pioneer creature decks too. Ruric Thar has shown up in Modern and Pioneer sideboards against spell-heavy decks, and Wear // Tear remains a flexible sideboard card in several formats. Maze's End has been the payoff for Pioneer Gates decks that ramp out Guildgates. In Legacy, Notion Thief has seen play as a punishing answer to decks that rely on Brainstorm-style card draw, and Aetherling has appeared in Legacy reanimator lists.

No Dragon's Maze card is banned in Pioneer, Modern or Legacy. In Commander, Notion Thief is a popular draw-punisher, the maze runners like Varolz, the Scar-Striped and Teysa, Envoy of Ghosts lead their own decks, and Maze's End anchors Gates decks. Our [Commander primer](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) explains how a single legend becomes a whole deck.

## Cards from Dragon's Maze worth owning

- **Voice of Resurgence**: a two-drop that's been played across Standard, Modern and Pioneer.
- **Notion Thief**: a Commander and Legacy answer to extra card draw.
- **Wear // Tear**: a flexible sideboard card in many formats.
- **Maze's End**: the centerpiece of any Gates deck.
- **Aetherling**: a resilient finisher for control and reanimator.
- **Putrefy**: instant-speed Golgari removal for Commander.
- **Warleader's Helix**: burn and life gain in one card.
- **Possibility Storm**: a unique engine for chaos and lock decks in Commander.

Dragon's Maze is a small set with one great card and a lot of glue. Its job was to connect the guilds, so its best cards tend to be flexible ones: fuse spells, gold creatures and Gates.
