Commander 2018 is the Magic: The Gathering preconstructed Commander product released in August 2018: four 100-card, three-color decks, each led by a planeswalker that can be your commander. It's the release that gave the format Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow, Coveted Jewel and Loyal Apprentice, and it introduced two command-zone twists on old keywords, commander ninjutsu and "commander storm."

## What the set was for

Commander's yearly product had settled into a pattern by 2018: four or five decks, a handful of new legends, a pile of useful reprints, and a mechanic or two designed only for multiplayer. Commander 2018, led by designer Ken Nagle and developer Gavin Verhey, picked "planeswalkers as commanders" as its hook, an idea first tried in Commander 2014.

Unlike most years, the decks don't share a color pattern. Each is simply built around one strategy, which made it easy to pick a deck by playstyle.

There's no single story, but the planeswalkers have backstory. Saheeli Rai is the Kaladesh inventor from the Aether Revolt era. Lord Windgrace is a panther-like planeswalker from Dominaria's distant past, long dead in the current story, shown here in his prime. Estrid is a new planeswalker whose masks protect what she cares about, and Aminatou is a young planeswalker with power over fate.

## The decks

| Deck | Colors | Commander | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exquisite Invention | Blue, red | Saheeli, the Gifted | Artifacts |
| Nature's Vengeance | Black, red, green | Lord Windgrace | Lands and landfall |
| Adaptive Enchantment | Green, white, blue | Estrid, the Masked | Enchantments and Auras |
| Subjective Reality | White, blue, black | Aminatou, the Fateshifter | Controlling the top of your library |

Exquisite Invention is the only two-color deck. Each deck has two alternate legendary creatures that can lead it instead, plus a "sub-commander" legend missing one of the deck's colors, so it can't lead that deck but can lead another.

- **Saheeli, the Gifted** makes Servos, gives your next spell affinity for artifacts, and can copy every artifact you control with her ultimate. Alternates: Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer, who turns all your tokens into copies of one, and Tawnos, Urza's Apprentice, who copies artifact abilities. Sub-commander: Varchild, Betrayer of Kjeldor.
- **Lord Windgrace** loots, brings back two lands from your graveyard, and at -11 destroys six nonland permanents. Alternates: Gyrus, Waker of Corpses and Thantis, the Warweaver. Sub-commander: Xantcha, Sleeper Agent, a 5/5 that you hand to an opponent.
- **Estrid, the Masked** untaps your enchanted permanents and makes "Mask" Aura tokens with umbra armor. Alternates: Tuvasa the Sunlit and Kestia, the Cultivator. Sub-commander: Arixmethes, Slumbering Isle, a 12/12 that starts as a land.
- **Aminatou, the Fateshifter** sets up your next draw, blinks a permanent, and with her ultimate shifts every player's nonland permanents one seat to the left or right. Alternates: Varina, Lich Queen and Yennett, Cryptic Sovereign. Sub-commander: Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow.

### New mechanics

**Planeswalker commanders.** Each face planeswalker has "can be your commander" printed on it, the same rule exception Commander 2014 introduced.

**Commander ninjutsu.** Yuriko's version of ninjutsu works from the command zone as well as your hand. Pay `{U}{B}` and return an unblocked attacker, and Yuriko comes in attacking without paying her commander tax.

**"Commander storm."** A cycle of five rares (Empyrial Storm, Echo Storm, Skull Storm, Fury Storm and Genesis Storm) copies itself once for each time you've cast your commander from the command zone this game. The more often your commander has died, the better they get. R&D never settled on a keyword name, so the cards just carry "Storm" in the title.

**Lieutenant** returned from Commander 2014 on the five "Loyal" uncommons, which get a bonus at the start of combat if you control your commander. Loyal Apprentice makes a hasty 1/1 flying Thopter every turn, and Loyal Drake draws a card.

The decks also used older mechanics on new cards: bestow (Kestia), constellation (Nylea's Colossus), evoke (Night Incarnate), improvise (Saheeli's Directive), landfall (Nesting Dragon), manifest (Primordial Mist), metalcraft (Vedalken Humiliator), miracle (Entreat the Dead), retrace (Reality Scramble) and umbra armor (Octopus Umbra).

The reprints included plenty of Commander basics: Sol Ring, Brainstorm, Swiftfoot Boots, Myriad Landscape, Blasphemous Act, Chaos Warp, Avenger of Zendikar, Putrefy and several Signets and bounce lands.

Two other cards stand out for how they play. **Sower of Discord** links two players so damage to one makes the other lose that much life, and **Aminatou's Augury** exiles eight cards and lets you cast one of each card type for free.

## How it plays

The four decks were built to be fair against each other, and out of the box they fall in the core, precon range of the [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained). None of the cards in the four decks is on the official Game Changers list as of October 2026.

- **Exquisite Invention** is a slow-building artifact deck. It can be explosive once Saheeli has a few artifacts out, but it's fragile to artifact wipes.
- **Nature's Vengeance** ramps and recurs lands, and Lord Windgrace's ultimate can reset the table. It's the best of the four at grinding long games.
- **Adaptive Enchantment** sets up a sticky board of enchanted permanents protected by umbra armor. It's the most defensive deck.
- **Subjective Reality** is the trickiest: Aminatou sets up draws, blinks permanents for value, and can swap boards around the table.

A common upgrade path, as covered in our [precon upgrade guide](/blog/how-to-upgrade-a-commander-precon), is to cut the weakest top-end cards for cheaper interaction and more ramp. A popular rebuild is pulling Yuriko out of Subjective Reality into her own blue-black Ninja deck.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Because the new cards aren't legal in Standard, Pioneer or Modern, Commander 2018's footprint is almost entirely in Commander. As of October 2026, none of its new cards is banned in Commander.

The cards that lasted:

- **Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow** became a well-known competitive Commander deck, using cheap evasive creatures and high-mana-value cards on top of the library to drain the table. See our [competitive Commander guide](/blog/cedh-competitive-commander-guide).
- **Coveted Jewel** found a home in combo decks, since untapping it repeatedly produces large amounts of mana.
- **Loyal Apprentice, Forge of Heroes and Myth Unbound** became everyday Commander cards for any deck that relies on its commander.
- **The commander storm cycle**, especially Echo Storm and Genesis Storm, rewards decks whose commanders die often.
- **Estrid and Tuvasa** became popular commanders for enchantment decks.

## Cards from Commander 2018 worth owning

- **Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow**: a commander for competitive and casual Ninja decks.
- **Loyal Apprentice**: a steady stream of fliers for any red deck.
- **Coveted Jewel**: big mana, with risks you can play around.
- **Myth Unbound**: card draw for any commander that keeps dying.
- **Forge of Heroes**: a free bonus land for most decks.
- **Retrofitter Foundry**: a mana sink that turns spare mana into creatures.
- **Treasure Nabber**: punishes opponents who rely on mana rocks.

## The takeaway

Commander 2018 is a set of four very different, well-built decks, each led by a planeswalker. If you find one secondhand, Nature's Vengeance and Adaptive Enchantment are the easiest to enjoy right away, while the Yuriko and Coveted Jewel singles are the cards most worth seeking out.
