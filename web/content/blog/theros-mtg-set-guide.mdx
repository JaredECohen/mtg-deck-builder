Theros is the Magic: The Gathering expansion released in September 2013, the first set on a plane inspired by Greek mythology, where gods walk among mortals and heroes win glory by fighting monsters. It opened the Theros block with heroic, devotion, bestow and monstrosity, and made enchantments the center of the set. It also printed the devotion engine Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx, a reprint of Thoughtseize, and the Temple scry lands.

## The story

Theros is a world shaped by belief. Its gods live in Nyx, the starry realm of the night sky, and their power grows or fades with the devotion of mortals. Five major gods anchor the pantheon, one per color: **Heliod**, the sun god (white); **Thassa**, god of the sea (blue); **Erebos**, god of the dead (black); **Purphoros**, god of the forge (red); and **Nylea**, god of the hunt (green). Ten minor two-color gods arrived later in the block. The gods meddle constantly. They pick champions, start rivalries and punish mortals who forget them. The plane's three great city-states, Meletis, Akros and Setessa, each favor different gods.

The set follows two planeswalkers. **Elspeth Tirel** arrives battered after the fall of Mirrodin to New Phyrexia, where she fought and lost. All she wants is a quiet life. Priests at a temple of Pharika nurse her back to health, and she meets Daxos, a young man from Meletis who becomes her closest friend on the plane. But when she kills a monstrous hydra that had already slain many heroes, Heliod takes notice and decides she will be his champion, whether she likes it or not.

The other is **Xenagos**, a satyr from Theros who spent his youth in endless revels until his planeswalker spark ignited. After seeing how vast the Multiverse is, his home and its gods look small to him. He decides to become a god himself, and starts gathering followers through wild celebrations, his "revels," to build the worship he needs.

Theros sets up the block rather than resolving it. In Born of the Gods, Xenagos succeeds and ascends as the god of revels. In Journey into Nyx, Elspeth climbs into Nyx and kills him with Godsend, the weapon Heliod gave her, and Heliod, fearing a mortal who can kill gods, turns on her. The planeswalker **Ashiok**, a sculptor of nightmares, also roams the plane and appears in the set as a planeswalker card.

## Mechanics

### Heroic

Heroic triggers whenever you cast a spell that targets the creature. **Favored Hoplite** gets a +1/+1 counter and prevents all damage to it each time, so cheap tricks and Auras turn it into a big threat. Heroic decks play lots of one-mana spells that target their own creatures, which also makes them vulnerable to removal in response.

### Devotion

Your devotion to a color is the number of mana symbols of that color among the mana costs of permanents you control. The Gods use it: each is indestructible, but it isn't a creature until your devotion to its color reaches five. **Gray Merchant of Asphodel** drains each opponent for your devotion to black when it enters, and **Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx** adds mana equal to your devotion to a color. Devotion rewards decks that commit hard to one color.

### Bestow

Bestow creatures can be cast as an Aura for their bestow cost. If the creature it enchants leaves, the Aura falls off and becomes a creature again, so you don't lose the card to removal. **Boon Satyr** is a flash 4/2, or a +4/+2 bonus you can bestow on another creature.

### Monstrosity

Monstrosity N is an activated ability: if the creature isn't monstrous yet, put N +1/+1 counters on it and it becomes monstrous, often with a trigger. **Polukranos, World Eater** is a four-mana 5/5 whose monstrosity deals X damage divided among the opponent's creatures. Monstrosity gives creatures a late-game mana sink.

### Scry and enchantments

Scry returned as a common effect, on cards like **Magma Jet** and the five rare Temples, which enter tapped and scry 1. Enchantments were everywhere: enchantment creatures, Auras and a cycle of legendary enchantment artifacts, the gods' weapons, like **Whip of Erebos**. ## Limited

Theros is mostly a monocolored set with a handful of gold cards, and its Limited play revolved around a few strong themes:

- **White-blue heroic.** Fliers and cheap targeting spells; Battlewise Hoplite grows with each one.
- **Red-white heroic aggro.** Akroan Hoplite and cheap tricks push damage fast.
- **Blue-black control.** Shipwreck Singer forces attacks and shrinks attackers.
- **Black-red Minotaurs.** Kragma Warcaller gives Minotaurs haste and extra power.
- **Green-white and green-based monsters.** Big monstrosity creatures and Chronicler of Heroes.
- **White-black enchantments and fliers.** Sentry of the Underworld anchors the slower decks.
- **Blue-red spells.** Spellheart Chimera grows with instants and sorceries.
- **Black-green value.** Pharika's Mender gets back creatures or enchantments.
- **Green-blue fliers and ramp.** Horizon Chimera adds evasive pressure.
- **Devotion.** Heavy single-color decks with Gray Merchant at common.

Gray Merchant of Asphodel was a top black common, especially in heavy-black decks. Removal like **Lightning Strike**, **Voyage's End** and **Sip of Hemlock**, and bestow creatures that are hard to two-for-one, shaped the format. Heroic decks were strong, but they could lose badly to instant-speed removal. Our [deck archetypes guide](/blog/mtg-deck-archetypes-explained) explains how to read a set's archetypes.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Theros made devotion the defining theme of its first Standard season. At Pro Tour Theros in Dublin in October 2013, Jérémy Dezani won with Mono-Blue Devotion, beating his teammate in a Master of Waves mirror in the final. Mono-Black Devotion, with Gray Merchant, Erebos and Whip of Erebos, made the Top 8 and became one of the season's best-known decks. Esper Control used Elspeth, Sun's Champion as its finisher, and green-red Monsters decks used Polukranos, Stormbreath Dragon and Xenagos.

When Khans of Tarkir arrived in 2014, Theros stayed relevant. Abzan midrange decks used Elspeth, Hero's Downfall, Thoughtseize and Sylvan Caryatid, and Stormbreath Dragon kept a place in red decks. No Theros card was banned in Standard, and the set rotated out when Battle for Zendikar arrived in October 2015. Outside Standard, Prophet of Kruphix, a green-blue rare, was banned in Commander in January 2016 because untapping on every opponent's turn is overwhelming in multiplayer games.

Theros matters even more in Pioneer, which begins with Return to Ravnica. Its printing of **Thoughtseize** is the reason that card is Pioneer-legal, and it has been one of the format's most-played black cards. **Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx** powers devotion decks. Green Nykthos combo decks were strong enough that Wizards banned Leyline of Abundance, a key partner card from another set. **Voyaging Satyr** untaps lands in Lotus Field combo, and the **Temples** fill out mana bases. In Modern, Anger of the Gods, Swan Song and Destructive Revelry have been sideboard options. As of October 2026, no Theros card is on the Pioneer, Modern or Legacy ban lists.

In [Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh), the Gods are popular mono-color commanders. Thassa, God of the Sea, Purphoros, God of the Forge and Erebos, God of the Dead each lead their own decks, Gray Merchant is a mono-black favorite, and Nykthos doubles as a ramp land.

## Cards from Theros worth owning

- **Thoughtseize**: a one-mana discard spell that's a staple of Pioneer, Modern and Legacy.
- **Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx**: devotion ramp for Pioneer and mono-color Commander decks.
- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion**: a classic finisher for white decks in casual and Commander play.
- **The Temple cycle** (Temple of Abandon, Deceit, Mystery, Silence and Triumph): scry lands that fit Pioneer and Commander.
- **Gray Merchant of Asphodel**: the drain payoff for any black devotion deck.
- **Hero's Downfall**: instant-speed removal for creatures and planeswalkers.
- **Thassa, God of the Sea**: a strong mono-blue commander.

Theros rewards commitment. Devotion pays off when you load up on one color, heroic when you lean into cheap targeting spells, and bestow when you want creatures that survive removal. In Pioneer, start with Thoughtseize, Nykthos and Gray Merchant. Try a list in the [Deck Lab builder](/).
