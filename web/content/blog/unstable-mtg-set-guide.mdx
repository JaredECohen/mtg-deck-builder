Unstable is the fourth Magic: The Gathering "Un-set," the humor line that bends or breaks the game's normal rules, released in December 2017. Set on Bablovia, a world of mad science, it split its cards between five rival factions and introduced two new systems: Contraptions, and host and augment creatures that combine into one.

## What the set was for

Un-sets exist to let designers try ideas that would never work in a tournament set: cards that ask you to do things in the real world, mechanics that need a ruler or a die, and jokes about Magic itself. Unstable was the first Un-set in more than a decade, following Unhinged in 2004, and it set out to be both funny and a genuinely good draft format.

Its flavor hook is Bablovia, a world where inventors compete with ridiculous creations. Instead of a plot with a beginning and end, the set has five factions, each shown by a watermark on its cards and each owning a color pair and a mechanical identity:

- **Order of the Widget (white-blue):** tidy inventors who build Contraptions and Cyborg creatures.
- **Agents of S.N.E.A.K. (blue-black):** spies and ninjas who sneak past blockers, led by Phoebe, Head of S.N.E.A.K. and the legendary spy X.
- **League of Dastardly Doom (black-red):** cartoon villains with doomsday devices, like Baron Von Count and Grusilda, Monster Masher.
- **Goblin Explosioneers (red-green):** Goblins who roll dice and blow things up, headed by Ol' Buzzbark.
- **Crossbreed Labs (green-white):** mad biologists who stitch creatures together, led by Dr. Julius Jumblemorph.

A handful of cards care about the watermarks themselves. Watermarket is a land whose mana can only be spent on spells with watermarks, and Druid of the Sacred Beaker taps for more mana for each Crossbreed Labs permanent you control.

## Mechanics

### Contraptions and Riggers

Contraptions are artifacts that live in a separate Contraption deck, not in your main deck. You have three "sprockets," and a CRANK! counter moves from one sprocket to the next at the beginning of each of your turns, triggering (cranking) every Contraption on that sprocket. To get Contraptions out, a Rigger creature or another effect "assembles" one, putting the top card of your Contraption deck face up onto a sprocket of your choice. Crafty Octopus assembles one when it enters, and Steamflogger Boss makes every Rigger assemble two instead of one. Contraptions range from simple, like Division Table making a player lose 2 life each crank, to wild, like Hard Hat Area assembling more Contraptions.

### Host and augment

Host creatures, such as Adorable Kitten, have a "When this creature enters" trigger. Augment creatures are half-cards you keep in hand: pay the augment cost at sorcery speed to stick one onto a host you control, and the two become a single combined creature with both halves' abilities and a mashed-up name. If you attach an augment to a host that's already on the battlefield, its enters trigger fires again. It's the Crossbreed Labs mechanic, and Clever Combo tutors up a host or augment card.

### Dice and variants

Dice rolling is everywhere in red and green. Box of Free-Range Goblins makes a number of Goblins equal to a die roll, and Hydradoodle rolls X dice to set its size. Unstable also printed several cards in multiple versions with the same name but different text. Very Cryptic Command has six versions with different modes, Everythingamajig and Garbage Elemental come in several flavors, and the Killbots are a family of identical 2/1 artifact creatures with different expressions.

### Real-world jokes

As in every Un-set, some cards reach outside the game. Chivalrous Chevalier asks you to compliment an opponent, Kindslaver hands control of a player's turn to someone outside the game, and Summon the Pack has you open a sealed booster pack mid-game.

## Limited

Unstable was designed to be drafted, and it holds up as one of the most loved casual draft formats. Each faction is effectively a two-color archetype:

- **White-blue Contraptions:** Riggers like Aerial Toastmaster and Chipper Chopper assemble Contraptions that pump, protect or drain every turn. This deck rewards you for drafting a solid Contraption deck along with your main cards.
- **Blue-black spies:** evasive creatures and disruption, often with unblockable attackers like Defective Detective.
- **Black-red villains:** removal and nasty legends, with dice and life loss.
- **Red-green Goblins and dice:** go wide with Goblin tokens and roll dice for value, often with Contraptions too.
- **Green-white crossbreeds:** hosts and augments that stack enters triggers and build oversized combined creatures.

Contraptions are drafted alongside regular cards, and a Contraption deck is optional, so you only need one if you're playing Riggers. The format runs a bit slower than a typical tournament set because so many cards create incremental value. If you've never drafted, our guide to [draft versus sealed](/blog/draft-vs-sealed-limited-guide) covers the basics.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Unstable had almost no impact on Constructed, by design. Silver-bordered cards aren't legal in Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper or Commander. The exceptions are the black-bordered cards: the full-art basic lands, which are legal everywhere, and the Steamflogger Boss reprint, which is legal in Modern, Legacy, Vintage and Commander. Steamflogger Boss is mainly a novelty there, since Contraptions themselves aren't legal.

That changed in spirit with Unfinity in 2022, which printed black-bordered Un-cards that are tournament-legal and marked the casual-only ones with an acorn. You can read more in our [Unfinity set guide](/blog/unfinity-mtg-set-guide). Unstable predates that split, so in Commander its cards are rule-zero territory: fine if your group agrees, not legal otherwise. See [Commander rule zero](/blog/commander-rule-zero-guide) for how to have that conversation.

## Cards from Unstable worth owning

- **Steamflogger Boss:** the only legal nonbasic, and a fun Goblin for Commander collectors.
- **The full-art basic lands:** legal in every format and some of the most distinctive basics ever printed.
- **Very Cryptic Command:** the variants are a collection in themselves.
- **Urza, Academy Headmaster and Sword of Dungeons & Dragons:** the iconic jokes of the set, great for casual cubes.
- **A box of commons and Contraptions:** if you want to draft it with friends, it still plays well.

Unstable is a set to play for fun: draft it with friends, or ask your Commander group if they'll allow a few Un-cards. If you'd rather build something fully legal with the same playful spirit, a Squirrel deck is a natural fit, and the [MTG Deck Lab builder](/) can put one together.
