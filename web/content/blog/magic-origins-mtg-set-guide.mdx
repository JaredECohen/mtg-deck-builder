Magic Origins is the Magic: The Gathering core set released in July 2015, and it told the origin stories of the five planeswalkers who would become the Gatewatch. Each of them got a double-faced legendary creature that flips into a planeswalker. At the time it was billed as the last core set, it added two new mechanics and made menace an evergreen keyword, and Jace, Vryn's Prodigy and Hangarback Walker became staples well beyond Standard.

## The story

Magic Origins had two jobs. As a core set, it was meant to be an entry point for newer players, with simple commons and familiar effects. As a story set, it introduced the cast for the next several years of Magic storytelling, so its five headline legends each show a planeswalker in the moment before their spark ignites. Wizards also tied each two-color Limited archetype to one of the planes in the set.

### Gideon: Theros

Kytheon Iora is a street kid from Akros who leads a gang of young fighters, Kytheon's Irregulars. In prison he learns hieromancy, the magic of order, from the warden Hixus. After he defends the city, the sun god Heliod picks him to kill a titan sent by Erebos, the god of the dead. Kytheon succeeds, then in a moment of pride throws Heliod's spear at Erebos himself. Erebos turns it aside, and it kills all of the Irregulars. Shattered, Kytheon planeswalks to Bant, where he takes the name Gideon.

### Jace: Vryn

Jace Beleren is a gifted young telepath on Vryn, a plane split by a war between two factions. He trains under the sphinx Alhammarret, who uses him as a spy and secretly wipes his memories, including the memory that his spark already ignited once. When Jace finds out that Alhammarret is feeding both sides to keep the war going, he challenges him to a mental duel and destroys his mind. The fight costs Jace much of his own memory, and he planeswalks to Ravnica without knowing who he is.

### Liliana: Dominaria

Liliana Vess is a young healer in a holy order who secretly studies necromancy. When her brother Josu is struck by a magical illness, a mysterious "Raven Man" talks her into a necromantic cure. It turns Josu into a crazed undead creature. Liliana raises his victims as zombies to fight him, and in that moment her spark ignites and she lands on Innistrad.

### Chandra: Kaladesh

Chandra Nalaar is the fiery daughter of two inventors, Pia and Kiran Nalaar, who smuggle aether, a controlled resource, on the artifice plane of Kaladesh. Soldiers of the Consulate, led by the officer Baral, burn her village and hunt her family. She's taken to the city of Ghirapur to be executed, and her spark ignites in a burst of flame that carries her to Regatha, where the monks of Keral Keep take her in.

### Nissa: Zendikar

Nissa Revane is an elf animist, able to speak with the land itself, from a tribe that fears her visions. She leaves home, and Zendikar's soul guides her toward a horror trapped inside a mountain: the Eldrazi. Trying to destroy it ignites her spark. She arrives on Lorwyn, where she is pulled into an elf hunt against innocent boggarts just as the Great Aurora turns the plane into Shadowmoor. She finds her way home to Zendikar.

## Mechanics

### Flip-walkers

The five mythic legends are double-faced cards. Each starts as a creature and transforms into a planeswalker when you meet a condition. **Jace, Vryn's Prodigy** loots (draws, then discards) each turn and flips into Jace, Telepath Unbound once you have five or more cards in your graveyard. Unlike most transforms, the card is exiled and returns transformed, so the planeswalker arrives fresh with its full loyalty. Our guide to [how to read a Magic card](/blog/how-to-read-a-magic-card) covers card frames and double-faced cards.

### Renown

Renown N puts N +1/+1 counters on a creature the first time it deals combat damage to a player, and the creature becomes "renowned." Some cards also trigger when they become renowned. **Relic Seeker** is a two-mana 2/2 that searches your library for an Equipment once it's renowned. Renown rewards attacking early.

### Spell mastery

Spell mastery is an ability word that gives a bonus if you have two or more instant and sorcery cards in your graveyard. **Exquisite Firecraft** deals 4 damage to any target, and with spell mastery it can't be countered. **Dark Petition** is a tutor that refunds `{B}{B}{B}` with spell mastery.

### Menace and prowess

Menace, "can't be blocked except by two or more creatures," became an evergreen keyword here, replacing intimidate. **Boggart Brute** is the common example. Prowess, which gives a creature +1/+1 until end of turn whenever you cast a noncreature spell, was also made evergreen, and scry joined it.

### Thopters

Kaladesh's artifice shows up as a red and blue Thopter subtheme. **Whirler Rogue** makes two 1/1 flying Thopters and can tap two artifacts to make a creature unblockable, and **Hangarback Walker** turns its +1/+1 counters into Thopters when it dies.

## Limited

Each two-color pair is tied to a plane, with a gold uncommon to point the way:

- **White-blue (Vryn): fliers and spell mastery.** Thunderclap Wyvern pumps your other fliers.
- **Blue-black (Innistrad): graveyard value.** Possessed Skaab returns a spell or creature.
- **Black-red (Regatha): sacrifice.** Blazing Hellhound sacrifices creatures to ping.
- **Red-green (Zendikar): lands and big creatures.** Zendikar Incarnate grows with your land count.
- **Green-white (Bant): renown and pump.** Citadel Castellan gets bigger as it connects.
- **White-black (Dominaria): Auras and enchantments.** Blood-Cursed Knight gains lifelink with an enchantment.
- **Blue-red (Kaladesh): artifacts and tempo.** Reclusive Artificer pings with artifacts.
- **Black-green (Lorwyn): Elves.** Shaman of the Pack drains for each Elf.
- **Red-white (Theros): go wide.** Iroas's Champion has double strike.
- **Green-blue (Ravnica): tempo.** Bounding Krasis taps or untaps a creature with flash.

As a core set, Magic Origins kept most of its commons simple. Removal like **Fiery Impulse**, **Celestial Flare** and **Suppression Bonds**, along with renown attackers like **Topan Freeblade**, kept games moving. The Thopter and Elf subthemes gave drafters synergy without many moving parts. ## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Magic Origins entered a Standard defined by the Khans of Tarkir block. At Pro Tour Magic Origins in Vancouver in August 2015, Joel Larsson won with Mono-Red Aggro, and the new Abbot of Keral Keep and Exquisite Firecraft were key parts of red's success. Blue-red Thopter decks also reached the Top 8, using the set's Thopter makers and Hangarback Walker.

Over the following year, **Jace, Vryn's Prodigy** became one of the defining cards of Standard, showing up in many blue decks from control to midrange. **Hangarback Walker** fit artifact and midrange decks, **Languish** and **Tragic Arrogance** gave control decks sweepers, and **Nissa, Vastwood Seer** was a staple for green midrange. No Magic Origins card was banned in Standard. The set rotated out with Dragons of Tarkir when Kaladesh arrived in September 2016.

Magic Origins has had a long life since. In Pioneer, **Kytheon, Hero of Akros** joins Human aggro decks, **Chandra, Fire of Kaladesh** appears in red aggro, **Languish** turns up in control, **Dark Petition** finds combo pieces in Lotus Field decks, and **Shaman of the Pack** and **Dwynen's Elite** power Elves. In Modern, **Hangarback Walker** is a key part of Hardened Scales artifact decks. Jace, Vryn's Prodigy saw play in Vintage, **Day's Undoing** in Legacy control and **Chief of the Foundry** in Vintage artifact aggro. As of October 2026, no Magic Origins card is on the Pioneer, Modern or Legacy ban lists.

In [Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh), Sigil of the Empty Throne and Starfield of Nyx lead enchantment decks, Pia and Kiran Nalaar and Thopter Spy Network help artifact decks, and Archangel of Tithes protects its controller in multiplayer games.

## Cards from Magic Origins worth owning

- **Jace, Vryn's Prodigy**: a card-selection engine that plays well in eternal formats.
- **Hangarback Walker**: an artifact threat that fits Modern Hardened Scales and casual artifact decks.
- **Kytheon, Hero of Akros**: a one-drop for white aggro in Pioneer.
- **Chandra, Fire of Kaladesh**: a red aggro card that flips into a burn planeswalker.
- **Dark Petition**: a tutor for spell-heavy combo decks.
- **Sigil of the Empty Throne**: a strong payoff for enchantment Commander decks.
- **The painlands** (Battlefield Forge, Caves of Koilos, Llanowar Wastes, Shivan Reef, Yavimaya Coast): reprinted dual lands that enter untapped, useful in Pioneer and Commander.

Magic Origins is the rare core set with a story worth reading, and its best cards still matter. The flip-walkers reward you for building around their conditions: give Jace a fuller graveyard, give Kytheon two attackers alongside him.
