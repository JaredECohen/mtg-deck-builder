Murders at Karlov Manor is a Magic: The Gathering murder-mystery set on Ravnica, the city-plane of the ten guilds, released in February 2024. A string of killings among Ravnica's leaders sends a detective agency hunting for the culprit, and the cards turn that into mechanics about hidden identities, evidence and suspects. Several of its cards became Constructed staples, and one, Proft's Eidetic Memory, was eventually banned in Standard.

## The story

After the Phyrexian invasion, Ravnica is rebuilding, and the guilds are holding a gala at Karlov Manor, the estate of Orzhov leader Teysa Karlov. During the party, Zegana, former leader of the Simic Combine, is murdered. Kaya discovers the body, and the newly formed Agency of detectives is brought in.

The lead investigator is Alquist Proft, an eccentric genius modeled on classic fictional detectives. Working with Kaya and the young detective Kellan, he quickly identifies the assassin Etrata. But Etrata has no memory of the killing, and Proft concludes she was mind-controlled: she was the weapon, not the killer. More killings follow, including the inventor Kylox, and the Rakdos assassin Massacre Girl tries to take out Proft himself before Etrata protects him. Krenko, the Goblin crime boss, and Teysa are drawn in as targets and suspects.

Proft eventually traces the mind-controlling pollen to a flower that only someone in command of Vitu-Ghazi, the Selesnya world-tree, could cultivate. The culprit is Oba, one of the three dryad sisters who make up Trostani. Oba believed each victim had failed Ravnica during the invasion, from Zegana's experiments with Phyrexian oil to Kylox's research into Phyrexian technology. When exposed, she seizes control of the tree and traps everyone inside, until Kaya weakens her by appealing to Ravnica itself and subdues her. The remaining sisters cut Oba away from Trostani.

The cast shows up all over the set: Alquist Proft, Master Sleuth, Kaya, Spirits' Justice, Kellan, Inquisitive Prodigy, Etrata, Deadly Fugitive, Massacre Girl, Known Killer, Teysa, Opulent Oligarch, Krenko, Baron of Tin Street, Kylox, Visionary Inventor and Trostani, Three Whispers. Even the investigation tools get cards, like Proft's Eidetic Memory.

## Mechanics

### Disguise

Disguise is an updated version of morph. You can cast a disguise card face down for `{3}` as a 2/2 creature with ward `{2}`, then turn it face up any time for its disguise cost. Turning it up doesn't use the stack, so it can't be responded to. Unyielding Gatekeeper is a two-mana 3/2 that, when turned face up for `{1}{W}`, exiles another nonland permanent, returning it if it was yours. Ward `{2}` makes face-down creatures much harder to remove cheaply than old morphs.

### Cloak

Cloak puts a card onto the battlefield face down as a 2/2 with ward `{2}`. If it's a creature card, you can turn it up for its mana cost. Cryptic Coat cloaks the top card of your library, attaches itself, and makes that creature unblockable with +1/+0; you can bounce the Coat for `{1}{U}` and do it again.

### Collect evidence

Collect evidence N means exiling cards from your graveyard with total mana value N or more. It's a cost that unlocks bonuses. Deadly Cover-Up is a board wipe that, if you collect evidence 6, also exiles every copy of a card from an opponent's deck. Conspiracy Unraveler lets you collect evidence 10 instead of paying mana costs. It pairs naturally with self-mill.

### Suspect

A suspected creature has menace and can't block. Suspecting your own creature turns it into an attacker; suspecting an opponent's removes a blocker. Agrus Kos, Spirit of Justice suspects a creature when he enters or attacks, and exiles it if it was already suspected.

### Cases

Cases are enchantments with an ongoing effect, a "to solve" condition checked at your end step, and a stronger "solved" ability. Case of the Uneaten Feast gains you life whenever a creature enters, is solved once you gain 5 life in a turn, and can then be sacrificed to let you cast creature cards from your graveyard that turn.

### Investigate and surveil lands

Investigate creates Clue tokens that you can sacrifice for `{2}` to draw a card. Clues are everywhere in the set, from Novice Inspector at common to Alquist Proft. The set also introduced ten surveil dual lands, such as Meticulous Archive and Underground Mortuary, which enter tapped and surveil 1.

## Limited

Murders at Karlov Manor has ten two-color archetypes built around the guilds, with gold uncommon signposts:

- **White-blue (Azorius) Detectives:** Private Eye pumps your Detectives and makes them unblockable.
- **Blue-black (Dimir) Clues and value:** Curious Cadaver returns to hand when you sacrifice a Clue.
- **Black-red (Rakdos) suspects:** Rune-Brand Juggler suspects your own creature to turn it into an attacker.
- **Red-green (Gruul) disguise:** Tin Street Gossip makes mana for face-down spells and flipping them.
- **Green-white (Selesnya) face-down payoffs:** Sumala Sentry grows creatures as they turn face up.
- **White-black (Orzhov) small creatures:** Wispdrinker Vampire drains whenever a small creature enters.
- **Blue-red (Izzet) artifacts and Clues:** Gleaming Geardrake and Detective's Satchel make Clues.
- **Black-green (Golgari) graveyard:** Insidious Roots turns creature tokens into mana and makes Plants as cards leave your graveyard.
- **Red-white (Boros) go-wide attacks:** Meddling Youths investigates whenever you attack with three or more creatures.
- **Green-blue (Simic) collect evidence:** Evidence Examiner collects evidence each combat for Clues.

Interaction was plentiful at common: Murder, Galvanize, Make Your Move and Shock kill creatures, and Out Cold locks down two attackers or blockers. Disguise creatures were valuable because every face-down 2/2 with ward `{2}` can be anything, which makes combat a guessing game. Reading face-down creatures, and holding up the mana to flip your own, is the key skill. Cards that mill or fill the graveyard were also worth more than usual, because collect evidence needs fuel.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

In Pioneer, the set made an immediate splash. Pro Tour Murders at Karlov Manor, held in February 2024, was a Pioneer event, and Seth Manfield won it with Rakdos Vampires. His deck's new trick was Vein Ripper paired with Sorin, Imperious Bloodlord, which can put the six-drop onto the battlefield as early as turn three.

In Modern, Leyline of the Guildpact found a home in Domain Zoo, where starting the game with it gives every land all five basic types, turning on cards like Scion of Draco immediately. Domain Zoo lists in 2024 also ran Pick Your Poison in their sideboards. Aftermath Analyst became part of Modern Amulet Titan, which was still among the decks Wizards discussed in its November 2025 ban update.

In Standard, the set's effect built up over time. Its surveil lands, Long Goodbye, No More Lies and Lightning Helix were staple role-players. Later, Proft's Eidetic Memory became a key draw engine in Izzet Cauldron, the deck Wizards called the strongest in Standard, and it was banned in Standard on November 10, 2025, alongside Vivi Ornitier and Screaming Nemesis. It remains legal in Pioneer and Modern. As of September 2026, Case of the Uneaten Feast was still showing up in Orzhov life-gain decks in Wizards' Standard metagame reports.

In Commander, the set is well loved: Etrata, Deadly Fugitive, Massacre Girl, Known Killer, Kellan, Inquisitive Prodigy and Teysa, Opulent Oligarch all lead decks, and Leyline of the Guildpact is a good fit for multicolor builds. If you're new to that format, start with [what Commander is](/blog/what-is-commander-edh).

## Cards from Murders at Karlov Manor worth owning

- **Leyline of the Guildpact:** a Modern Domain piece and a Commander utility card.
- **Vein Ripper:** a Pioneer threat and an aristocrats finisher in Commander.
- **Pick Your Poison:** a cheap, flexible sideboard card in nearly every format.
- **The surveil lands:** long-lived dual lands for Pioneer and Commander after they leave Standard.
- **Aftermath Analyst:** a lands-deck staple in Modern and Commander.
- **Etrata, Deadly Fugitive:** one of the set's most popular commanders.

## The takeaway

Murders at Karlov Manor is one of the most flavorful Ravnica sets: a full detective story whose mechanics actually feel like investigating. For Limited, value removal and learn to read face-down creatures. For Constructed, its staying power is in the surveil lands, Leyline of the Guildpact, Vein Ripper and Pick Your Poison, which will matter in Pioneer and Modern long after the set leaves Standard.
