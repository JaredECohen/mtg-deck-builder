Duel Decks were a Magic: The Gathering product line of paired preconstructed decks, sold from November 2007 to April 2018, each box holding two ready-to-play 60-card decks built to battle each other. The series ran to 21 numbered releases, starting and ending with Elves, plus a 2014 Anthology box and a 2018 Global Series spin-off. Many of them pitted planeswalkers against each other and carried foil reprints of iconic cards.

## What the set was for

Duel Decks solved a simple problem: how do two people start playing Magic together without building anything? Each box contained two balanced decks designed to be played against each other, so friends, siblings or a teacher and a new player could open it and shuffle up. The decks were tuned to be fair matchups, and they leaned on recognizable themes so that each side felt different.

They were also a reprint vehicle. Most releases had a foil "face card" for each deck, often with new art, and slipped in older cards that had become hard to find. Over time they became a casual-player favorite, and Commander players still mine them for staples.

There's no single story. Some decks were built around a theme (Divine vs. Demonic, Heroes vs. Monsters), some around a plane's factions (Phyrexia vs. the Coalition, Zendikar vs. Eldrazi), and many around a pair of planeswalkers whose rivalry appeared in Magic's story at the time (Jace vs. Chandra, Sorin vs. Tibalt, Elspeth vs. Kiora).

## The early years (2007 to 2010)

- **Elves vs. Goblins (2007):** the first Duel Decks release. Ambush Commander led the Elves and Siege-Gang Commander led the Goblins. The Elves deck also packed Llanowar Elves, Wood Elves and Harmonize, all Commander-friendly reprints.
- **Jace vs. Chandra (2008):** the first planeswalker matchup, with foil Jace Beleren and Chandra Nalaar. The blue deck included Counterspell, Mulldrifter, Fact or Fiction and Ancestral Vision.
- **Divine vs. Demonic (2009):** angels against demons, with Akroma, Angel of Wrath and Lord of the Pit as the face cards and a reprint of Demonic Tutor and Dark Ritual on the black side.
- **Garruk vs. Liliana (2009):** green beasts against black recursion, with Garruk Wildspeaker and Liliana Vess as mythic face cards and Rancor, Harmonize and Snuff Out in the lists.
- **Phyrexia vs. the Coalition (2010):** a battle from the Invasion storyline, with Phyrexian Negator and Urza's Rage as the face cards and Phyrexian Arena and Lightning Greaves among the reprints.
- **Elspeth vs. Tezzeret (2010):** white soldiers against blue artifacts, with Elspeth, Knight-Errant, Tezzeret the Seeker, Steel Overseer and Swords to Plowshares.

## The middle years (2011 to 2014)

- **Knights vs. Dragons (2011):** Knight of the Reliquary against Bogardan Hellkite.
- **Ajani vs. Nicol Bolas (2011):** Ajani Vengeant against Nicol Bolas, Planeswalker, a nod to their clash during the Shards of Alara story.
- **Venser vs. Koth (2012):** Venser, the Sojourner against Koth of the Hammer, with Path to Exile and Preordain in the blue-white deck.
- **Izzet vs. Golgari (2012):** Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind against Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord, with Eternal Witness and Brainstorm among the reprints.
- **Sorin vs. Tibalt (2013):** Sorin, Lord of Innistrad's tokens against Tibalt, the Fiend-Blooded's chaos.
- **Heroes vs. Monsters (2013):** a Theros-themed release with Sun Titan and Polukranos, World Eater as face cards.
- **Jace vs. Vraska (2014):** Jace, Architect of Thought against Vraska the Unseen.
- **Speed vs. Cunning (2014):** Zurgo Helmsmasher against Arcanis the Omnipotent, with Goblin Bombardment in the speed deck.

### Duel Decks Anthology (December 2014)

The Anthology collected the first four releases (Elves vs. Goblins, Jace vs. Chandra, Divine vs. Demonic and Garruk vs. Liliana) in one box with eight decks, so players who missed the early releases could get them. Scryfall lists each pair as its own set (EVG, JVC, DVD and GVL).

## The late years (2015 to 2018)

- **Elspeth vs. Kiora (2015):** Elspeth, Sun's Champion against Kiora, the Crashing Wave, with Mother of Runes on the white side.
- **Zendikar vs. Eldrazi (2015):** Avenger of Zendikar against Oblivion Sower, timed with the Battle for Zendikar story.
- **Blessed vs. Cursed (2016):** an Innistrad-themed release with Geist of Saint Traft and Mindwrack Demon as face cards.
- **Nissa vs. Ob Nixilis (2016):** Nissa, Voice of Zendikar against Ob Nixilis Reignited.
- **Mind vs. Might (2017):** Jhoira of the Ghitu's spells against Lovisa Coldeyes's Barbarians and Warriors, with Talrand, Sky Summoner and Snap in the blue-red deck.
- **Merfolk vs. Goblins (2017):** Master of Waves against Warren Instigator, with Krenko, Mob Boss on the Goblin side.
- **Elves vs. Inventors (2018):** the 21st and final release, bringing the series full circle with Elves. Ezuri, Renegade Leader led the Elves and Goblin Welder was the Inventors deck's mythic.

Wizards announced the end of the series alongside the debut of Challenger Decks, a preconstructed product aimed at players heading to competitive events.

### The Global Series and a digital one-off

**Global Series: Jiang Yanggu & Mu Yanling** (June 2018) used the Duel Decks format for a release created with the Chinese office of Wizards of the Coast, introducing two planeswalkers inspired by Chinese mythology: Jiang Yanggu, a green planeswalker with a loyal dog named Mowu, and the blue Mu Yanling. It was released in Simplified Chinese and English. A separate **Mirrodin Pure vs. New Phyrexia** pair, built from community-submitted decklists around Platinum Angel and Phyrexian Plaguelord, was released only on Magic Online in January 2013.

Duel Decks also printed piles of everyday staples: Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Counterspell, Brainstorm, Eternal Witness, Harmonize, Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse, Mind Stone, Fact or Fiction and Mulldrifter all appear. For a newer player, that made them a cheap way to pick up cards that still see play in Commander. ## How it plays

Duel Decks are best played the way they were sold: two players, one box, a fair fight. The matchups were tuned so neither side was a big favorite, and each deck has a clear identity, so they're a great way to teach the basic [deck archetypes](/blog/mtg-deck-archetypes-explained). Elves vs. Goblins teaches go-wide creature combat; Jace vs. Chandra shows card advantage against direct damage; Speed vs. Cunning contrasts aggression with tricks.

For experienced players, the decks are a source of parts. A Duel Decks list makes a reasonable starting point for a casual 60-card deck, and many face cards are legendary creatures that can lead Commander decks: Krenko, Mob Boss, Ezuri, Renegade Leader, Talrand, Sky Summoner, Jhoira of the Ghitu, Niv-Mizzet, the Firemind and Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord all have their own Commander followings.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Duel Decks never added cards to Standard, and they rarely contained cards that mattered in competitive formats. The exceptions were reprints that eased supply for older formats: Path to Exile and Knight of the Reliquary were Modern-relevant reprints at the time, and Demonic Tutor and Swords to Plowshares were welcome for Legacy and Vintage players.

In Commander, their legacy is bigger. Garruk Wildspeaker, Avenger of Zendikar, Siege-Gang Commander, Krenko, Mob Boss, Ezuri, Renegade Leader and Talrand, Sky Summoner remain popular, and the steady reprints of Eternal Witness, Harmonize and similar staples kept those cards accessible. Demonic Tutor is on the Commander Game Changers list as of October 2026, which matters if your group uses the [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained).

## Cards from Duel Decks worth owning

- **Krenko, Mob Boss:** one of the best Goblin commanders ever printed.
- **Ezuri, Renegade Leader:** a strong Elf commander and a finisher for Elf decks.
- **Talrand, Sky Summoner:** a simple, effective spellslinger commander.
- **Avenger of Zendikar:** a top-end threat for landfall decks.
- **Garruk Wildspeaker:** a ramp-and-finisher planeswalker for green Commander decks.
- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion:** a board-stabilizing planeswalker for white decks.
- **Demonic Tutor:** the classic tutor, now a Game Changer in Commander.

Duel Decks remain one of the friendliest ways to introduce someone to Magic, and old boxes are still a fun way to spend an evening.
