Commander 2021 is a set of five preconstructed Magic: The Gathering Commander decks released in April 2021 alongside Strixhaven: School of Mages. Each deck represents one of Strixhaven's five colleges, is led by one of the school's professors, and adds 16 new cards plus a shared new land to a 100-card list. The headline new mechanic was demonstrate, a sorcery keyword that lets you copy a spell if you also let an opponent copy it.

## What the set was for

Commander 2021 was the last product to carry the old yearly "Commander 20XX" name. Early Commander products were standalone themes, but like Commander 2020, which was built around Ikoria, these decks were tied to a premier set. They extend Strixhaven's own design: each college of the school is a two-color pair with its own magic and its own creature tokens, and each deck takes that identity and stretches it into a 100-card singleton game.

The flavor is light rather than plot-driven. Strixhaven is a magical university where students study under five colleges, and the main set's story follows a secret society, the Oriq, plotting against the school. The Commander decks stay out of that plot. Their commanders are the professors who run each college, and the new cards depict lectures, campus life and the school's rivalries. If you want the full Strixhaven story, see our [Strixhaven set guide](/blog/strixhaven-school-of-mages-mtg-set-guide).

### Demonstrate

Demonstrate appears on one sorcery in each color. When you cast a demonstrate spell, you may copy it; if you do, you choose an opponent, who also copies it. Replication Technique copies one of your permanents, Excavation Technique destroys a nonland permanent (its controller gets two Treasures), and Creative Technique reveals cards until it finds a nonland card you can cast for free. The opponent gets the copy too, so the mechanic is political by nature: you're usually picking the opponent least able to use it, or the one you want to help.

### The professors

- **Osgir, the Reconstructor** sacrifices artifacts for a quick pump and later exiles artifact cards from your graveyard to make two token copies.
- **Zaffai, Thunder Conductor** scrys on every instant or sorcery, makes 4/4 Elementals off spells with mana value 5 or more and deals 10 damage off spells with mana value 10 or more.
- **Adrix and Nev, Twincasters** has ward 2 and doubles every token you create.
- **Breena, the Demagogue** rewards attacks against the opponent with the most life: the attacker draws a card and you get two +1/+1 counters.
- **Willowdusk, Essence Seer** puts +1/+1 counters on a creature equal to the life you gained or lost this turn.

### Notable reprints

The decks are full of reliable staples. All five include Sol Ring, Arcane Signet and Command Tower, and the lists also carry Exotic Orchard, Reliquary Tower, Rogue's Passage, Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Beast Within, Blasphemous Act, Solemn Simulacrum, Ghostly Prison, Brainstorm, Ponder, Faithless Looting and Sun Titan. The decks also reprinted Dig Through Time and Treasure Cruise, two delve cards that are banned in Modern and restricted or banned in older formats but legal in Commander.

## How it plays

Commander 2021 decks are some of the most consistent precons of their era, mostly because each college gave the designers a clear two-color plan.

**Quantum Quandrix** is often rated the strongest out of the box. Adrix and Nev doubles every token, which turns Fractal makers like Fractal Harness, Paradox Zone and Geometric Nexus into large threats, and Esix, Fractal Bloom adds a clone engine as an alternate commander. Expect it to win long games.

**Prismari Performance** is the most explosive. Zaffai rewards expensive spells, and the deck is full of big instants and sorceries to feed him. It can stumble if it draws its expensive spells early.

**Lorehold Legacies** builds an artifact board and recurs it with Osgir and Wake the Past. It's slower to start but very resilient.

**Silverquill Statement** plays politics. Breena gives cards and counters to anyone who attacks the life-total leader, so you can direct the table's aggression. It's a good deck for players who like making deals.

**Witherbloom Witchcraft** gains and drains life, using Willowdusk to turn that into +1/+1 counters and Gyome, Master Chef to make Food. It's the most grindy and defensive of the five.

All five sit in the casual-to-focused range. Under the official [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained), an unmodified deck fits the core bracket, and a few targeted upgrades can push it higher.

### Upgrading the decks

The upgrade path depends on the commander's engine. Quantum Quandrix wants more cheap token makers and other doublers. Prismari Performance wants better card draw and more mana rocks so its big spells come down on time. Lorehold Legacies benefits from cheaper artifacts and sacrifice outlets. Silverquill Statement improves with more +1/+1 counter cards and ways to protect Breena. Witherbloom Witchcraft gets better with repeatable life gain triggers. For a step-by-step method, our guide on [how to upgrade a Commander precon](/blog/how-to-upgrade-a-commander-precon) covers what to cut first.

## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Commander 2021 never touched Standard, Pioneer or Modern; its new cards are legal in Commander, Legacy and Vintage, and none became a regular in competitive Legacy or Vintage decks.

In Commander, the set produced several long-term favorites. Adrix and Nev, Twincasters is a popular token commander and a common addition to token decks led by other commanders. Veyran, Voice of Duality became a go-to spellslinger commander because it doubles magecraft and prowess-style triggers on your permanents. Esix, Fractal Bloom and Felisa, Fang of Silverquill have their own followings. Among the new noncreature cards, Cursed Mirror, Archaeomancer's Map, Monologue Tax and Inkshield are the ones that show up most often outside their original decks. As of October 2026, none of the set's new cards are banned in Commander or on the Game Changers list.

## Cards from Commander 2021 worth owning

- **Adrix and Nev, Twincasters:** a token doubler on a commander-ready body.
- **Veyran, Voice of Duality:** a strong commander or support card for instant-and-sorcery decks.
- **Esix, Fractal Bloom:** a unique clone engine for green-blue.
- **Archaeomancer's Map:** ramp for white, the color that usually lacks it.
- **Monologue Tax:** steady Treasure in multiplayer games.
- **Cursed Mirror:** a mana rock that doubles as a hasty clone.
- **Study Hall:** a flexible land for any multicolor deck.

Commander 2021 is a strong pick for new Commander players: each deck has a clear plan, good reprints and a commander that still gets built today. If you want the most powerful deck to start with, take Quantum Quandrix; if you want the most interactive, take Silverquill Statement.
