Amonkhet is the Magic: The Gathering expansion released in April 2017, set on a desert plane inspired by ancient Egypt and ruled in secret by the dragon Nicol Bolas. Its people train their whole lives for deadly trials, and the Gatewatch arrives to take Bolas down. The set brought embalm, exert and aftermath, revived cycling, and gave Standard Glorybringer, five new Gods and a Pro Tour-winning Zombies deck.

## The story

On Amonkhet, life looks glorious. The city of Naktamun sits behind a great magical barrier, the Hekma, which keeps out the undead and the sandstorms of the desert. Five Gods live among the people. Every young person joins a "crop" of initiates and trains to face the **Trials of the Five Gods**: Oketra's trial of solidarity, Kefnet's of knowledge, Rhonas's of strength, Bontu's of ambition and Hazoret's of zeal. Those who pass become the Worthy, and a glorious death in the trials earns a place in the afterlife. Everyone waits for the God-Pharaoh's promised return, when the second sun settles between two great horns on the horizon.

The God-Pharaoh is Nicol Bolas. He conquered the plane long ago, bent its gods to his will and wove his own worship into their teachings. The trials are his way of finding the strongest warriors, and the mummified dead, the Anointed, are being turned into an army.

The Gatewatch arrive to stop him: Gideon Jura, Jace Beleren, Liliana Vess, Chandra Nalaar and Nissa Revane. Oketra greets Gideon as one of her own, and he joins a crop led by the warrior Djeru to take the trials from the inside. His faith cracks during Bontu's trial, when the god asks the initiates to kill their own crop-mates and he refuses. Liliana, meanwhile, has her own reason for coming: one of the demons who holds her contract is hiding here. Nissa senses something rotten in the plane's weak, corrupted leylines. The dissenter Samut, who knows the truth about Bolas, becomes the Gatewatch's ally.

The set ends at the final trial. Gideon and Samut are forced to fight the Worthy, and Gideon steps between Hazoret and Djeru to spare him from the trial's "reward." Hazoret tells him he will die at immortal hands. The second sun keeps moving toward the horns, and the Hour of Revelation is close. The story continues in Hour of Devastation, where Bolas returns and soundly defeats the Gatewatch.

## Mechanics

### Cycling (returning)

Cycling lets you pay a cost and discard the card to draw a new one. Amonkhet put it on spells, creatures and a cycle of rare dual lands, and many cards reward you for cycling or discarding. **Censor** is a counterspell that taxes `{1}`, or you can cycle it for `{U}` when it's no longer useful. **Archfiend of Ifnir** puts a -1/-1 counter on each opposing creature whenever you cycle or discard another card.

### Embalm

Embalm lets you exile a creature card from your graveyard to make a token copy of it. The token is a white Zombie with no mana cost. **Sacred Cat** is a one-mana 1/1 lifelink that comes back for `{W}`, so it chump blocks twice.

### Exert

When you attack with a creature that has exert, you can choose to exert it for a bonus. In return, it doesn't untap during your next untap step. **Glorybringer** is the famous one: a 4/4 flying, hasty Dragon that deals 4 damage to a non-Dragon creature when exerted, so it can kill a creature every other turn.

### Aftermath

Aftermath cards are split cards. You cast the top half from your hand. The bottom half can only be cast from your graveyard, and then it's exiled. **Never // Return** destroys a creature or planeswalker, then later makes a 2/2 Zombie. **Commit // Memory** puts a spell or permanent back into its owner's library, then later resets both players' hands. If split cards are new to you, our guide to [how to read a Magic card](/blog/how-to-read-a-magic-card) covers the basics.

### -1/-1 counters, Cartouches and Trials

Black and green use -1/-1 counters, often placed on your own creatures for a bonus. **Channeler Initiate** puts three on itself and then removes them to make mana. Cartouches are Auras for your own creatures, and each color's Trial enchantment returns to your hand whenever a Cartouche enters. **Cartouche of Solidarity** makes a 1/1 Warrior and gives first strike.

## Limited

Amonkhet's ten two-color archetypes each have a gold uncommon:

- **White-blue: embalm and fliers.** Aven Wind Guide gives your tokens flying and vigilance.
- **Blue-black: cycling and discard.** Shadowstorm Vizier grows whenever you cycle or discard.
- **Black-red: aggressive discard.** Merciless Javelineer turns extra cards into -1/-1 counters.
- **Red-green: big attackers.** Khenra Charioteer gives your team trample.
- **Green-white: exert.** Ahn-Crop Champion untaps your team after an exert attack.
- **White-black: Zombies.** Wayward Servant drains each time another Zombie enters.
- **Blue-red: spells.** Enigma Drake grows with instants and sorceries in your graveyard.
- **Black-green: -1/-1 counters.** Decimator Beetle moves counters onto enemy creatures.
- **Red-white: Warrior aggro.** Honored Crop-Captain pumps your other attackers.
- **Green-blue: ramp.** Weaver of Currents makes extra mana for big creatures.

Removal was plentiful. **Magma Spray**, **Electrify**, **Final Reward** and **Splendid Agony** were all good commons, and the Trials, Cartouches and embalm creatures rewarded decks that stuck to a plan. Cycling made the format more forgiving of floods, since dead cards could turn into new ones. ## The meta: how it played in Constructed

Amonkhet arrived just after Wizards banned Felidar Guardian, ending the Saheeli Rai combo deck. At Pro Tour Amonkhet in May 2017, Gerry Thompson won with Mono-Black Zombies, a deck built largely on the set's new Zombies. Aetherworks Marvel decks, from Kaladesh, were the other big story, and Marvel was banned a few weeks later.

For the rest of its time in Standard, Amonkhet fed a range of decks. **Glorybringer** became the face of red decks, and **Hazoret the Fervent** gave aggressive red lists an indestructible finisher. **Rhonas the Indomitable** joined green energy decks. White-blue control decks used **Approach of the Second Sun**, **Cast Out**, **Censor**, **Pull from Tomorrow** and the cycling lands. A short-lived combo deck used **New Perspectives** to cycle its whole hand for free. No Amonkhet card was banned in Standard, and the set rotated out when Guilds of Ravnica arrived in October 2018.

Amonkhet stayed in play long after. In Pioneer, **Soul-Scar Mage** joins red prowess and burn decks, **Liliana, Death's Majesty** and **Lord of the Accursed** have Zombie and black midrange homes, and the cycling lands and Cast Out turn up in control. In Modern, **As Foretold** powered a deck that cast suspend cards like Ancestral Vision for free, and **Vizier of Remedies** combos with Devoted Druid for infinite mana. As of October 2026, no Amonkhet card is on the Pioneer, Modern or Legacy ban lists.

In [Commander](/blog/what-is-commander-edh), Anointed Procession is a token staple, the Monument cycle (such as **Bontu's Monument**) helps mono-color creature decks, and Vizier of the Menagerie lets you cast creatures off the top of your library.

## Cards from Amonkhet worth owning

- **Anointed Procession**: doubles tokens for Commander and casual token decks.
- **Soul-Scar Mage**: a one-drop for Pioneer and Modern red decks.
- **Glorybringer**: a strong Dragon for red midrange and casual play.
- **Liliana, Death's Majesty**: a Zombie engine for Pioneer and Commander.
- **The cycling dual lands** (Irrigated Farmland, Fetid Pools, Canyon Slough, Sheltered Thicket, Scattered Groves): flexible lands that work with land-type searching.
- **Vizier of the Menagerie**: card advantage for creature-heavy Commander decks.
- **As Foretold**: a unique engine for free spells.

Amonkhet's best cards reward patience and planning: cycling smooths your draws, embalm gets two uses from one card, and exert trades tempo for power.
