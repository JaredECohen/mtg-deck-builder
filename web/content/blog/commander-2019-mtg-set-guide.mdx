Commander 2019 is the Magic: The Gathering product released in August 2019 that added four preconstructed Commander decks, each built around a returning mechanic: morph, madness, flashback and populate. It introduced popular commanders like Kadena, Slinking Sorcerer and Anje Falkenrath, and it is also remembered for one card that was eventually banned in Commander: Dockside Extortionist.

## What the set was for

Commander 2019 was part of Wizards' annual run of Commander decks that ran through the 2010s. Each year brought a new set of multiplayer precons with fresh commanders, built so a playgroup could buy them, shuffle up and play against each other. The 2019 design hook was mechanical: rather than tribes or colors, each deck took a mechanic from Magic's past that had never had real Commander support and built a full deck around it.

There is no storyline. The legendary creatures come from many corners of the Multiverse; some are new characters like Kadena, and some are returning Dominarians such as Gerrard, Weatherlight Hero, Tahngarth, First Mate and Volrath, the Shapestealer, nods to the old Weatherlight saga. If you're new to the format, start with our [Commander explainer](/blog/what-is-commander-edh).

## The decks

### Faceless Menace (blue-black-green)

The face commander is **Kadena, Slinking Sorcerer**, who makes your first face-down creature spell each turn cost `{3}` less and draws a card whenever a face-down creature enters under your control. The alternate commanders are **Rayami, First of the Fallen** and **Volrath, the Shapestealer**, with Grismold, the Dreadsower also included. The deck is full of morph and megamorph creatures, such as Den Protector, Deathmist Raptor, Sagu Mauler and Thousand Winds, plus manifest cards like Thieving Amalgam, which manifests the top of each opponent's library on their upkeep.

### Merciless Rage (black-red)

**Anje Falkenrath** has haste, discards a card to draw a card, and untaps whenever you discard a card with madness. The alternate commanders are **Chainer, Nightmare Adept** and **Greven, Predator Captain**. The deck loots constantly to cast madness cards like Anje's Ravager and Archfiend of Spite cheaply, and it also includes Bone Miser, K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth and Skyfire Phoenix.

### Mystic Intellect (blue-red-white)

**Sevinne, the Chronoclasm** prevents all damage dealt to it and copies the first instant or sorcery you cast from your graveyard each turn. The alternate commanders are **Elsha of the Infinite** and **Pramikon, Sky Rampart**, a Wall that forces everyone to attack in a single direction. The deck is a flashback spellslinger list with Backdraft Hellkite, Ignite the Future, Sevinne's Reclamation and Mass Diminish, and it is the deck that contained Dockside Extortionist.

### Primal Genesis (red-green-white)

**Ghired, Conclave Exile** enters with a 4/4 Rhino token and populates every time it attacks, putting the copy onto the battlefield tapped and attacking. The alternate commanders are **Marisi, Breaker of the Coil** and **Atla Palani, Nest Tender**. The deck makes big creature tokens and copies them with populate, using cards like Trostani, Selesnya's Voice, Doomed Artisan and Ghired's Belligerence.

## How it plays

The four decks are themed precons from 2019, so they are slower and less optimized than many later precons, but each has a clear plan:

- **Faceless Menace** is the most complex. Kadena makes morph creatures cheap and turns each one into a card, so the deck builds a wide board of face-down 2/2s that flip into surprises. It rewards players who are comfortable bluffing and tracking hidden information.
- **Merciless Rage** is fast and aggressive. Anje lets you discard every turn and cast madness cards for less, while Chainer brings creatures back from the graveyard with haste.
- **Mystic Intellect** wants long games. It fills the graveyard with flashback spells, and Sevinne copies the first one each turn. Pramikon gives it a strong political tool to control who gets attacked.
- **Primal Genesis** is a midrange creature deck. Ghired's free Rhino token and populate triggers create a growing army, and token doublers let it snowball.

### The mechanics in plain terms

- **Morph and megamorph:** cast a card face down as a nameless 2/2 for `{3}`, then turn it face up at any time by paying its morph cost. Megamorph adds a +1/+1 counter when it flips. Den Protector, for example, returns a card from your graveyard to your hand when it's turned face up.
- **Manifest:** put the top card of a library onto the battlefield face down as a 2/2. If it's a creature card, you can flip it for its mana cost. Thieving Amalgam does this with your opponents' libraries.
- **Madness:** when you discard a madness card, you may cast it for its madness cost instead. Anje's Ravager costs `{1}{R}` this way.
- **Flashback:** cast the card again from your graveyard for its flashback cost, then exile it. Sevinne's Reclamation does even more when it's cast from the graveyard.
- **Populate:** create a copy of a creature token you control. A 4/4 Rhino from Ghired is the usual target.

## The meta: how it played

Commander 2019 had no effect on Standard or Pioneer. Its main impact was in Commander and, through one card, in competitive multiplayer play.

**Dockside Extortionist** turned out to be far stronger than it looked in a precon. In a four-player game, opponents often control many artifacts and enchantments, so it could make a pile of Treasures for two mana and fuel combos. It became one of the most-played red cards in Commander and a fixture of competitive Commander. The Commander Rules Committee banned it on September 23, 2024, in the same announcement that banned Mana Crypt, Jeweled Lotus and Nadu, Winged Wisdom. It remains legal in Legacy and Vintage.

**Seedborn Muse**, reprinted here, is on the official Game Changers list as of October 2026, which affects the [Commander bracket](/blog/commander-brackets-explained) a deck lands in.

Beyond Commander, Chainer, Nightmare Adept and K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth became Modern-legal through later reprints.

## Cards from Commander 2019 worth owning

- **Kadena, Slinking Sorcerer:** the definitive morph commander.
- **Anje Falkenrath:** a fast, focused madness commander.
- **Chainer, Nightmare Adept:** recursion and haste for any black-red graveyard deck.
- **K'rrik, Son of Yawgmoth:** turns life into mana for mono-black decks.
- **Elsha of the Infinite:** a strong Jeskai spells commander.
- **Volrath, the Shapestealer:** flexible copy effects for counters decks.
- **Seedborn Muse:** a powerful reprint for green decks.
- **Elemental Bond:** cheap card draw for creature-heavy green decks.
