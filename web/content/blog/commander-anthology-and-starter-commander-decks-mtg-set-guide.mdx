Commander Anthology, Commander Anthology Volume II and the Starter Commander Decks are three Magic: The Gathering reprint products that repackaged ready-to-play Commander decks for new and returning players. The two Anthologies (2017 and 2018) each boxed four popular out-of-print precons from earlier years, and the 2022 Starter Commander Decks offered five new two-color decks made entirely of reprints, aimed squarely at first-time Commander players.

## What these products were for

Commander precons go out of print quickly, and by 2017 several of the most-loved early decks had become hard to find. The Anthologies solved that by reprinting four of them at a time in a single box, using the original commanders and themes. Commander Anthology Volume II added premium foil versions of its decks' commanders.

The Starter Commander Decks had a different goal. By 2022, yearly precons had grown more complex, often three or four colors with intricate themes. Wizards wanted an entry point for players who had never built a Commander deck, so it made five simple two-color decks, each with a clear plan, a foil-etched commander, 10 double-sided tokens and punchout counters. All the cards are reprints, picked to teach what a working Commander deck looks like.

None of these products has a story; each card keeps the flavor of its original set. If you're new to the format, our [Commander explainer](/blog/what-is-commander-edh) covers the basics.

## The decks

### Commander Anthology (2017)

- **Heavenly Inferno, Kaalia of the Vast (white-black-red):** from the original 2011 Commander. Kaalia flies in and puts an Angel, Demon or Dragon from your hand onto the battlefield attacking whenever she attacks an opponent, so the deck is packed with big fliers like Akroma, Angel of Fury and Basandra, Battle Seraph.
- **Evasive Maneuvers, Derevi, Empyrial Tactician (green-white-blue):** from Commander 2013. Derevi can be put onto the battlefield from the command zone for `{1}{G}{W}{U}` and taps or untaps permanents when your creatures connect, which suits a deck of small evasive creatures and untap tricks.
- **Guided by Nature, Freyalise, Llanowar's Fury (mono-green):** from Commander 2014. Freyalise is a planeswalker commander that makes mana Elves, so the deck is Elf tribal with Elvish Archdruid, Priest of Titania and Imperious Perfect.
- **Plunder the Graves, Meren of Clan Nel Toth (black-green):** from Commander 2015. Meren earns experience counters when your creatures die and returns a creature from your graveyard each end step, making a sacrifice-and-recursion engine with Shriekmaw, Eternal Witness and Mycoloth.

### Commander Anthology Volume II (2018)

- **Devour for Power, The Mimeoplasm (blue-black-green):** from the original 2011 Commander. The Mimeoplasm exiles two creatures from graveyards and becomes a copy of one, grown by the other, so the deck fills graveyards with cards like Buried Alive and Living Death.
- **Built from Scratch, Daretti, Scrap Savant (mono-red):** from Commander 2014. Daretti is a planeswalker commander that loots and swaps artifacts between your graveyard and the battlefield, with Goblin Welder, Wurmcoil Engine and Scrap Mastery.
- **Wade into Battle, Kalemne, Disciple of Iroas (red-white):** from Commander 2015. Kalemne has double strike and vigilance and grows with experience counters each time you cast a creature with mana value 5 or more, so the deck plays Giants and big creatures like Sun Titan and Gisela, Blade of Goldnight.
- **Breed Lethality, Atraxa, Praetors' Voice (white-blue-black-green):** from Commander 2016. Atraxa proliferates every end step, and the deck is full of +1/+1 counter cards like Hardened Scales, Cathars' Crusade, Corpsejack Menace and Master Biomancer.

### Starter Commander Decks (2022)

- **First Flight, Isperia, Supreme Judge (white-blue):** a fliers deck that draws a card whenever a creature attacks you or your planeswalkers. It includes Swords to Plowshares, Counterspell and Sephara, Sky's Blade.
- **Grave Danger, Gisa and Geralf (blue-black):** Zombie tribal that casts a Zombie from your graveyard each turn, with Gray Merchant of Asphodel, Diregraf Captain, Army of the Damned and Liliana, Untouched by Death.
- **Chaos Incarnate, Kardur, Doomscourge (black-red):** forces opponents to attack each other and drains whenever an attacking creature dies, with Brash Taunter, Dictate of the Twin Gods and Blasphemous Act.
- **Draconic Destruction, Atarka, World Render (red-green):** Dragon tribal where every attacking Dragon gains double strike, with Thundermaw Hellkite, Drakuseth, Maw of Flames, Dragon Tempest and plenty of ramp.
- **Token Triumph, Emmara, Soul of the Accord (green-white):** makes a lifelink Soldier whenever Emmara becomes tapped, and goes wide with Hornet Queen, Felidar Retreat, Mentor of the Meek and Dictate of Heliod.

## How they play

The Anthology decks are reprints of 2011-2016 precons, so they reflect the design of their year. The oldest lists, Heavenly Inferno and Devour for Power, have more filler and fewer efficient mana rocks than modern precons, while Breed Lethality and Plunder the Graves are close to the strength of later products and are still popular upgrade starting points. Every Anthology deck includes Sol Ring, and Skullclamp appears in both Guided by Nature and Plunder the Graves.

The Starter Commander Decks are deliberately simple. Each is two colors, has a single clear plan and includes basic staples (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Command Tower and Commander's Sphere appear in all five). They are good for teaching: a new player can learn how ramp, removal and a commander's ability fit together without tracking a complicated engine.

When you're ready to improve one, our guide on [how to upgrade a Commander precon](/blog/how-to-upgrade-a-commander-precon) walks through the process, and [building your first Commander deck](/blog/how-to-build-your-first-commander-deck) explains the ratios of lands, ramp, draw and removal to aim for.

## The meta: how they played

As all-reprint products, none of these created new decks or affected Standard, Pioneer or Modern. Their value was access: Atraxa, Meren, Kaalia, Derevi and The Mimeoplasm were all hard to find before the Anthologies, and the Starter decks put Swords to Plowshares, Counterspell, Hornet Queen, Thundermaw Hellkite and Gray Merchant into many new players' hands.

As of October 2026, none of the cards in these thirteen decks are on the official Commander Game Changers list or the Commander ban list, so the stock decks sit in the lower and middle [Commander brackets](/blog/commander-brackets-explained). A few cards are banned in other formats, such as Skullclamp (Modern and Legacy) and Treasure Cruise, which appears in Breed Lethality and is banned in Modern, Legacy and Pauper.

## Cards from these products worth owning

- **Atraxa, Praetors' Voice:** a premier counters and superfriends commander.
- **Meren of Clan Nel Toth:** a classic graveyard commander.
- **Wurmcoil Engine:** a colorless threat for any Commander deck.
- **Skullclamp:** one of the strongest card-draw artifacts in Commander.
- **Hardened Scales and Corpsejack Menace:** counter doublers for counters decks.
- **Grave Pact:** a punishing enchantment for black sacrifice decks.
- **Eternal Witness:** green recursion that fits nearly any green deck.
- **Swords to Plowshares and Counterspell:** efficient interaction.
- **Hornet Queen and Thundermaw Hellkite:** strong finishers for green and red decks.
